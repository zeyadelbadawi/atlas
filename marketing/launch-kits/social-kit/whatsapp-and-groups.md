# WhatsApp and teacher groups

> PREPARED ONLY. Nothing here is posted to Status, sent as a broadcast, posted in a group or sent to anyone before the owner's launch go-ahead and approval of each message.
> Arabic is Egyptian colloquial, **not natively reviewed** (RV-14). Claims → `../INDEX.md` §4. The WhatsApp Business number is pending (OD-04): `[WA-NUMBER]`.

## 1. Rules for every WhatsApp and group message
1. **Only people who reached out or opted in.** No cold messages, no numbers taken from groups, pages or lists, no adding anyone to a group or broadcast without asking.
2. **Broadcast lists** only reach contacts who saved the Atlas number. Send a broadcast only to people who also said yes to updates (log the date and wording in the CRM consent tab).
3. **Every broadcast ends with the opt-out line:** «لو مش عايز رسايل تانية اكتب «إيقاف».» Honour it at once and confirm with WR-13.
4. **A reply on any channel stops every other follow-up** to that person (sales-kit `outreach-sequences.md`).
5. **No student data** in statuses, broadcasts or groups. Screenshots only from the demo academy, with «محتوى تجريبي».
6. **Voice notes** are fine for warmth, but always repeat prices, links and limits in text.
7. **Groups belong to other people.** Read the rules, ask the admin before any post that mentions Atlas, say who you are, and never drop links where links are not allowed (see §3).
