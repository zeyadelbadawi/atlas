# Short-video scripts: TikTok, Instagram/Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts (R01–R14)

> PREPARED ONLY. Not to be filmed for publication, uploaded, scheduled or posted before the owner's launch go-ahead and per-video approval.
> Voiceover is Egyptian colloquial, **not natively reviewed** (RV-14). Claims → `../INDEX.md` §4; disclosures → INDEX §5.

## Production rules (all scripts)
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 9:16, 15–45 s. Hook in the first 2 seconds with all three layers at once: visual + spoken line + on-screen text.
- **On camera (OD-06):** each script has a "talking head" version (founder, if the owner agrees) and a "voice-over only" version (screen recording + captions). Never generated people, never students, never minors.
- **Screen recordings:** only from the demo academy on the local capture stack (SPEC D.1: never production, never edited inside the UI). Every recorded screen shows «محتوى تجريبي» in the top corner for its whole duration. Demo receipts read «إيصال تجريبي». Never record: the lesson player (on-screen code overlay, OC-10), devices pages, custom-domain tab, revenue charts, integrity settings, upload screens, live sessions, real names.
- **Captions:** burned in, Arabic, max 2 lines, 3–6 words per line, white Rubik Bold with a dark outline, key word in gold #E0A526. Keep text inside the 720×1200 central safe area (platform buttons cover the edges).
- **Sound:** the voiceover is the main audio. Background music only from the platform's **commercial-use library** for business accounts (TikTok Commercial Music Library, Meta Sound Collection, YouTube Audio Library), low under the voice. No copyrighted songs, no trending sounds that are not cleared for business use, no claim that a track is licensed unless the library says so. Record the track name in the approval log.
- **Length check:** the timings below are targets; cut, don't speed up the voice.
- **CTA:** one per video. TikTok and Instagram: «اللينك في البايو». YouTube Shorts: link in the description and pinned comment with `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_content=rNN`.
- **YouTube Shorts title:** ≤ 70 characters, searchable Arabic. **Description:** two lines + link + 3 hashtags.
