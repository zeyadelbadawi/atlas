# Outreach and follow-up sequences

> # DO NOT SEND BEFORE LAUNCH
> PREPARED ONLY. No message, email or broadcast here has been sent, loaded into a tool, or scheduled. Each sequence starts only after the owner's launch go-ahead, and each message template needs owner approval before first use.
> Arabic is polite Egyptian (WhatsApp) and simple Egyptian-leaning Arabic (email), **not natively reviewed** (RV-14). Prices carry "[check live price at launch]".

## 0. Rules for every sequence (cold-email and lead-gen rules)
1. **Only people who contacted us, signed up, or opted in.** No cold lists, no scraped numbers or emails, no messaging group members who didn't ask.
2. **A reply on any channel stops every other sequence** for that person. Continue the conversation by hand.
3. **One ask per message.** No "just checking in". Each follow-up adds something new.
4. **Maximum three follow-ups after silence**, then a polite close.
5. **Opt-out in every message after the first:** WhatsApp «لو مش عايز رسايل تانية اكتب «إيقاف».» · Email: one-click unsubscribe + «لو مش عايز رسايل تانية من أطلس، رد بكلمة «إيقاف».» Honour it at once (WR-13).
6. **Contact-form leads:** the form promises «نستخدم بياناتك فقط للرد عليك». So contact leads get **replies to their request only** (S3 CF1–CF3). Marketing emails (CF-M1, CF-M2) go **only** to people who ticked the separate marketing box, once that consent field is verified (SUP-04, OD-13).
7. **Trial users:** the owner's phone is not visible to the Atlas team after self-serve signup (GTM §10.2). WhatsApp follow-up to trialists only if they wrote to us first or opted in. Email is the default channel.
8. **Tools:** Atlas can email organisation owners (trialists) through the Platform Owner broadcast (manual, by plan/status cohort, MSG-10), but cannot email prospects with no account; prospect email needs an external tool with consent records [BUILD]. The product already sends its own transactional trial emails (`trial.started`; `ending_soon` and `expired` are flag-gated): **check them before sending S4 so the teacher doesn't get two welcomes.** Open/click tracking off; measure replies.
9. **No incentives, discounts, founding offers or deadlines** (none approved; OD-12).
10. Every link uses the UTM register (`../INDEX.md` §3): WhatsApp `whatsapp/whatsapp-chat`, email `email/email`, campaign `{yyyy-mm}-sales-followup`, `{yyyy-mm}-trial-onboarding` or `{yyyy-mm}-referral`.
