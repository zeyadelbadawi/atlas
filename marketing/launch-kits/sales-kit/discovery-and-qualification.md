## 1. The discovery call (10–15 minutes, or the first minutes of a demo)
**Opening (say it as written):** «أهلًا يا أستاذ/ة، شكرًا على وقتك. هسألك كام سؤال عن شغلك دلوقتي علشان أوريك اللي يهمك بس. ولو لقينا إن أطلس مش مناسب ليك، هقولك بصراحة.»

Ask 6–8 of these, not all. Write the answers in the buyer's words; you will reuse them.

## 2. Qualification: fit, not-yet-fit, score
### 2.1 Fit (from the live site "fit" block)
A good fit if the teacher:
- sells lessons to their own students;
- collects payments by wallet, InstaPay or bank transfer;
- keeps lesson videos on YouTube (or is fine doing so);
- wants an academy with their own name on it.

### 2.2 Not yet a fit (knock-outs: say so politely, offer to note their need)
| Knock-out | What they need | Say |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | A student app in the store, now | «لو التطبيق شرط دلوقتي، أطلس لسه مش مناسب.» |
| K2 | Card or Fawry checkout, or automatic activation without a person approving | «الدفع عندنا بالمحافظ وإنستاباي والتحويل، بموافقتك. مفيش كارت أو فوري دلوقتي.» |
| K3 | Live classes inside the platform | «الحصص المباشرة مش موجودة.» |
| K4 | WhatsApp or SMS messages to parents or students from the platform | «مفيش رسايل واتساب أو SMS من المنصة.» |
| K5 | A guarantee that videos "can't be recorded" | «مفيش موقع يقدر يوعدك بكده، ومش هنوعدك.» + D-1 |
| K6 | Hosted video upload (no YouTube) | «الدروس فيديوهات يوتيوب جوه أكاديميتك. مفيش رفع ملفات فيديو.» |
| K7 | An assistant must approve payments | «الموافقة لصاحب الحساب بس دلوقتي.» |
| K8 | Coupons or discount codes for students | «مفيش كوبونات خصم دلوقتي.» |
| K9 | A physical centre looking for attendance or in-person operations software | «أطلس معمول للبيع أونلاين، مش لإدارة الحضور.» |
| K10 | A very small group at low fees where the plan price makes no sense to them | Show the Teacher plan honestly; don't push |

Close a knock-out with WR-09 (`../social-kit/whatsapp-and-groups.md`) and mark the CRM stage "Not a fit" with the reason code (K1–K10). If the owner later ships the missing piece, these are the first people to tell.

### 2.3 Score (out of 100; lead-gen §4.2)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Paying online students today | none or < 20 → 0 · 20–49 → 8 · 50–149 → 18 · 150+ → 25 |
| Current tools | groups + YouTube + wallets → 20 · another platform → 12 · own site → 10 · not online yet → 3 |
| Payment method | wallets/InstaPay/bank → 20 · mostly cash → 6 · insists on card → 0 (also K2) |
| Content ready | at least one full course on YouTube → 15 · some videos → 8 · none → 2 |
| Decision maker on the conversation | owner → 10 · assistant with owner engaged → 7 · assistant only → 3 |
| Timing | within 30 days or in season → 10 · within 90 days → 5 · later/unknown → 1 |

**Tiers:** Hot ≥ 70 → offer two demo times now · Warm 45–69 → trial link + check-in · Cold < 45 → stay in touch only with their permission · any knock-out → Not a fit.

## 3. Plan recommendation logic
Catalog limits (re-read from `GET /public/plans` at launch) [check live price at launch]:
| Plan | Academies | Students | Instructors | Courses | Monthly / yearly (EGP) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teacher / المعلّم (`starter`) | 1 | 100 | 3 | 20 | 1,190 / 11,900 |
| Academy / الأكاديمية (`growth`) | 1 | 400 | 8 | unlimited | 2,790 / 27,900 |
| Organization / المؤسسة (`enterprise` key) | 5 | 1,500 | 25 | unlimited | 7,490 / 74,900 |

**Decision steps (use their peak-season numbers, not today's quiet month):**
1. **Needs more than one academy** (separate brands, separate sites)? → Organization, if ≤ 5 academies, ≤ 1,500 students and ≤ 25 instructors. Above any of those → "talk to us" (sales, no catalog plan; never `plan=enterprise` for this case).
2. **One academy:**
   - students ≤ 100 **and** instructors ≤ 3 **and** courses ≤ 20 → **Teacher**;
   - otherwise students ≤ 400 **and** instructors ≤ 8 → **Academy**;
   - otherwise students ≤ 1,500 **and** instructors ≤ 25 → **Organization** (it also gives room for a second academy later);
   - otherwise → talk to us.
3. **Headroom:** if their peak is within ~20 % of a limit, tell them the limit and how upgrading works: at a limit nothing is deleted, new students and courses wait until they upgrade, and they can upgrade any time; the new limits start when the payment is approved (CL-23). Let them choose; don't upsell by default.
4. **Monthly or yearly:** if they expect to use Atlas for 10 months or more of the year, yearly costs the same as 10 monthly payments and covers 12 (CL-21). If they're unsure, start monthly.
5. **Trial plan:** start the 14-day trial on the plan the steps above give, so the trial shows the right limits (one trial per customer).
6. **Open question (OD-15):** whether managers count toward the instructor limit. Until the owner confirms, count every assistant who will grade as an instructor.
