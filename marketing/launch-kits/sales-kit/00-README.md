## 1. Who sells, and how
Atlas sells as **assisted self-serve** (GTM §11): most teachers start the 14-day trial themselves; a person helps on WhatsApp, on a short demo call, or with «جهّزها معانا» setup. Teams with several brands (Organization plan) and anyone above the Organization limits get a sales-led conversation. Today the "rep" is the owner or someone the owner names.

## 3. Rules a rep never breaks
1. **Say only what the claims list says** (INDEX §4). If a buyer asks about something not on it, the answer is «مش موجود دلوقتي» or «هرجعلك بالإجابة الأكيدة», never a guess.
2. **Say the limits before the close**, every time: no app; approval is manual and done by the account owner; no card or Fawry checkout; lessons are YouTube videos played inside the academy and a determined student can still copy the YouTube link itself; no live classes; no WhatsApp or SMS messages to parents.
3. **No proof we don't have:** no customer names, counts, logos, quotes, case studies, ratings or "teachers like you saw X". We are new; say so.
4. **No competitor names**, no claims about what other platforms do or don't do. Ask the buyer what their current tool does instead.
5. **No promises about dates, speed or results**: no "in minutes", no reply-time promises, no income or time-saved figures, no roadmap dates.
6. **No "free"** in any language. The trial is «تجربة ١٤ يوم من غير كارت» / "14-day trial, no card needed".
7. **No discounts, founding offers or incentives** unless the owner approves them in writing (none are approved).
8. **No student data** in demos, decks or messages. Demo academy and demo accounts only, labelled «محتوى تجريبي».
9. **Disqualify politely** when the buyer needs something we don't have (discovery file §3). A lost deal beats a teacher who moves students and then hits a missing piece.
