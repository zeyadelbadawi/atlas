## Copy

### Header
**Atlas | Your teaching academy online, under your name**
A branded academy platform for Egyptian teachers who sell their lessons online.

### The problem
Many teachers in Egypt who sell lessons online run on three tools: a WhatsApp group, a YouTube link and a growing pile of transfer screenshots. That works until the group gets big. Then someone has to match every screenshot to a name, keep track of who paid, and hope the link stays with paying students. And nothing carries the teacher's own name.

### How it works
**1. Create your academy.** It gets a website on its own Atlas subdomain the moment you create it. Pick one of four designs and add your logo, and the colours come from it. You can connect a domain you already own.
**2. Add your courses.** Lessons are YouTube videos played inside your academy, with quizzes, homework and certificates.
**3. Students pay you, and you approve.** A student pays by Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay, InstaPay or bank transfer and uploads the proof in your academy. You approve it once; the student is enrolled and gets an email. If something is wrong, reject it with a reason and the student can pay again.

### What every plan includes
- **A website under your name:** subdomain, four designs, colours from your logo, Arabic and English pages you edit and publish yourself.
- **Payments you approve:** six Egyptian payment methods; every order and payment in one list with its proof and status. The money goes straight to you, and Atlas takes no commission on these payments.
- **Your team:** owner, managers and instructors. Instructors see and grade only the courses assigned to them; only the account owner approves payments.
- **Quizzes, homework and certificates:** five question types, homework with due dates and feedback, certificates anyone can check on a public page.
- **Student follow-up:** open, approval-based or invitation-only sign-up; reports on progress and students falling behind; email announcements.
- **Accounts and data:** each student account works on two devices, one screen at a time. Each academy's data is kept separate in the database.

### Plainly: what Atlas doesn't do yet
There is no mobile app (academies run in the phone browser). There are no live classes, no video file uploads, and no WhatsApp or SMS messages to parents. Payment approval is manual and done by the account owner; there is no card or Fawry checkout today. Lessons are YouTube videos played inside your academy, and a determined student can still copy the YouTube link itself.
