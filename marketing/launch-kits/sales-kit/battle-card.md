# Battle cards (internal only)

> PREPARED ONLY. Internal rep guidance; never sent to prospects. **No competitor is named, and no claim is made about what any other product does or doesn't do.** Where a buyer describes another tool, ask questions about it instead of asserting facts. "Where Atlas wins" lines are **hypotheses to test** (no win/loss data exists yet): log the outcome of every deal against them.
> Last verified: 20 Oct 2026 against the claims list (`../INDEX.md` §4). Re-check monthly.

## Card 1 · The status quo: WhatsApp groups + YouTube links + manual transfers
**When you'll see it:** almost every secondary or preparatory teacher who already sells online. It's the main thing Atlas replaces, and often the only "competitor".

**Why teachers stay on it (their real reasons):** it costs nothing extra; students are already in the groups; transfers by Vodafone Cash or InstaPay are routine; no setup; everyone knows how it works.

**Where it's genuinely strong (say so):** zero setup; zero learning for students; WhatsApp is where the conversation happens and will stay.

**Where Atlas may win, and for whom (hypothesis):**
| Pain they describe | What Atlas does (claim) |
|---|---|
| Matching each screenshot to a name, then sending the link by hand | Student uploads the proof inside the academy; owner approves once; enrolled and emailed (CL-06) |
| Not sure who paid this month | Every order and payment in one list with proof and status (CL-17) |
| Links reaching students who didn't pay | Lessons open inside the academy after the approved payment; each account works on two devices, one screen at a time (CL-09, OD-10). **Always add D-1:** a determined student can still copy the YouTube link itself |
| Nothing carries their name | Website on an Atlas subdomain from creation, own domain, four designs, logo colours, Arabic and English (CL-01…04) |
| Assistants mixing tasks | Owner / manager / instructor roles; instructors see only their courses (CL-10) |
Strongest fit (hypothesis): teachers with enough paying students that confirming payments takes real time each week (their words), and who already collect by wallet, InstaPay or bank.

**Discovery questions:**
- «بتأكد كام تحويل في الأسبوع؟ ومين بيعمل ده؟»
- «الطالب بيوصل للدرس إزاي بعد ما يدفع؟»
- «حصل إن حد دخل من غير ما يدفع؟ عرفت إزاي؟»
- «لو ولي أمر سأل "المستر بيدرّس فين؟"، بتبعتله إيه؟»

**Top objections:** O2 (students used to WhatsApp), O1 (price), O11 (manual approval in season), O16 (no time to move) → `objection-handling.md`.

**Key line:** «الجروب يفضل مكان الكلام. الأكاديمية تبقى مكان الدفع والكورسات، بنفس طرق الدفع اللي طلابك متعودين عليها.»

**When to walk away:** small groups at low fees where the plan price doesn't make sense to them; teachers happy with the routine and not feeling the payment work; anyone who mainly needs WhatsApp or SMS messaging to parents (K4).

## Card 2 · Generic course platforms (international or general-purpose)
**When you'll see it:** IG/American-curriculum tutors, teachers who searched "course platform", or buyers who already tried one.

**Why buyers consider them:** an established name and many features; buyers may expect a store app, card checkout, coupons or live-class tools, and some are priced in foreign currencies. Ask what theirs includes rather than assuming.

**Where they may be strong (don't argue; ask):** card payments and international buyers; apps; coupons and marketing tools; long track records. If the buyer needs one of these now, that platform may be the better fit.

**Where Atlas may win, and for whom (hypothesis):**
| What the buyer cares about | Atlas fact | Question to ask about their option |
|---|---|---|
| Students pay by Egyptian wallets and InstaPay | Six Egyptian methods; owner approves the proof (CL-05, CL-06) | «طلابك يقدروا يدفعوا عليها فودافون كاش أو إنستاباي؟» |
| Keeping all of the student's payment | Money goes straight to the teacher; 0 % Atlas commission on these payments (CL-07) | «بتاخد نسبة من كل بيعة؟ والفلوس بتوصلك إمتى؟» |
| Paying in pounds | Plans in EGP, monthly or yearly (CL-19) | «بتدفع اشتراكها بالجنيه ولا بعملة تانية؟» |
| Arabic and English for Egyptian students and parents | Arabic and English pages; dashboard in both languages (CL-04, CL-24) | «الموقع عندهم بيتعرض بالعربي من اليمين للشمال؟» |
| A team under one roof, several brands | Roles; up to 5 academies on Organization (CL-10, CL-11) | «تقدر تدير أكتر من براند من حساب واحد؟» |
| Certificates parents can check | Public verification page (CL-14) | «الشهادة ليها صفحة تحقق؟» |

**Discovery questions:** «إيه أكتر حاجة عاجباك فيها؟ وإيه اللي مضايقك؟» · «طلابك بيدفعوا إزاي عليها؟» · «محتاج تطبيق أو دفع بالكارت ضروري؟»

**Top objections:** O9 (already on another platform), O4 (app), O5 (card), O25 (coupons), O21 (video upload).

**Key line:** «مش هقارن بمنصة معينة. لو طلابك بيدفعوا محافظ وإنستاباي وعايز الفلوس توصلك كاملة، خلّينا نشوف أطلس بند بند، ومعاه الحاجات اللي لسه مش موجودة.»

**When to walk away:** they need card checkout, international buyers, an app, coupons, live classes or hosted video **now** (K1–K3, K6, K8). Tell them so, and offer to tell them if that changes.

## Card 3 · A custom-built website (freelancer or agency quote)
**When you'll see it:** a teacher who has a quote for "an educational platform" or already paid for a site that needs upkeep.

**Why buyers consider it:** the feeling of owning the platform; one-off payment; custom features.

**Where it may be strong:** fully custom design or features; possibly an app or card gateway if built in.

**Where Atlas may win (hypothesis):** no build to wait for and no one to chase for fixes; payments by Egyptian wallets with owner approval, team roles, quizzes, homework and certificates already in the product; own domain can be connected (CL-02…CL-14).

**Discovery questions (let the buyer do the math with their own quote):**
- «مين هيصلّح الموقع لو حصلت مشكلة بعد التسليم؟ وبكام؟»
- «الاستضافة والصيانة بكام في الشهر؟»
- «إمتى هيبقى جاهز؟ والدفع والامتحانات والمساعدين داخلين في العرض؟»
Use the worksheet in `pricing-faq-and-calculator.md` §5 with *their* quote; never supply an agency price.

**When to walk away:** they need a bespoke feature or an app that Atlas doesn't have.

## How we lose (be ready, log it)
| Reason | Honest response |
|---|---|
| Needs a store app now | Agree; not a fit yet (K1) |
| Needs card or Fawry checkout | Agree; not a fit yet (K2) |
| Wants a "can't be recorded" guarantee | Explain D-1; don't compete on promises (K5) |
| Needs live classes or parent messaging | Not a fit yet (K3, K4) |
| Price feels high for a small group | Show the Teacher plan honestly; let them go |
| Assistant must approve payments | Not today (K7) |
| Wants proof from other teachers | We have none to show yet; offer the demo and the trial |

Every loss gets a reason code in the CRM (`crm-and-pipeline.md` §2, `lost_reason`). After 10+ closed deals, review wins and losses by reason before changing these cards.
