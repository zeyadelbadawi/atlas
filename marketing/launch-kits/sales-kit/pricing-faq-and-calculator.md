## 3. Yearly math: pay for 10 months, use 12
| Plan | 12 × monthly | Yearly | You keep | Yearly ÷ 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teacher | 14,280 | 11,900 | 2,380 | ≈ 992 a month |
| Academy | 33,480 | 27,900 | 5,580 | 2,325 a month |
| Organization | 89,880 | 74,900 | 14,980 | ≈ 6,242 a month |
[check live price at launch]

- **Rule of thumb:** yearly costs the same as 10 monthly payments. If the teacher will use Atlas 10 months or more in the year, yearly is the same price or lower; if fewer than 10, monthly is cheaper.
- Yearly also means one payment and one proof review a year instead of twelve (CL-22).
- During a yearly subscription, upgrades go through support (live pricing copy).

**Price per student at full capacity** (only meaningful when the plan is full; say "at full capacity" every time):
| Plan | Monthly ÷ max students |
|---|---|
| Teacher | 1,190 ÷ 100 = 11.90 EGP per student per month |
| Academy | 2,790 ÷ 400 ≈ 6.98 |
| Organization | 7,490 ÷ 1,500 ≈ 4.99 |

## 4. Scenarios (plan mapping from catalog limits)
### Scenario 1: one teacher, 80 paying students
- **Fits:** Teacher (80 of 100 students; 1 academy; up to 3 instructors; up to 20 courses).
- **Cost:** 1,190 a month, or 11,900 a year (≈ 992 a month). At 80 students that's about 14.9 EGP per student per month on monthly, about 12.4 on yearly.
- **Watch:** 20 students of headroom. If the season brings more than 100, or more than 20 courses, move to Academy. Upgrading is possible any time; nothing is deleted at a limit, and new students wait until the upgrade (CL-23).
- **Say:** «بـ٨٠ طالب، المعلّم كفاية. لو متوقع تعدّي ١٠٠ في الموسم، نبدأ بالأكاديمية أو ترقّي لما تقرب.»

### Scenario 2: teacher + 3 assistants, 300 paying students
- **Teacher plan doesn't fit:** 300 students is above its 100.
- **Fits:** Academy (300 of 400 students; 3 assistants as instructors of 8; unlimited courses).
- **Cost:** 2,790 a month, or 27,900 a year (2,325 a month). At 300 students that's 9.30 EGP per student per month on monthly, 7.75 on yearly.
- **Watch:** only the owner approves payments, so the assistants can't take over approvals (CL-08). Whether managers count toward the instructor limit is open (OD-15); with 3 assistants either way it fits.
- **Say:** «بـ٣٠٠ طالب و٣ مساعدين، الأكاديمية هي اللي تكفيك. والمساعدين يبقوا مدرّسين، كل واحد على كورساته.»

### Scenario 3: a team running 3 brands
- **Teacher and Academy plans don't fit:** each allows one academy.
- **Fits:** Organization (3 of 5 academies), as long as the three brands together stay within 1,500 students and 25 instructors.
- **Cost:** 7,490 a month (≈ 2,497 per brand), or 74,900 a year (≈ 6,242 a month, ≈ 2,081 per brand).
- **Why one account:** each brand keeps its own site, students and payment methods, students don't appear across academies, and the team switches between them from one account (CL-11, CL-18). Separate accounts per brand would mean separate subscriptions and separate logins.
- **Above the limits** (more than 5 brands, 1,500 students or 25 instructors): contact sales.
- **Say:** «كل براند أكاديمية ليها موقعها وطلابها وطرق دفعها، والحساب واحد. باقة المؤسسة تكفي لحد ٥.»

## 5. Worksheet (filled with the teacher's numbers; no defaults)
Use on a call or in a shared sheet. Leave every input blank until the teacher gives it.
| # | Input (teacher's answer) | Value |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Paying students at peak season | ____ |
| I2 | Courses or groups running at the same time | ____ |
| I3 | Assistants who will grade (instructors) | ____ |
| I4 | Separate brands needing their own site | ____ |
| I5 | Months a year they teach online | ____ |
| I6 | Hours a week spent confirming payments today (their own estimate) | ____ |

| # | Output | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Plan | §3 of `discovery-and-qualification.md` with I1–I4 |
| R2 | Monthly cost | plan's monthly price |
| R3 | Cost of the months they use, paying monthly | R2 × I5 |
| R4 | Yearly cost | plan's yearly price (= 10 × monthly) |
| R5 | Cheaper option | yearly if I5 ≥ 10, otherwise monthly |
| R6 | Plan cost per paying student per month | R2 ÷ I1 (monthly) or (R4 ÷ 12) ÷ I1 (yearly) |
| R7 | Hours a week on payment checks today | I6, shown back as their own number |

**Rules:** don't add "money lost to leaks", "hours saved" or income lines; there is no measured data for them. Show R7 only as "what you told me", never as a promise that Atlas removes it. Atlas takes no commission, so no line for "platform fees on sales" is needed for Atlas.
