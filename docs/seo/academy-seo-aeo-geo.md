# Academy websites: SEO, AEO and GEO readiness

Status: November 2026 (frontend branch `dec-d`, backend branch `be-d`).

This document covers what every Academy website on Atlas gives search engines (SEO: Google, Bing), answer engines (AEO) and generative engines (GEO: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Overviews) from the moment it is published. It lists what was audited, what was wrong before this pass, what each piece does now, the sources it follows, and what the Academy Owner still has to do by hand.

## 1. Where an Academy website starts

- **Provisioning.** A new Academy gets a website configuration (`status: 'draft'`, `seo: {}`) and its core pages (Home, About, Courses, Course Details, FAQs, Contact), with the template's bilingual starter content (`WebsiteBootstrapService`, `WebsiteGenerationService`).
- **Not published by default.** Until the Owner publishes, every host shows _Coming Soon_: `noindex` (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), and `robots.txt` answers `Disallow: /`. Publishing is the required "website" step of onboarding (`OnboardingStatusService`).
- **Decision left unchanged.** Publishing on creation would put template copy ("Horizon Academy brings practical, expert-led learning…") and an empty catalogue in search indexes under the Academy's name. That is thin, near-duplicate content across every new Academy, which harms the Academy's later ranking. The audit therefore keeps "indexable from the first publish" as the target. Everything below applies the moment the Owner presses Publish, with no SEO field filled in. Changing this is a product decision for the lead and owner; this pass does not change it.

## 2. Checklist: before → after

All results were measured on the production bundles with the SSR test fixtures (Theme 1, Atelier, Manara and Riwaq generated websites, `seo: {}`). Production was spot-checked read-only: `elzozo.atlass.dpdns.org` is server-rendered and correctly `noindex` while unpublished, and unknown hosts receive the app shell with `Disallow: /`.

## 3. What each piece does

### Frontend (`/home/user/wt-dec-d`)

- `src/features/public-website/components/PublicWebsitePage.tsx`: computes each page's SEO. It sets default descriptions, the share image chain, theme colour, monogram icons and verification tokens. It builds the per-page JSON-LD, flags not-found pages and passes the course's real id to the renderer.
- `src/features/public-website/hooks/useDocumentSeo.ts`: `buildSeoHeadTags` is the one tag list that both the server head (`renderSeoHeadHtml`) and the browser effect write. It adds `x-default`, `og:locale`, `og:site_name`, `theme-color`, `apple-touch-icon` and the verification metas, and handles `notFound`.
- `src/ssr/entry-server.tsx`, `server/ssr/handler.mjs`: HTTP 404 for not-found pages (`NOT_FOUND_MARKER` keeps cache hits at 404).
- `src/features/website/utils/structured-data.utils.ts`: builders for EducationalOrganization, WebSite, Course, ItemList, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList.
- `src/features/website/utils/seo-content.utils.ts`: derives the default description from page copy, and collects FAQ items exactly as rendered.
- `src/features/website/utils/seo-resolution.utils.ts`: Home's canonical path is `/`, the root of a prefixed locale has no trailing slash, and `x-default` is set.
- `src/features/website/primitives/FaqDisclosureList.tsx`: an accessible disclosure list that keeps answers in the HTML. It is used by `T1Faq` and `FaqSection`.
- `src/features/public-website/utils/academy-brand-images.utils.ts`: URLs of the generated card and monogram, versioned by `brandImageVersion` (shared with the backend).
- `src/features/public-website/hooks/useListedPublicCourses.ts`: the courses the Courses page lists, read from the query cache with no extra request.
- `src/features/website/components/WebsiteSeoTab.tsx`, `schemas/website.schemas.ts`: the verification fields and the "Search engines and AI assistants" guidance card.
- `Caddyfile`: routes `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt` and `/indexnow-key.txt` on Academy subdomains and connected domains to the backend, and adds `noindex` to sign-in and learner paths.

### Backend (`/home/user/wt-be-d`)

- `src/public-website/utils/seo-files.util.ts`: the sitemap lists every language version and dates pages with the last publish; robots.txt links llms.txt.
- `src/public-website/utils/llms-txt.util.ts`: `/llms.txt` follows the llmstxt.org shape: an H1 name, a blockquote summary, details, `## Pages`, `## Courses` and `## Optional`. `/llms-full.txt` holds the page summaries, every FAQ answer and every course in full (description, facts, instructors, outcomes, requirements). Content is neutralised, so it cannot open a heading or a link of its own. Both files are served only for published, indexable sites.
- `src/public-website/services/brand-images.service.ts` + `utils/brand-images.util.ts` draw the share card and the monogram with sharp and Pango. They use the Noto fonts already shipped in `assets/fonts` (Latin and Arabic, shaped). Results are cached in memory, and `?v=` matching the current name and colour is cached for a year.
- `src/public-website/services/index-now.service.ts`: listens to `EdgeCachePurgeService.onAcademyChanged` (the same signal that purges the edge cache), debounces 60 s per Academy, then POSTs to `https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow` only the sitemap URLs whose `lastmod` is newer than the last submission, in both languages. Each Academy's key is `HMAC(INDEXNOW_KEY_SECRET or JWT_ACCESS_SECRET, academyId)`. It is served at `/indexnow-key.txt` on the Academy's host and passed as `keyLocation`. The service is on by default, and `INDEXNOW=off` disables it.
- `src/public-website/controllers/public-website.controller.ts`: new routes `host/llms.txt`, `host/llms-full.txt`, `host/indexnow-key.txt`, `:academyId/og-image.png` and `:academyId/icon.png?size=48|96|180|192|512`. All are listed in the route-surface inventory.
- `src/website/validation/website-config.schemas.ts`: `googleSiteVerification` and `bingSiteVerification` (tokens of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`, at most 128 characters). These are stored in the existing `seo` JSON, so **no migration** is needed.
- `src/website/seo/seo-resolution.util.ts`: mirrors the frontend's Home canonical fix.

## 4. Decisions, with the reasoning

1. **robots.txt: one `User-agent: *` group, everything allowed.** Every crawler without its own group follows it. That includes Googlebot, Bingbot and Applebot, the AI search and answer crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User) and the AI-use tokens (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended). Google-Extended also governs _grounding_ in Gemini, so blocking it would reduce Gemini visibility. Listing each bot with the same rule would change nothing. A per-Academy opt-out from AI _training_ (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended) is a possible later setting and a product decision.
2. **No `SearchAction`.** The catalogue's search does not read a URL parameter, and Google retired the sitelinks search box. Markup for a search that cannot be linked to would be misleading.
3. **FAQPage and Course stay, even though Google retired those rich results** (Course info: September 2025; FAQ: May–June 2026). They are valid schema.org and describe visible content. Bing, the answer engines and LLM crawlers read them. "Course list" (ItemList + Course) is still a Google feature.
4. **Organization only on Home and About**, as Google recommends. Every other node references it by `@id`.
5. **Course URLs stay `/courses/<slug>` as canonical.** In-site cards still link `/courses/<id>`, which is a valid URL whose canonical points to the slug. Moving every card to slugs touches nine components and their tests, so it is left as a follow-up.
6. **Verification tokens show on a `noindex` home page too.** An Owner can verify in Search Console before opening the site to search engines.
7. **The generated monogram replaces the Atlas favicon** on Academies without their own. This is a visible product change in browser tabs and in Google's favicon slot. Its purpose is to stop the platform's brand from standing in for the Academy's.

## 5. What the Owner must do manually

The SEO tab shows these steps under "Search engines and AI assistants".

1. **Publish the website** (onboarding step). Nothing is indexable before that.
2. **Google Search Console** (https://search.google.com/search-console):
   - Add a _URL-prefix_ property for the website address. Choose **HTML tag** and paste the tag into _Website → SEO → Google Search Console verification_. Save, then **publish** the website (SEO settings, like every website setting, go live with the next publish), then press **Verify**. Keep the tag: Search Console re-checks it.
   - For a connected custom domain, a _Domain_ property is verified by a DNS TXT record at the domain provider.
   - Submit the sitemap: `https://<your-address>/sitemap.xml`.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (https://www.bing.com/webmasters): either **import from Google Search Console**, or paste Bing's `msvalidate.01` tag into _Bing Webmaster Tools verification_, save and publish, and press Verify. IndexNow submissions then appear under _IndexNow_.
4. Optionally, fill in the meta title and description per page, a share image, the Academy description and the footer social links (the latter feed `sameAs`). Upload a favicon and a logo of at least 112×112 px; Google requires that size for the Organization logo.

Platform operators: set `INDEXNOW_KEY_SECRET` (16+ characters) if the IndexNow keys should not derive from `JWT_ACCESS_SECRET`. Rotating either changes the keys, and the key files follow automatically.

## 7. Follow-ups not done here

- Make course cards link `/courses/<slug>` (see §4.5).
- Course Details curriculum: only the first section is open in Theme 1; the lesson titles of the others are not in the HTML (Radix Accordion).
- `canonicalBaseUrl` in the SEO tab is stored but unused by the public runtime, and its help text predates connected domains.
- IndexNow does not submit _removed_ URLs (an unpublished course). Engines find those through the 404 on their next crawl.
- An Owner setting to opt out of AI training crawlers (see §4.1).
