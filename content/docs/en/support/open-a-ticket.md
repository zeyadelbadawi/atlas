Anyone on your academy team (owner, manager or instructor) can open a ticket with the Atlas team. Learners can't: they get help from their academy (see [How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003)).

## Before you open a ticket

Many problems have a quick fix in [Troubleshooting](doc:DOC-TRB-001). If it's still not solved, open a ticket.

## Open a ticket

Tickets are opened from the Atlas dashboard at `dashboard.atlass.dpdns.org`.

1. In the sidebar, open **Support**. You can also find it in the account menu at the top.

   ![The Support page with its entry in the sidebar highlighted](shot:support/support-page)

2. Choose **New ticket**.

   ![The New ticket button](shot:support/new-ticket-button)

3. In **New support ticket**, write a short **Subject**, then describe the problem under **What is happening?**.

   ![The new ticket form filled in](shot:support/new-ticket-form)

4. Optional: choose **Attach an image** to add a screenshot. PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB.

   ![The image attachment field in the form](shot:support/new-ticket-attach)

5. Select **Submit ticket**.

   ![The Submit ticket button](shot:support/new-ticket-submit)

6. To follow up, open the ticket from your Support list. Replies from the Atlas team are marked **Atlas Support**. Write under **Add a reply** and choose **Send reply**.

   ![A ticket with the Send reply button highlighted](shot:support/ticket-reply)

## What to include

The more detail you give, the faster the team can help:

- **Which academy** it's about. Tickets are filed for your organization, so name the academy.
- **What you did**, step by step, and on which page.
- **What you expected**, and **what happened instead**, with the exact error message if there is one.
- **When it started**, and whether it happens every time.
- **Who is affected**: you, one learner, or everyone.
- A **screenshot** of the problem.

> [!WARNING]
> Never put a password, a sign-in code, or full card or wallet details in a ticket.

## What happens next

You get a notification when the Atlas team replies. You also get an email if the **Operations** category is on in your [email preferences](doc:DOC-MSG-002).

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Waiting for the Atlas team |
| **In progress** | The team is working on it |
| **Resolved** | The team thinks it's solved. You can still reply if it isn't |
| **Closed** | Finished. You can't reply; open a new ticket if you still need help |

The Atlas team reviews tickets manually and replies in the ticket. There is no guaranteed reply time.

## Good to know

- **Your tickets are private.** Only you and the Atlas team can see a ticket you opened. Your colleagues can't, even in the same organization.
- **It works when your subscription has ended.** You can still open and follow tickets.
- **Academy setup failures.** If an academy setup fails repeatedly, Atlas opens a ticket for you automatically. See [Academy creation status, retries and failures](doc:DOC-ACD-011).
- **Need work done, not a fix?** For a logo, a custom theme or help with a domain, the academy owner can [request a service](doc:DOC-SUP-002).
