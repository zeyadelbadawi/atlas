Your academy runs your courses, takes your payments and manages your enrollment, so your academy is the right place to ask for help. Atlas runs the platform behind it.

## If you're a learner: contact your academy

In My Learn, choose **Contact your academy** at the bottom of the menu. It opens the academy website's contact page, where you can send a message to the academy team.

![Contact your academy in the My Learn menu](shot:support/learner-contact-academy)

You can also use any phone number or email the academy shows on its website.

When you write, say which course you mean, what you were doing, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

## What only your academy can decide

- **Payments and refunds.** You pay the academy directly, so the academy reviews your payment and decides on refunds. See [Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005).
- **Access to courses.** Enrollments, approvals and blocks are managed by the academy team.

Your own data is different: in My Learn, open **Profile**, then the **Account** tab. Under **Your data** you can ask for a copy of your personal data or for your account to be deleted. See [Data retention and account deletion](doc:DOC-SEC-005).

> [!LIMITATION]
> Learners can't open a support ticket with Atlas. Support tickets are for academy teams.

## If you're the owner: where learner messages arrive

Messages sent through your website's contact form arrive in your dashboard under **Website**, in the **Messages** tab. The owner and managers can read them there, and you get a notification when a new one arrives.

![The Messages tab of Website in the dashboard](shot:support/website-messages)

- The contact form works only while your website is published and serving. If your site shows "Coming soon", learners can't reach you through it. See [The Coming Soon page](doc:DOC-ACD-009).
- Make sure your contact email and phone are up to date in your [academy profile](doc:DOC-ACD-002).

For the full guide to the inbox, see [Contact form and website messages](doc:DOC-WEB-013).

If you need help from Atlas yourself, [open a support ticket](doc:DOC-SUP-001).
