The academy owner can send service requests. Managers and instructors can't.

A service request is for work you'd like the Atlas team to do for your academy. If something is broken or you have a question, [open a support ticket](doc:DOC-SUP-001) instead.

## What you can request

| Request | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | A professional logo designed for your academy |
| **Custom domain** | Help registering or connecting your own domain |
| **Custom theme** | A website theme designed around your brand |
| **Website section** | A section for your website that the editor doesn't offer yet |
| **Custom feature** | Something new in Atlas, built for the way your academy works |

## Send a request

1. In the sidebar, under **Settings**, open **Ask Atlas for help**. The page is called **Requests**.

   ![The Requests page with its entry in the sidebar highlighted](shot:support/requests-page)

2. Choose **New request**.

   ![The New request button](shot:support/requests-new-button)

3. Under **What do you need?**, choose the kind of request.

   ![The list of request types in the New request dialog](shot:support/requests-type)

4. Write a short **Title** and a **Description**: what you need, who it's for and any deadline. Fill in the extra **Details** for that kind of request, and choose a **Priority** (choose **High** only when there's a real deadline).

   ![The request form with the Title field filled in](shot:support/requests-brief)

5. Select **Send request**.

   ![The Send request button](shot:support/requests-submit)

You see "Request sent". The Atlas team replies in your dashboard and by email.

## Follow a request

Open the request from the **Requests** list to see its status and its **History**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** / **Received** | Your request reached the Atlas team |
| **Under review** | The team is working out the details |
| **In progress** | The team is working on it |
| **Waiting for you** | The team needs your answer. Read their message and reply under **Reply to the Atlas team** |
| **Completed** | Done. If something isn't right, start a new request and mention its reference |
| **Declined** | The team's message explains why |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it |

## Cancel a request

Open the request, choose **Cancel request** and confirm. A cancelled request can't be reopened, but you can start a new one at any time.

## Cost and timing

The Atlas team reviews every request and replies in the request with next steps. Atlas does not promise a delivery time.

> [!TIP]
> You can also start a request from the page it's about: **Request a logo** on [Visual identity](doc:DOC-ACD-004), and **Request a domain** on the **Domain** tab of your website settings. You can always [connect your own domain](doc:DOC-ACD-006) yourself.

> [!NOTE]
> Requests have no file attachments. Put links to examples you like in the description or details.
