Only the **academy owner** can open these settings. A manager sees "Only the
academy owner can view or change content protection, video and device
settings."

## Steps

1. In the dashboard, open your academy and choose **Academy Settings** in the sidebar.

   ![The academy dashboard with Academy Settings highlighted in the sidebar](shot:protection/settings-nav)

2. Scroll to **Content protection**. Read the **Video protection** card: **Forensic watermark on every video** and the **Player protections** are marked **Always on**. There is nothing to change here.

   ![The Video protection card with Forensic watermark on every video highlighted](shot:protection/settings-video-protection)

3. In **Learner devices**, enter a number in **Devices per learner**, from 1 up to the maximum the page shows.

   ![The Learner devices card with the Devices per learner field highlighted](shot:protection/settings-devices)

4. Choose **Save** in the **Learner devices** card. If you lowered the number, confirm in **Lower the device limit?** with **Lower limit**.

   ![The Learner devices card with its Save button highlighted](shot:protection/settings-devices-save)

## What happens next

You see "Device limits updated", and the line under the fields says
"These limits are set for this academy." The new limit applies to every
learner's next lesson. If you lowered it, learners who are over the new limit
are refused on their most recently added devices until they remove one from
their **Devices** page.

## The other settings on this page

- **Concurrent sessions** is shown to learners on their Devices page. Lessons
  always play on one device at a time whatever this number says
  ([Device limits](doc:DOC-MED-003)).
- **Video protection tier** says there is no tier to choose yet: video
  lessons use YouTube links.

> [!LIMITATION]
> What these settings do **not** do:
>
> - You **can't turn off or change the watermark**, and you can't choose its
>   text. It is always on for every academy.
> - None of these settings stops screen recording or filming.
> - You can't reset or remove a single learner's devices from here, or
>   anywhere in the dashboard. Learners do that themselves
>   ([Manage your devices](doc:DOC-MED-010)).
> - On YouTube lessons, a learner who copies the YouTube link can still watch
>   the video on YouTube without the watermark.
