Owners and managers can open the academy's **Reports**.

## Open the report

1. In the dashboard, open your academy and choose **Reports** in the sidebar.

   ![The academy dashboard with Reports highlighted in the sidebar](shot:protection/reports-nav)

2. At the top of the page, choose the **Window**: the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

   ![The Reports page with the Window selector highlighted](shot:protection/reports-window)

3. Scroll to **Sharing signals** and read the figures.

   ![The Sharing signals section of the Reports page](shot:protection/reports-sharing)

## What the report shows

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Grants** | Times a lesson was opened successfully. |
| **Refusals** | Times a lesson was refused. |
| **Learners refused** | How many different learners were refused. |
| **Devices refused** | How many different devices were refused. |
| **Refusals by reason** | Refusals grouped by why they happened. |
| **Courses with the most refusals** | Where refusals happen most. |
| **Learners with the most refusals** | Who is refused most often. |

The reasons include **Device limit reached**, **Another session active**,
**Access ended**, **Not enrolled** and **Too many requests**. When a period
has a lot of activity, the page says the top lists rank its most recent
records only; the headline figures still cover the whole period.

## What to do with a signal

Many **Device limit reached** and **Another session active** refusals for the
same learner suggest the account is used on too many devices, possibly by
more than one person. You can contact the learner, lower or keep your device
limit ([Content protection settings](doc:DOC-MED-004)), or, if you are sure,
[block the learner](doc:DOC-ORG-008).

> [!LIMITATION]
> What this report does **not** do:
>
> - It doesn't block, warn or flag anyone automatically. It only counts.
> - A refusal isn't proof of sharing: a learner who changed browsers or
>   cleared their browser data also uses up device slots.
> - It can't see screen recording, downloads or copies shared outside Atlas.
> - It can't be exported.
