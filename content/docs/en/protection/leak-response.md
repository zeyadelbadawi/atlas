Every lesson video carries the viewer's personal watermark code
([The forensic watermark explained](doc:DOC-MED-002)). If a recording leaks
and the code is readable, Atlas can tell you which account it came from.
Only Atlas can look codes up; your academy has no lookup screen.

## Before you open a ticket

**Save the evidence.** Keep the link to the copy, the date you found it, and
screenshots of a few frames where the code is clearest. Don't contact the
learner you suspect yet.

**Read the code.** Look for the moving label on the video: ten letters and
digits shown as two groups of five, next to a masked hint of the account.
The same code is also repeated faintly across the whole picture. Write it
down exactly as you see it.

## Ask Atlas to trace it

1. In the dashboard sidebar, open **Support** and choose **New ticket**.

   ![The Support page with the New ticket button highlighted](shot:protection/support-center)

2. In **New support ticket**, write a **Subject** and, under **What is happening?**, give the code, the course, and where and when you found the copy.

   ![The New support ticket form with the Subject field highlighted](shot:protection/support-new-ticket)

3. Under **Image (optional)**, attach a screenshot of a frame that shows the code.

   ![The New support ticket form with the image attachment highlighted](shot:protection/support-ticket-attach)

4. Choose **Submit ticket**. The Atlas team replies in the same ticket.

   ![The New support ticket form with the Submit ticket button highlighted](shot:protection/support-ticket-submit)

## What Atlas does

Atlas looks the code up in its own records, which link each code to the
account and sign-in it was issued to and the lesson it was shown on. The
lookup tolerates common misreadings of characters, but it can't invent a code
that isn't readable. Atlas replies in your ticket.

## Act in your academy

What happens to the learner is your decision. In your academy you can
[block the learner](doc:DOC-ORG-008) or
[end their enrollment](doc:DOC-ORG-010). Actions apply to your academy
only.

> [!LIMITATION]
> When tracing is **not** possible, or limited:
>
> - **No readable code.** A copy that is cropped, blurred, re-encoded at low
>   quality or covered may not show a readable code.
> - **Copied YouTube links.** If someone shared the YouTube video itself, that
>   copy has no watermark and can't be traced.
> - **No promised response time.** Atlas handles tickets as they come and
>   doesn't promise how fast it replies.
> - **Records expire.** A code's record is kept up to two years after it was
>   last shown.
> - Tracing tells you which account watched; it doesn't remove the copy from
>   the site where it was posted. Reporting it to that site, or legal steps,
>   happen outside Atlas.
