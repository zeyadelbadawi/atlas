Atlas groups everything you own under an **organization**. You create it when you sign up, and you are its owner.

## What an organization is

- It holds your **subscription**: the plan, the trial, payments and usage limits. See [Your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-001).
- It holds your **academies**. All academies in one organization share its plan. See [Create another academy](doc:DOC-ACD-007).
- Its owner is the only person who can add or remove staff, see billing and change owner-only settings.

Managers and instructors work in academies inside your organization. Atlas doesn't let a manager's or instructor's account create an organization.

## Your organization page

The organization page at [/dashboard/organization](app:/dashboard/organization) shows its **Slug**, **Your relationship** and the date it was **Created**. It has nothing to edit.

![The Organization page with its slug, your relationship and creation date](shot:team/organization-overview)

## Create another organization

A second organization is useful for a separate business with its own plan and billing. Open [/dashboard/organization/create](app:/dashboard/organization/create), type the **Organization name** and select **Create organization**.

![The Create your organization page](shot:team/organization-create)

A new organization starts with no plan. The trial can be used only once per email address, so choose and pay for a plan before you create academies in it. See [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002).

## Switch organizations

When you belong to two or more organizations, **Switch organization** appears in the top bar. Choose the organization you want to work in. With only one organization, the switcher isn't shown. See [Switch between academies and organizations](doc:DOC-ACD-008).

## What you can't change

> [!LIMITATION]
> An organization can't be renamed, deleted or handed over to another owner. Organization names are unique across Atlas, so pick the name carefully when you create one.
