The **Students** tab is your academy's learner list. The owner and managers use it to see and manage learners. Instructors see their own students from the **Teaching Dashboard** instead (see below).

## Open the list and find a learner

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Members**, then the **Students** tab.

   ![The Students tab on the Members page](shot:team/students-tab)

2. Use the search box to find a name or email, **Filter by status** to narrow the list, and **Sort by** to change the order.

   ![The search box, status filter and sort menu above the list](shot:team/students-filters)

3. Select a learner to open their details.

   ![A learner's details panel with enrollments and progress](shot:team/student-drawer)

## What the list shows

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Name and email. Owners and managers also see the phone number, if the learner added one. |
| **Status** | **Active**, **Pending approval**, **Inactive** (a rejected registration) or **Blocked**. |
| **Joined via** | How they joined: **Website sign-up**, **Website sign-in**, **Added by staff**, **Purchase**, **Invite link**, **Imported** or **Other**. |
| **Enrollments** | Active enrollments out of all their enrollments. |
| **Joined** | The date they joined your academy. |
| **Last activity** | The last time they did something in your academy. |

The sort options are **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Recently active**, **Name (A–Z)** and **Name (Z–A)**.

## What the details show

- Whether their email is verified, and how many **Active sessions** they have.
- **Joined via**, **Joined** and **Last activity**, and the **Reason for blocking** if they are blocked.
- **Enrollments**: each course, with its enrollment date, its expiry date if it has one, and its **Course progress** (percentage and lessons or activities done).
- **Quiz outcomes**: each attempt, with its score and result.
- **Assignment outcomes**: each submission, its grade and whether feedback was given.

Owners and managers also see the action buttons here: **Approve** and **Reject** for pending learners, **Approve** for a learner whose registration you rejected (**Inactive**), **Block** or **Unblock**, and **Enroll in a course** for active learners. See [Approve pending learners](doc:DOC-ORG-007), [Block or unblock a learner](doc:DOC-ORG-008) and [Enroll a learner manually](doc:DOC-ORG-010).

## What instructors see

Instructors don't have the Members page. In the **Teaching Dashboard**, they open one of their courses and its **Students** page, which lists that course's students with their progress and last activity. They never see phone numbers, and they can't change a learner's membership or enrollments.

![The Students page of a course in the Teaching Dashboard](shot:team/students-instructor)

## Phone numbers and privacy

A learner's phone number is visible to the academy owner and managers, and to Atlas platform administrators. Instructors and other learners never see it. Learners are told this when they add their number. Use phone numbers only to help learners with their studies and their account.

> [!LIMITATION]
> You can't export or download the student list.
