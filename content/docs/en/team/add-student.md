The academy **owner** can add a student by email, instead of waiting for them to sign up on the website. Managers can't add students; they see **Only the account owner can add people.** You need the student's email and full name.

## Add a student

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Members**. The **Team** tab is open.

   ![The Members page with the Team tab selected](shot:team/members-team-tab)

2. Select **Add Student**.

   ![The Add Student button on the Members page](shot:team/add-student-button)

3. Type the student's **Email address**. Atlas tells you if they are already in this academy.

   ![The Create a Student Account dialog with an email typed in](shot:team/add-student-email)

4. Type their **Full name**. People who already use Atlas keep their own name.

   ![The Full name field filled in](shot:team/add-student-name)

5. Select **Add student**.

   ![The Add student button in the dialog](shot:team/add-student-submit)

## What the student receives

The dialog then shows one of these results. Select **Done** to close it.

- **Invitation sent**: they had no Atlas account. They get an email with a link to choose their own password. You never share a password with them.
- **Student added**: they already had an account. They sign in to your academy website with their usual email and password. We email them to let them know, or send a fresh setup link if they never finished setting up their account.

They appear on the **Students** tab with **Added by staff** under **Joined via**.

## Give them a course

Adding a student doesn't enroll them in anything. Open them on the **Students** tab and choose **Enroll in a course**. See [Enroll a learner manually](doc:DOC-ORG-010).

## If it doesn't work

- **This student is blocked. Unblock them first.** You blocked this person earlier. [Unblock them](doc:DOC-ORG-008) instead of adding them again.
- **A learner in this academy already has this name. Add a middle or family name so it is unique here.** Two learners in one academy can't share the same name.
- **This Atlas account can't be added right now.** The account is suspended or deleted.

> [!LIMITATION]
> You add students one at a time. There is no bulk import or CSV upload.
