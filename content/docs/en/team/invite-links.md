Invite links let specific people register on your academy website. The owner and managers can create and revoke them.

## When you need invite links

Invite links are needed only when your [registration policy](doc:DOC-ORG-005) is **Invite only**. With **Open** or **Approval required**, learners sign up without a link, and approval still applies even if they use one.

## Create an invite link

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Academy Settings** and choose **Student registration** in the list of sections. **Invite links** is below the registration policy.

   ![The Invite links card in Academy Settings](shot:team/invites-card)

2. Select **Create invite**.

   ![The Create invite button](shot:team/invites-create-button)

3. In **Create an invite link**, leave **Email address (optional)** empty for a link anyone can use, or type one address. Then set **Maximum uses** and **Valid for (days)**.

   ![The Create an invite link dialog with its three fields](shot:team/invites-create-dialog)

4. Select **Create invite**.

   ![The Create invite button at the bottom of the dialog](shot:team/invites-create-submit)

## Copy and share the link

Right after you create it, Atlas shows the **Invite link** with a copy button. **This is the only time it is shown.** Copy it straight away and send it, for example on WhatsApp or by email. If your academy has no public web address yet, Atlas shows an **Invite code** instead.

If you lose a link, you can't show it again. Create a new one, and revoke the old one if you need to.

> [!WARNING]
> Treat an "anyone" link like a key. Everyone who has it can register, until its uses run out or it expires.

## Track your links

The table on the card lists every link: who it is **For** (an email, or **Anyone with the link**), its **Uses**, when it **Expires** and its **Status**: **Active**, **Revoked**, **Expired** or **Fully used**.

## Revoke a link

1. In the table, select **Revoke** next to the link.

   ![The Revoke action next to an invite link](shot:team/invites-revoke-button)

2. Confirm with **Revoke invite**.

   ![The confirmation with the Revoke invite button](shot:team/invites-revoke-confirm)

The link stops working immediately. Anyone who already registered with it keeps their account.

## If a learner can't use the link

- The link may have expired, been revoked or been fully used. Check its status in the table and create a new one.
- A link made for one email works only for that email. The learner must sign up with exactly that address.
- See [A learner can't join the academy](doc:DOC-TRB-014) for more.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Each link is created one at a time. There is no bulk invite or CSV upload, and Atlas doesn't send the link for you: you share it yourself.
