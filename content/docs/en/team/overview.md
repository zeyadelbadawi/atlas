An academy on Atlas has two kinds of people. Your **team** runs the academy from the Atlas dashboard. Your **learners** study on the academy website. They are separate lists with separate rules, and they share one place in the dashboard: **Members**.

![The Members page with the Team tab open](shot:team/members-team-tab)

## Team members and learners are different

| | Team | Learners |
|---|---|---|
| Who | The owner, managers and instructors | Students |
| Where they sign in | The Atlas dashboard | Your academy website |
| Where you see them | **Members** → **Team** | **Members** → **Students** |
| How they get in | The organization owner adds them by email | They sign up on your website, or the owner adds them |

Both use the same kind of Atlas account: one email, one password. A learner's account can also be used at other academies, but each academy sees only its own learners. See [How learner accounts work across academies](doc:DOC-ORG-011).

## The three team roles

- **Owner**: the person who owns the organization. They can do everything, including billing, payments, adding and removing people, and owner-only settings.
- **Manager**: runs the academy day to day. Managers manage courses, content, the website and most academy settings, and they look after learners: approve, block, enroll and invite them. They can't add or remove anyone from the team, change the registration policy, or see billing, payments, revenue, Student Analytics or the activity log.
- **Instructor**: teaches the courses they are assigned to. Instructors work from the **Teaching Dashboard**: they see the students of their own courses, review quiz results and grade assignments. They don't see the Members page or academy settings.

There is no way to change someone's role. To give a person a different role, remove them and add them again with the new role. For the full list, see [Roles and permissions](doc:DOC-ROLE-005).

## Who can add whom

| Action | Owner | Manager | Instructor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add a manager or instructor | Yes | No | No |
| Remove a manager or instructor | Yes | No | No |
| Add a student by email | Yes | No | No |
| Approve, reject, block or unblock learners | Yes | Yes | No |
| Enroll a learner in a course by hand | Yes | Yes | No |
| Create invite links for learners | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change the registration policy | Yes | No | No |

A manager opening **Members** sees the lists, plus the note **Only the account owner can add people.** instead of the add buttons.

![The Members page as a manager, with the owner-only note](shot:team/members-manager-view)

## How learners join

Learners usually join from your academy website. Your registration policy decides how: anyone can join, each sign-up waits for your approval, or only people with an invite link can register. See [Choose who can join](doc:DOC-ORG-005), [Invite links](doc:DOC-ORG-006) and [Approve pending learners](doc:DOC-ORG-007).

The owner can also add a student by email. See [Add a student yourself](doc:DOC-ORG-004).

## Plan limits

Instructors count toward your plan's instructor limit. Managers don't. Learners count toward the student limit once they hold a course enrollment. To see your limits and what you use, open the Usage page or see [Plans and limits](site:/pricing).

> [!LIMITATION]
> There is no bulk import, CSV upload or export of team members or learners. People are added one at a time.
