Block a learner when they should no longer use your academy, for example after sharing their account. The owner and managers can block and unblock.

## What blocking does

- They are signed out of your academy website and can't sign in to it again.
- They lose access to every course in your academy at once. A lesson they already have open stops shortly after.
- Their account, enrollments and progress are kept, and come back when you unblock them.
- It affects your academy only. If they learn at another academy on Atlas, that doesn't change.
- Atlas tells the learner they were blocked.

## Block a learner

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Members**, then the **Students** tab.

   ![The Students tab on the Members page](shot:team/students-tab)

2. Select the learner to open their details, then select **Block**.

   ![The learner's details with the Block button](shot:team/block-drawer)

3. In **Block this student**, type a **Reason (optional)** if you want, then select **Block student**.

   ![The Block this student dialog with a reason typed in](shot:team/block-dialog)

The message **Student blocked** confirms it, and their status becomes **Blocked**. The reason is visible to your team in the learner's details, under **Reason for blocking**.

## Unblock a learner

1. Open the blocked learner on the **Students** tab and select **Unblock**.

   ![A blocked learner's details with the Unblock button and the reason for blocking](shot:team/unblock-drawer)

The message **Student unblocked** confirms it. They can sign in again and find their courses and progress as they left them.

> [!NOTE]
> You can't enroll a blocked learner in a course, or add them again with **Add Student**. Unblock them first.

## After a leak

If you think a learner shared their account or recorded your lessons, block them first, then trace the account. See [If your content leaks](doc:DOC-MED-005) and [Protect your content from sharing](doc:DOC-TASK-004).
