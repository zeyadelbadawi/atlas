The registration policy decides who can create an account on your academy website. Only the **organization owner** can change it. Managers see the options but can't save them.

## The three options

| Option | What a new learner gets | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | They sign up and can start learning straight away. | Nothing. |
| **Approval required** | Their sign-up waits as **Pending approval**. They can open courses only after you approve it. | [Approve or reject](doc:DOC-ORG-007) each one on the Students tab. |
| **Invite only** | They can register only with a valid invite link. | [Create invite links](doc:DOC-ORG-006) and send them. |

## Change the policy

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Academy Settings** and scroll down to **Registration**.

   ![The Registration section of Academy Settings with the three options](shot:team/registration-section)

2. Under **Registration policy**, choose **Open**, **Invite only** or **Approval required**.

   ![Approval required selected](shot:team/registration-choose)

3. Select **Save policy**.

   ![The Save policy button](shot:team/registration-save)

The message **Registration policy updated** confirms the change. New sign-ups follow it from that moment.

## How invite links behave under each policy

- **Open**: no link is needed. Anyone can sign up.
- **Approval required**: no link is needed, and a link doesn't skip approval. Every new sign-up still waits for you.
- **Invite only**: a valid link is required. A link made for one email address works only for that address.

## People who already have an Atlas account

A learner who already uses Atlas at another academy can join yours with the same account. With **Open**, they join as soon as they sign in on your website. With **Approval required** or **Invite only**, signing in isn't enough: they go through sign-up like everyone else.

A learner whose registration you rejected can sign up again the same way, and the policy in force at that moment applies to them: with **Approval required** they wait for you again, with **Open** they are admitted, and with **Invite only** they need a valid link. See [Approve pending learners](doc:DOC-ORG-007).

## Who can change it

Only the organization owner. Anyone else sees **Only the account owner can change the registration policy.** under the options.

![The registration options as a manager, with the owner-only note](shot:team/registration-manager)

> [!NOTE]
> Changing the policy doesn't remove anyone. Learners who already joined stay, and blocking is a separate action. See [Block or unblock a learner](doc:DOC-ORG-008).

## Check that it works

Open your academy website in a private browser window and try to sign up with a test email. If a learner tells you they can't join, see [A learner can't join the academy](doc:DOC-TRB-014).
