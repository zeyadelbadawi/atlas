Atlas gives each person one account, tied to one email address. A learner who studies with two teachers on Atlas uses the same email and password at both academies. What they do in each academy stays in that academy.

## One account, many academies

When a learner who already uses Atlas signs up on your academy website, they can join with their existing account instead of creating a new one. After joining, the learner sees which other academies already use this account, and that their courses, progress and certificates stay separate in each academy. Only the learner sees that list. You never learn which other academies they belong to.

## What stays separate

- **Courses and progress**: enrollments, lesson progress, quiz attempts and assignments belong to the academy that runs the course.
- **Certificates**: each academy issues its own.
- **Sign-in**: a learner signs in on each academy's own website. A session on one academy's site doesn't open another academy.
- **Blocking**: if you block a learner, they lose access to your academy only. See [Block or unblock a learner](doc:DOC-ORG-008).

Learners don't sign in to the Atlas dashboard; that is for academy staff. See [Where to sign in](doc:DOC-IAM-002).

## What your academy can see about a learner

Your academy sees its own learners only. On the [Students tab](doc:DOC-ORG-009), the owner and managers see a learner's name, email, phone number if they added one, how and when they joined, their last activity, and their enrollments, progress and results in your courses. Instructors see the students of their own courses, without phone numbers. No academy sees another academy's learners or what they do there. See [How your academy's data is kept separate](doc:DOC-SEC-002).

## Two learners with the same name

Inside one academy, two learners can't have exactly the same name when they sign up or are added by staff. Atlas asks the person to add a middle or family name so the name is unique in that academy. The same name can exist in different academies.

## Deleting a learner account

An academy can't delete a learner's Atlas account. You can block them, reject their registration or revoke their courses. Because one account serves every academy, only the learner can ask for it to be deleted. See [Delete your account](doc:DOC-IAM-010).
