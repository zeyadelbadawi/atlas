Only the **organization owner** sees the **Remove** action, and only next to active managers and instructors. The owner can never be removed.

## Remove someone

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Members**. The **Team** tab is open.

   ![The Members page with the Team tab selected](shot:team/members-team-tab)

2. Next to the person, select **Remove**.

   ![The Remove action next to a team member](shot:team/remove-staff-button)

3. In **Remove this team member?**, select **Remove from academy**.

   ![The confirmation with the Remove from academy button](shot:team/remove-staff-confirm)

A message confirms that the person was removed from the academy.

## What happens

- They lose access to this academy straight away, including any courses they teach here. Their next action in the dashboard is refused.
- Their assignments to this academy's courses are removed. Assignments in your other academies stay.
- If this was the last academy they worked in for your organization, they also leave your organization.
- Their Atlas account is not deleted, and the record of what they did stays in your activity log.
- Removing an instructor frees a place in your plan's instructor limit.

## Adding them back, or changing a role

Atlas has no "change role" option. To change someone's role, remove them, then [add them again](doc:DOC-ORG-002) with the new role. Adding them back reactivates their place in the team. If they were an instructor, assign them to their courses again: their old course assignments don't come back.

> [!NOTE]
> Removing someone doesn't sign them out of Atlas: their account may still work in other organizations. In your academy, everything they try is refused from that moment on.
