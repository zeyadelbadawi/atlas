## Still stuck?

- **Academy owners, managers and instructors:** open a ticket from **Support** in the dashboard sidebar ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)). See [Getting help](category:support).
- **Learners:** Atlas support tickets are for academy staff. Contact your academy: in My Learn, choose **Contact your academy**.
