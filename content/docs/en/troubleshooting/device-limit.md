## Symptom

You open a lesson and see **"You have reached your device limit"**, with "This browser is not one of your registered devices, and all of them are in use." or a list of your devices. The full message can also read: "You are already learning on the maximum number of devices allowed. Open Devices in your learner dashboard, remove one you no longer use, then try again."

A different message, **"You are already learning on another device"**, means something else: see [Learning on two screens at once](#learning-on-two-screens-at-once) below.

## Why it happens

Each academy sets how many devices a learner can learn on. A "device" is a browser on a phone, tablet or computer, not the hardware itself. Atlas counts a new device when you:

- sign in from a new phone, tablet or computer,
- use a different browser on the same computer,
- use a private or incognito window,
- clear your browser's cookies or site data.

Signing in is never refused because of the limit: only lessons are. So you can always reach your Devices page from the new browser and fix it yourself.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Academy staff can't reset or remove a learner's devices. Only you can remove them, from your own Devices page. You don't need to wait for the academy.

## Fix

1. In My Learn, open **Devices**. If the lesson showed **Manage your devices**, you can choose that instead.

   ![My Learn with the Devices link highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/learner-devices-nav)

2. Under **Registered devices**, find a device you no longer use (check "Last used") and choose **Remove**, then confirm "Remove this device?".

   ![The Devices page with the Registered devices list highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/learner-devices-list)

3. Go back to the lesson and open it again. This browser registers in the free slot.

You can also remove a device from inside the limit dialog itself: each device in the list has **Remove**. The device you are using right now is marked **This device** and isn't offered there.

> [!NOTE]
> A removed device has to register again the next time you learn on it, and it takes a slot again when it does. If you remove the device you are using now (from the Devices page), your current learning session ends.

## Learning on two screens at once

Lessons play on one device at a time. If you open a lesson on a second device, you see **"You are already learning on another device"**. Choose **Continue here** to end the session on the other device and learn here, or **Keep learning there**. This doesn't add a device; it only moves where you are learning.

## For owners: the limit itself

The organization owner sets the limit in **Academy Settings**, under **Learner devices** (**Devices per learner**). The academy can set a limit up to the maximum the platform allows. Lowering it applies to devices learners have already registered: a learner over the new limit is refused on their most recently added devices until they remove one.

![Academy Settings with the Devices per learner setting highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/owner-device-policy)

## Still stuck?

- **Learners:** if you removed a device and the lesson still doesn't open, contact your academy (in My Learn, choose **Contact your academy**) and tell them the exact message.
- **Academy owners and managers:** open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)). See [Getting help](category:support).
