## Symptom

A panel at the top of the dashboard says one of these:

- **"Your trial has ended"**: "You were trialing … Subscribe to pick up exactly where you left off, or choose a different plan."
- **"Your subscription has expired"**: "You need an active subscription to continue managing your academies."

When you try to save something, Atlas refuses with "An active subscription is required to make this change." or "Your subscription isn't currently active. Renew or upgrade your plan to continue."

## Why it happens

Your trial or paid period ended and no new payment has been approved yet. Atlas then stops changes, but not reads:

| Still works | Stops until you subscribe |
|---|---|
| Opening and reading your academies, courses, students and reports | Saving any change: courses, website, members, settings |
| Plans, checkout, paying and uploading your payment proof | Your academy websites: visitors see Coming soon |
| Billing history and Support | Creating new academies |
| Approving or rejecting payments learners already sent (owner) | Setting up or changing payment methods |

"Nothing has been deleted. Your academies, courses, students and content are all still here, exactly as you left them."

Two other panels look similar but mean everything still works:

- **"Your payment is late — your site is still online"**: you are in the grace period after a paid period ended. Pay before the date shown and nothing changes.
- **"Your subscription is scheduled to end"**: you cancelled, and everything keeps working until the end of the current period.

## Fix

Only the organization owner can choose a plan and pay. Managers and instructors should ask the owner.

1. In the panel, choose **Continue with** your plan, **Choose a plan** or **Reactivate subscription**. You can also open **Plans** from the sidebar.

   ![The Plans page](shot:troubleshooting/plans-page)

2. Compare plans if you want to change, then go to checkout and pay with one of the payment methods shown. Upload your proof of payment when asked. Prices are on the [pricing page](site:/pricing).

   ![The Subscription page with the Compare plans button highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/compare-plans)

3. Follow the payment under **Billing** in its payment history. It shows **Awaiting review** until the Atlas team checks it. Once it is approved, your dashboard and websites work again.

   ![The Payment History page with the Review column highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/subscription-payment-history)

> [!NOTE]
> Don't pay twice while a payment is awaiting review. If your payment was rejected, see [Payment proof pending or rejected](doc:DOC-TRB-008).

## Still stuck?

If your payment has been awaiting review longer than you expected, or no payment method appears at checkout, open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)). Support stays available while your subscription is inactive. See [Getting help](category:support).
