## Symptom

You are waiting for an email from Atlas or your academy, such as a 6-digit sign-in code, a password reset link, a verification link or a notification, and it doesn't arrive. Or you see one of these messages:

- "Please wait a moment before requesting another code."
- "No more codes can be sent for this sign-in. Please sign in again."
- "We can't deliver email to this address right now, so a code can't be sent."

## Why it happens

| Cause | What to look for |
|---|---|
| The email went to spam, junk or another tab | Most mail apps file unknown senders away from the inbox. |
| You are looking at a different inbox | The email goes to the address on your account, which may not be the one you check every day. |
| You asked again too soon | Atlas makes you wait a moment between codes, and limits how many codes one sign-in can request. |
| Only the newest code or link works | A new code or link replaces the earlier ones. An older email won't work even if it arrives later. |
| The address can't receive mail | Earlier emails to this address bounced, so Atlas has stopped sending to it for now. |
| No account uses that address | For your security, "Forgot password" always says "If an account exists with that email, you will receive a password reset link shortly." Nothing is sent when no account uses the address. |
| You switched that kind of email off, or chose a daily digest | Notification emails follow your Email preferences. |
| It isn't sent by email | Announcements appear in your notifications, not by email. |

## Fix

### Codes and links

1. Search your mailbox, including spam and junk, for the newest email from Atlas or from your academy.
2. Wait a few minutes. Delivery to some email providers can be slow, and asking again replaces the code you are waiting for.
3. If you still have nothing, use **Send a new code** on the code screen, or **Send a new link** on the verification page. If the button shows a countdown, wait for it to finish.
4. After "No more codes can be sent for this sign-in. Please sign in again.", go back to the sign-in page and start again.
5. For a verification link that says "Sign in to confirm your email", sign in with the account the link was sent to, then open the link again. "Your link has not been used."

If you typed the wrong address when you signed up, the email went there. Your email address can't be changed from your profile ("Your email address can't be changed here yet."), so contact support (academy staff) or your academy (learners).

### "We can't deliver email to this address right now"

Atlas stopped sending to this address because earlier messages bounced. Check with your email provider that the mailbox exists and isn't full. Then academy staff should open a support ticket and learners should contact their academy, so the address can be checked.

### Notification emails

1. Open your profile and the **Preferences** tab. Academy staff open **Profile** from the account menu in the dashboard.

   ![The dashboard Profile page on the Preferences tab with Email preferences highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/owner-email-preferences)

2. Under **Email preferences**, check that the category you are missing is switched on, and whether it is set to **As it happens** or **Daily digest**. Changes save as you make them.

Learners find the same settings in My Learn, under **Profile**, on the **Preferences** tab.

![A learner's Profile page on the Preferences tab with Email preferences highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/learner-email-preferences)

> [!NOTE]
> Security alerts, receipts and account changes are **Always on**: you can't switch them off, so a missing receipt or security email is never caused by your preferences.

## Still stuck?

- **Academy owners, managers and instructors:** open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)) with the address and the kind of email you are missing. See [Getting help](category:support).
- **Learners:** contact your academy (in My Learn, choose **Contact your academy**).
