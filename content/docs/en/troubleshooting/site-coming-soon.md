## Symptom

You open your academy's address and see **"Coming soon"** with "This academy's website hasn't been published yet. Please check back soon — there will be something here shortly." Or your site is live, but the edits you just made don't appear.

## Why it happens

There are three causes:

1. **The website isn't published.** A new site stays unpublished until you publish it, and a site taken offline with **Unpublish** also shows Coming soon. In the dashboard, the publish bar says "Your website is not published yet. Visitors see a Coming Soon page." or "Your website is offline and not reachable by visitors."
2. **Your trial or subscription has ended.** Atlas stops serving the website and shows the same Coming soon page. Visitors are never told about billing; the page looks the same as an unpublished site. Your dashboard shows that the trial or subscription ended.
3. **Your changes are saved but not published.** The site is live, but visitors still see the last published version. The publish bar then says "This website is live." followed by how many pages or settings have unpublished changes.

## Fix

### Publish the website

Owners and managers can publish.

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Website**. Read the publish bar at the top: its status shows **Draft**, **Published**, **Publishing…** or **Publish failed**.

   ![The Website page with the publish bar status highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/website-publish-bar)

2. If the site isn't published, choose **Publish**, then confirm "Publish this website?". Everything you have saved, site settings and every page, goes live.
3. Open your academy's address again. If the status shows **Publish failed**, choose **Try again** under the bar.

### Publish your latest changes

- In the publish bar, choose **Publish changes** to publish everything you have saved.
- Or, in the Page Editor, choose **Publish page** to publish just that page. **Save draft** keeps your edits hidden from visitors.

![The Page Editor with the Publish page button highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/page-editor-publish-page)

### If your trial or subscription has ended

Publishing doesn't help while the subscription is inactive. Choose a plan and pay; once the payment is approved, your site is served again with everything as you left it. See [The dashboard is read-only or asks for a subscription](doc:DOC-TRB-015).

> [!NOTE]
> The Coming soon page carries your academy's name and shows "Powered by Atlas".

## Still stuck?

If the site is published, your subscription is active, and visitors still see Coming soon, open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)) with your academy's address. See [Getting help](category:support).
