## Symptom

You choose **Save changes** (or **Save draft**, or **Publish page**) in the Page Editor and a dialog opens: **"This page changed while you were editing"**, with "Someone saved a newer version of this page." or the name of the person who did.

## Why it happens

Two people, or the same person in two browser tabs, edited the same page. When the first one saved, your copy became out of date. Atlas doesn't silently overwrite their work, so it asks you what to do.

Owners and managers can edit website pages.

## Fix

1. Read the dialog: "Reload to see their version and start again from it, or keep your changes and apply them on top of theirs. Their work is never discarded either way."
2. Choose one option:
   - **Reload latest**: the editor loads the saved version. Your unsaved edits on this page are dropped, so copy any text you want to keep first, then redo your edit.
   - **Keep my changes**: Atlas saves the page as it is on your screen, as the newest version. Check the page afterwards to make sure the other person's edit is still the way you both want it.
3. Save again if needed. When the site is live, your saved changes reach visitors only when you publish them.

![The Page Editor with the save button highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/page-editor-save)

## Avoid conflicts

- When someone else has the same page open, the editor shows a banner such as "Karim (Manager) is currently editing this page." Agree who edits first.
- Save small changes often instead of keeping a page open for a long time.
- If you try to leave with unsaved edits, Atlas asks "Leave without saving?": choose **Stay on this page**, **Save and leave**, or **Leave and discard**.

## Still stuck?

If the dialog keeps coming back while nobody else is editing, close other tabs with the same page open and reload. If it still happens, open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)). See [Getting help](category:support).
