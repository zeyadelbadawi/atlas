## Symptom

A learner tries to sign up or sign in on the academy's website and sees one of these:

- "Your membership is awaiting approval" or "Your request to join … has been sent. You'll be able to sign in once the academy approves it."
- "This academy accepts new students by invitation only. Use the invitation link you were sent."
- "This invitation link is invalid, already used or expired. Ask the academy for a new one."
- "You are not a member of this academy yet."
- "You already have an Atlas account"
- "Your access to this academy has been blocked. Please contact the academy."
- "Please use a permanent email address you can receive mail at. Temporary or disposable addresses aren't accepted."
- "This phone number is already used by another account."

## Why it happens

| Message | Cause | Who fixes it |
|---|---|---|
| Awaiting approval | The academy uses **Approval required**: each registration waits until the academy approves it. | Owner or manager |
| Invitation only / invite link invalid | The academy uses **Invite only**: only someone with a valid invite link can register. Links can be used up or expire. | Owner or manager sends a new link |
| Not a member of this academy yet | The person has an Atlas account but isn't a learner here, and the academy isn't open. | The learner registers (or uses an invite), then the academy approves |
| You already have an Atlas account | The email is already registered on Atlas, usually from another academy. One Atlas account works in many academies. | The learner |
| Blocked | The academy blocked this learner. | Owner or manager |
| An email saying the registration was not approved, or "You haven't been admitted to this academy" after signing in | The academy rejected the registration. The learner can't open or buy its courses. A rejection isn't final. | The learner applies again, or the owner or a manager approves them |
| Permanent email address | Temporary or disposable email addresses are refused. | The learner uses a real address |
| Phone number already used | Each phone number can belong to one account only. | The learner, or support |
| "Your account name matches a learner already in this academy." | Learner names must be unique inside one academy. | The learner adds a middle or family name in their profile |

## Fix

### Learners

- **Awaiting approval:** wait for the academy. "You will be able to open your courses as soon as it is approved."
- **Invite only:** open the invite link you were sent, or ask the academy for a new one.
- **"You already have an Atlas account":** you don't need a new account. Choose **Continue with my Atlas account** and enter the password you already use. Forgot it? Choose **Forgot your password?**. Your courses and progress in each academy stay separate.
- **"You are not a member of this academy yet"** on the sign-in page: choose **Create a student account** to register with this academy.
- **"You haven't been admitted to this academy":** choose **Apply again**. You are signed out, then enter your Atlas account's email and password on the sign-up page. The academy decides again under its rules. Or contact it.
- **Blocked, phone or anything else:** contact the academy (use its Contact page).

### Owners and managers: approve a pending learner

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Members** and the **Students** tab. Learners waiting for you show **Pending approval**.

   ![The Students tab with a learner's Pending approval status highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/students-pending)

2. Open the learner and choose **Approve** (or **Reject**).

   ![The student panel with the Approve button highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/student-drawer-approve)

A learner whose registration you rejected shows **Rejected**. You can approve them the same way. See [Approve pending learners](doc:DOC-ORG-007).

### Owners and managers: unblock a learner

1. On the **Students** tab, find the learner marked **Blocked**.

   ![The Students tab with a Blocked status highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/students-blocked)

2. Open the learner and choose **Unblock**.

### Owners: check the registration policy

The registration policy decides how new learners get in: **Open**, **Approval required** or **Invite only**. "Only the account owner can change the registration policy."

1. Open **Academy Settings**, choose the **Student registration** section and find **Registration policy**.

   ![Academy Settings with the Registration policy card highlighted](shot:troubleshooting/registration-policy)

2. Choose the option you want and select **Save policy**.

> [!NOTE]
> Joining the academy doesn't count towards your plan's student limit. A learner counts once they are enrolled in a course. If enrolling fails, see [You've reached a plan limit](doc:DOC-TRB-010).

## Still stuck?

- **Learners:** contact the academy. Atlas support tickets are for academy staff.
- **Owners and managers:** open a ticket from **Support** ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)) with the learner's email and the message they see. See [Getting help](category:support).
