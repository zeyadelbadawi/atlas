Payments to your academy go straight from the learner to the academy's own bank account, wallet or InstaPay address. Atlas never holds that money, so it can't send it back. The academy returns it, and then records the refund in Atlas.

## If you are a learner

Open **Purchases** on the academy website. Next to a course you paid the academy for, you see "Paid to the academy directly. For a refund, contact the academy." There is no refund button for these purchases.

![The learner's Purchases page with the "contact the academy" refund note highlighted](shot:selling/learner-refund-contact)

Contact the academy, for example through its website's contact form, and agree the refund with them. When the academy records the refund, the order shows **Refunded** with the amount and date, your access to the course ends, and you get an email.

## If you are the academy owner

Only the organization owner can record a refund, the same person who approves payments.

1. Agree the refund with the learner.
2. Send the money back yourself, from your own bank, wallet or InstaPay app.
3. In the academy's **Payments**, open the **Approved** tab and select the learner's payment.
4. Choose **Record refund…**. If you want, write a **Note for the learner (optional)**: it's shown to the learner and sent in their email.
5. Choose **Record refund**, read the confirmation and choose **Record refund** again.

Record it only after the money has gone back: a refund can't be undone.

## What happens next

- The learner's access to the course ends straight away. Their progress is kept.
- The order shows **Refunded** in [Course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004) and in the learner's **Purchases**, and the payment shows **Refunded** in **Payments**, with who recorded it and when.
- The payment drops out of your sales figures (**Sales recorded in Atlas (manual payments)**).
- The learner gets an email and a notification that the academy recorded the refund, with your note.

Recording a refund also works while your Atlas subscription has lapsed, so you can settle a learner's payment at any time.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't move money and has no partial refunds: recording a refund always records the full amount of the payment. Your academy decides its own refund policy and sends the money itself.

> [!NOTE]
> **Revoke** on a student's course only ends access. It doesn't record a refund, and the order stays **Paid**. If you gave the money back, use **Record refund…** instead. See [Enroll a learner manually and set access expiry](doc:DOC-ORG-010).
