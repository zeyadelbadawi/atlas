Payments to your academy go straight from the learner to the academy's own bank account, wallet or InstaPay address. Atlas never holds that money, so it can't send it back. Refunds are arranged between the learner and the academy.

## If you are a learner

Open **Purchases** on the academy website. Next to a course you paid the academy for, you see "Paid to the academy directly. For a refund, contact the academy." There is no refund button for these purchases.

![The learner's Purchases page with the "contact the academy" refund note highlighted](shot:selling/learner-refund-contact)

Contact the academy, for example through its website's contact form, and agree the refund with them.

## If you are the academy owner

1. Agree the refund with the learner.
2. Send the money back yourself, from your own bank, wallet or InstaPay app.
3. End the learner's access to the course if you want to. In **Members**, open the student on the **Students** tab and use **Revoke** on the course. Choose a **Reason** (**Revoked by staff** fits a refund), then confirm with **Revoke access**. The learner loses access to the course straight away, and their progress is kept.

   The dialog reminds you: "Revoking access doesn't refund the learner. If they paid for this course, return the money to them yourself. Atlas doesn't move money, and the order stays recorded as paid in your sales." There is no **Refunded** reason to choose.

The owner and managers can revoke access. See [Enroll a learner manually and set access expiry](doc:DOC-ORG-010) for more on managing a student's courses.

## What changes in Atlas

- The order stays **Paid** in [Course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004). Revoking access doesn't record a refund or change the order. Keep your own record of refunds you make.
- The dashboard's sales total doesn't subtract money you give back outside Atlas.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas has no refund button for payments made to the academy, doesn't move money, and doesn't do partial refunds. Your academy decides its own refund policy and sends any refund itself.
