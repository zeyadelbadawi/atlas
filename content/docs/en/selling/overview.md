In Atlas, learners pay **your academy** directly. Atlas shows them your payment details at checkout and keeps track of each order and payment, but the money goes straight from the learner to your bank account, wallet or InstaPay address.

![The Payment methods page with the "Learners pay your academy directly" note highlighted](shot:selling/how-it-works)

## The steps of a sale

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Order | Learner | On your academy website, the learner opens a paid course and starts the purchase. |
| 2. Choose a method | Learner | Checkout lists the payment methods you turned on: bank transfer, e-wallet or InstaPay. |
| 3. Pay outside Atlas | Learner | The learner sends the exact amount from their own bank, wallet or InstaPay app, using the details you entered. |
| 4. Upload proof | Learner | The learner uploads a screenshot or receipt (PNG, JPG or PDF) and can add the transfer reference and a note. |
| 5. Review | You | You get an email and a notification. You check your own account, then approve or reject the payment. |
| 6. Access | Atlas | When you approve, the learner is enrolled in the course and emailed. When you reject, the learner is emailed your reason and can send a new payment. |

Course prices are in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

## Who can do what

Only the **organization owner** can set up payment methods, review payments and see course orders. Managers and instructors don't see these pages; if they open one, Atlas tells them that only the organization owner can use it.

## Payment methods you can offer

Each academy has its own payment methods, and you can turn each one on or off:

- **Bank transfer**: your bank name, account holder name and account number (IBAN and SWIFT/BIC are optional).
- **E-wallet**: a Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash or WE Pay number, or another wallet you name.
- **InstaPay**: your InstaPay address, like name@instapay.

You can have one method of each type per academy. See [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).

## Does Atlas take a share of my sales?

No. With these payment methods the money never passes through Atlas, so Atlas takes no commission on it. Your Atlas subscription is paid separately (see [Your Atlas subscription](category:billing)).

## Where to see your sales

- **Course orders** lists every purchase in an academy with its payment and refund status. See [Track course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).
- On your dashboard, the **Revenue** card shows the payments you approved over the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Only the organization owner sees it.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas does not check that a transfer arrived. It doesn't connect to your bank, wallet or InstaPay account. You confirm each payment yourself in your own app before you approve it.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't take card payments and has no coupon codes. Refunds for these payments happen between you and the learner, outside Atlas. See [Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005).
