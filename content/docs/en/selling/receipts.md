Each step of a course purchase sends a notification in Atlas, and the important ones also send an email.

## Emails and notifications by step

| Step | Who receives it | Notification | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| The order is created | Learner | Yes | No |
| The learner submits a payment proof | Learner (confirmation that the proof arrived) | Yes | Yes |
| The learner submits a payment proof | Organization owner (a payment to review) | Yes | Yes |
| The payment is approved | Learner | Yes | Yes |
| The payment is rejected, with the academy's reason | Learner | Yes | Yes |
| The order expires before payment | Learner | Yes | No |

The owner's email links straight to the payment on the **Payments** page. The learner's links open their **Purchases** or **Payments** page on the academy website.

## Can these emails be turned off?

No. Purchase and payment emails belong to **Receipts and orders**, which can't be turned off in notification settings, so learners always get confirmation of what happened to their money. See [Email preferences and unsubscribing](doc:DOC-MSG-002).

## Is there a PDF receipt or invoice?

No. Atlas doesn't create a downloadable receipt or invoice for course purchases. The proof-submitted and payment-approved emails are the learner's record. A learner can also see their orders on **Purchases** and every payment, with its status, on **Payments**.

> [!LIMITATION]
> These emails confirm what happened in Atlas. They don't confirm that money reached the academy's account: the academy checks that itself before approving.

> [!NOTE]
> If an email doesn't arrive, check the spam folder first. See [I didn't get an email](doc:DOC-TRB-009).
