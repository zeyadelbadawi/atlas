When a learner pays one of your academy's payment methods and uploads a proof, the payment waits for you on the **Payments** page. Only the **organization owner** can review payments. Atlas never reviews these payments for you.

## How you hear about a new payment

Atlas emails you and adds a notification each time a learner submits a proof. The link opens that payment directly on the **Payments** page.

## Review a payment

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Payments**.

   ![The Payments page with Payments highlighted in the sidebar](shot:selling/payments-nav)

2. Stay on the **Waiting for review** tab. Use the search box, the **Method** filter and **Sort** to find a payment. The other tabs are **Approved**, **Rejected** and **All**.

   ![The Waiting for review tab highlighted above the list of payments](shot:selling/payments-pending)

3. Choose a payment to open it. Look at the **Proof of payment**, the **Reference** and the **Learner's note**.

   ![The payment panel showing the learner's proof of payment](shot:selling/payment-sheet)

4. Open your own bank, wallet or InstaPay app and check that the money arrived. Compare the amount and reference with **Details the learner was shown**.

   ![The payment panel with Details the learner was shown and the approval hint highlighted](shot:selling/payment-decision-hint)

5. If the money arrived, choose **Approve**, then confirm with **Approve payment**.

   ![The Approve this payment? confirmation with Approve payment highlighted](shot:selling/payment-approve-confirm)

6. If it didn't, choose **Reject…** and, if you like, write a **Reason (optional)**. The learner sees the reason and receives it by email.

   ![The reject section with a reason typed in the reason box](shot:selling/payment-reject-reason)

7. Choose **Reject payment**, then confirm with **Reject payment** in the confirmation.

   ![The Reject this payment? confirmation with Reject payment highlighted](shot:selling/payment-reject-confirm)

## What happens next

- **Approved:** the learner is enrolled in the course straight away and receives a "payment approved" email and notification. The order shows as **Paid** in [Course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).
- **Rejected:** the learner receives an email with your reason and can send a new payment for the same order. The order is reopened for 72 hours so they can pay again.

## Good to know

- Each payment can be decided only once. If you open a payment that was already reviewed, Atlas shows "This payment was already reviewed. Its current status is shown."
- A learner can have only one payment waiting for review on an order, so they can't pay twice by mistake.
- The panel's **Review history** shows who approved or rejected a payment and when.
- Proof files are private: only the learner who uploaded a proof and the organization owner can open it.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas does not check your bank statement or wallet. Approving is your confirmation that the money arrived. Approve only after you see it in your own account.

> [!NOTE]
> If you need to give money back after approving, do it outside Atlas and then end the learner's access. See [Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005).
