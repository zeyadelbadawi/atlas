Use a link lesson when the material lives on another site: a video on Vimeo, a folder on Google Drive, an article or a form. Only YouTube links play inside the lesson; every other link opens outside the academy.

## Add the lesson

1. In the Course Builder, find the unit, choose **Add content**, then **Add Lesson**.

   ![The Add content menu with Add Lesson highlighted](shot:courses/add-content-menu)

2. Enter the **Lesson Title**, open **Content Type** and choose **File** for a document, or **Video** for a video on another site.

   ![The Content Type list with File highlighted](shot:courses/lesson-type-file)

3. Paste the full web address, starting with https://, into **Content URL**.

   ![The Add Lesson dialog with File selected and a link in the content URL field](shot:courses/lesson-file-link)

4. Open **Availability**, choose **Published** when the lesson is ready, then choose **Save Lesson**.

   ![The Availability list with Published highlighted](shot:courses/lesson-availability)

5. Check the lesson as a learner. It shows **This content is hosted somewhere else** with an **Open in a new tab** button.

   ![A learner's link lesson with the Open in a new tab button](shot:courses/learner-link-lesson)

## What learners see

The lesson page explains that the academy links to the content rather than hosting it, and that opening it takes the learner out of the course. Learners finish the lesson themselves with **Mark complete**.

> [!LIMITATION]
> A link lesson is not protected. There is no watermark, no device limit and no access check on the other site, and anyone who has the link can open it. Use the other site's own sharing settings if you need to restrict it.

## Files you want to keep private

For a PDF or image that only enrolled learners should open, owners and managers can upload it to the lesson instead of pasting a link: choose **File**, then **Upload file** under **Lesson file**. The learner gets a personal link that stops working shortly after it is issued. See [Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005).
