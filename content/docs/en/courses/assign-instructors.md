Instructors only work on the courses they are assigned to. Owners and managers assign them per course.

## Before you start

The person must already be an active instructor in this academy. The organization owner adds managers and instructors to the academy; see [Team and learners](category:team). If the academy has no instructors yet, the **Instructors** card says so.

## Assign an instructor

1. Open **Courses**, open the course's **Actions** menu and choose **Edit**.

   ![The course Actions menu with Edit highlighted](shot:courses/list-actions-edit)

2. Scroll to the **Instructors** card and tick the instructor's name. **Instructor assigned to this course** confirms it.

   ![The Instructors card with the academy's instructors and their checkboxes](shot:courses/instructors-card)

To remove an instructor, untick their name. **Instructor removed from this course** confirms it. You can assign more than one instructor to a course.

## What an assigned instructor can and can't do

| An assigned instructor can | An assigned instructor can't |
|---|---|
| Open the course from the **Teaching Dashboard** | Create courses or open the **Courses** list |
| Build the curriculum: sections, lessons and their order | Edit course details, visibility or price |
| Create and edit quizzes and assignments | Publish or unpublish the course |
| Review quiz attempts, grade essays and assignments | Change the completion rule or certificates |
| Give a learner a quiz exception | Upload lesson files or design the certificate template |
| Moderate the course's reviews and see its students' progress | Approve payments |

## What happens next

The instructor sees the course under **My Courses** and in the **Teaching Dashboard**. See [Your teaching dashboard](doc:DOC-LRN-024).

> [!NOTE]
> If you get "This person isn't an active instructor in this academy", check the person's role and status on the academy's **Members** page.
