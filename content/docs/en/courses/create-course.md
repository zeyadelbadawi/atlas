Owners and managers create courses with a guided wizard. Instructors can't create courses: an owner or manager creates the course and then [assigns them to it](doc:DOC-LRN-012).

## Before you start

- You need the owner or manager role in the academy.
- Your plan sets how many courses your organization can have. If you have reached it, the wizard shows **Course limit reached**. Compare plans on the [pricing page](site:/pricing).
- Have a course image ready. You pick it from the academy's media library in the **Media** step.

## Create the course

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Courses** and choose **Create Course**.

   ![The Courses page with the Create Course button highlighted](shot:courses/create-button)

2. On **Basics**, enter the **Course Title** (up to 150 characters). The **Course Slug**, the course's web address, is suggested from the title; it uses lowercase Latin letters, numbers and hyphens, and must be unique in this academy. Add an optional **Short Description** for the course card, then choose **Create Course**. The course is now saved as a private draft that only your staff can see.

   ![The Basics step with the course title, slug and short description fields](shot:courses/wizard-basics)

3. On **Details**, write the **Description**, then choose the **Level** and **Language**, and list **What learners will achieve** and the **Requirements**, one item per line. Choose **Save and continue**.

   ![The Details step with description, level, language, outcomes and requirements](shot:courses/wizard-details)

4. On **Media**, choose **Choose image** (or **Change image**) and pick a picture from the media library, or upload a new one there. PNG or JPEG works best. Choose **Save and continue**.

   ![The Media step showing the course image and the Change image button](shot:courses/wizard-media)

5. On **Curriculum**, choose **Add Section** to create a unit, then add lessons to it. Every change here is saved as you make it. Learners only see items whose status is **Published**. Choose **Continue**. For details, see [Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005).

   ![The Curriculum step with a unit and the Add Section button](shot:courses/wizard-curriculum)

6. On **Assessments**, optionally choose **Create quiz**, write it and pick the unit in **Place in unit**. A quiz created here starts as **Published**. Assignments are created on their own page: use **Manage assignments**. Choose **Continue**.

   ![The Assessments step with the Create quiz button](shot:courses/wizard-assessments)

7. On **Pricing**, choose the **Visibility** (**Public** or **Private**) and the **Pricing** (**Free** or **Paid**). For a paid course, enter a **Price** above zero. Choose **Save and continue**. See [Set a course price](doc:DOC-LRN-003).

   ![The Pricing and access step with visibility, pricing, price and currency](shot:courses/wizard-pricing)

8. On **Review**, check the lists. Fix everything under **Required before publishing**; items under **Recommended** and **Good to know** are optional. Each item has a **Go to** button that opens the right step. Choose **Continue**.

   ![The Review step with required, recommended and good-to-know checks](shot:courses/wizard-review)

9. On **Publish**, choose **Publish course**, then **Publish** to confirm. The button stays inactive until every required item is done.

   ![The Publish step with the Publish course button](shot:courses/wizard-publish)

## What happens next

The course shows **This course is published**. Learners can find it according to its visibility: a public course can appear on your academy website, a private one stays out of the public catalog. You can unpublish it later from **Course Settings**. See [Publish your course](doc:DOC-LRN-011).

## Leave and continue later

Every step is saved when you continue, so you can close the wizard at any time. To come back, open **Courses**, open the course's **Actions** menu and choose **Continue setup**. It is shown for draft courses and opens the first step that is not finished yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Pressing **Create Course** twice does not create two courses. If the address is already used by another course in this academy, choose a different **Course Slug**.
