Reviews come from learners with an active enrollment in the course. Each learner can leave one review per course: a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional text.

## How reviews reach your course page

- A new review starts as **Pending** and is not shown publicly.
- When a learner edits their review, it goes back to **Pending**.
- Only **Approved** reviews appear on the course page.
- The academy's managing staff are notified when a new review is submitted.

## Moderate reviews

1. Open the course and choose the **Reviews** tab.

   ![The course editor tabs with Reviews highlighted](shot:courses/reviews-tab)

2. Use the **Status** filter to show **All**, **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected** reviews.

   ![The Reviews page with the Status filter open](shot:courses/reviews-filter)

On each review, choose what to do:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | Shows the review on the course page. |
| **Reject** | Keeps it off the course page. You can approve it later. |
| **Remove** | Deletes the review permanently, after you confirm with **Remove review**. This can't be undone. |

## Who can moderate

Owners and managers can moderate every course in the academy. An instructor can moderate the courses they are assigned to; they open the course from the **Teaching Dashboard** with **Edit curriculum** and use the **Reviews** tab.
