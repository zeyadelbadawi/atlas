The course's assigned instructors, the owner and managers can review attempts. Other instructors can't open them.

## Grade an attempt

1. Open the **Teaching Dashboard**, open the course, choose **Assessments**, then choose the quiz. (Owners and managers can also open the course's **Quizzes** tab, open the quiz's menu and choose **Review attempts**.)

   ![The Assessments page of a course listing its quizzes and assignments](shot:courses/instructor-assessments)

2. On **Quiz Results**, find the attempt. The **Grading** column shows **Pending** for attempts that wait for a manual grade. Choose the attempt to open it.

   ![The Quiz Results page listing attempts with score, status and grading](shot:courses/quiz-results)

3. Read the answers. Under **Manual grading**, enter the points for each essay question, from 0 up to the question's points, and choose **Save grades**.

   ![The attempt review with the Manual grading card and the Save grades button](shot:courses/attempt-grading)

4. To cancel an attempt, choose **Void attempt**, write the reason and confirm.

   ![The attempt review with the Void attempt button](shot:courses/attempt-void)

## What happens next

- When every manual grade is in, the attempt is finalised and the score is computed. Until then, the learner sees the attempt as awaiting grading.
- The finalised score counts towards completion and certificates according to the quiz's **Score to keep** setting.
- A voided attempt is marked invalid, loses its result, and is given back to the learner so they can start a new one. The reason is recorded, and voiding can't be undone.

> [!NOTE]
> There is no gradebook export. The only export on Quiz Results is the integrity CSV; see [Integrity report and CSV export](doc:DOC-LRN-025).

## What you see on an attempt

- **Attempt**: student, score, points, duration, start and submit times, whether it was late or submitted automatically, and who graded it.
- **Answers**: each question with the learner's answer, the correct answer and the points awarded.
- **Worth a look** and the event timeline, when integrity was on for the quiz. See [Exam integrity and full-screen exams](doc:DOC-LRN-016).
