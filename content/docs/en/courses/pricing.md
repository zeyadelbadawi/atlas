Every course is either **Free** or **Paid**. New courses start as **Free**. Owners and managers can change the price at any time; instructors can't.

## Free or paid: what learners experience

| Pricing | How a learner gets access |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Enrolls directly from the course page. |
| **Paid** | Completes a purchase first: pays with one of your academy's payment methods (bank transfer, mobile wallet or InstaPay), uploads a proof, and gets access once the academy owner approves it. |

Prices are in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

## Set the price

1. In the academy sidebar, open **Courses**, open the course's **Actions** menu and choose **Edit**.

   ![The course Actions menu with Edit highlighted](shot:courses/list-actions-edit)

2. In the **Configuration** card, open **Pricing** and choose **Free** or **Paid**.

   ![The Configuration card with the Pricing field highlighted](shot:courses/edit-pricing)

3. For a paid course, enter the **Price**. It must be greater than 0. The **Currency** is EGP.

   ![The Price and Currency fields of a paid course](shot:courses/edit-price)

4. Choose **Save Changes**.

   ![The Save Changes button at the bottom of the Edit Course page](shot:courses/edit-save)

You can also set the price in the **Pricing** step of the [course wizard](doc:DOC-LRN-002), together with the course's visibility.

## What happens next

The course list shows the new price. On the wizard's **Review** step:

- A paid course without a price above zero is listed under **Required before publishing**, so it can't be published.
- If your organization hasn't set up payment collection yet, **Recommended** shows that learners can't buy this course yet. The organization owner sets up payment methods; see [Selling and payments](category:selling).

> [!NOTE]
> Only the organization owner reviews payment proofs and approves purchases. A manager can set a price but can't approve payments.

## Give access without a purchase

Owners and managers can enroll a learner in any course, free or paid, without a purchase, for example a scholarship or a learner who paid in person. See [Team and learners](category:team).
