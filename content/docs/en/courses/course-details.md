These details shape how a course appears in your catalog and on its own page. Owners and managers can change them at any time, before or after publishing. Instructors can't edit course details.

## Public or private

| Visibility | Who can find the course |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Can appear on your academy website, where learners can discover it once it is published. |
| **Private** | Visible only to your staff. It won't appear in the public catalog. New courses start as **Private**. |

A private course is useful while you build, or for a group you enroll yourself. See [Team and learners](category:team).

## Edit the details

1. Open **Courses**, open the course's **Actions** menu and choose **Edit**. The **Details** tab opens on the **Basic Information** card, where you can also change the title, web address and descriptions.

   ![The Edit Course page with the Basic Information card](shot:courses/edit-basic-info)

2. In the **Configuration** card, choose the **Visibility**: **Public** or **Private**.

   ![The Configuration card with the Visibility field highlighted](shot:courses/edit-visibility)

3. In **Catalog details**, choose the **Level** (**Beginner**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced** or **All levels**) and the **Language** the course is taught in. In **What learners will achieve** and **Requirements**, write one item per line.

   ![The Catalog details card with level, language, outcomes and requirements](shot:courses/edit-catalog)

4. Under **Thumbnail**, choose **Upload Thumbnail** or **Change Thumbnail** and pick a PNG or JPG image smaller than 5 MB.

   ![The Thumbnail field with the Change Thumbnail button](shot:courses/edit-thumbnail)

5. Choose **Save Changes**.

   ![The Save Changes button at the bottom of the Edit Course page](shot:courses/edit-save)

## What happens next

The changes show on the course card and course page straight away. In the wizard's **Review** step, a missing description, image or outcomes list appears under **Recommended**, and a private course is noted under **Good to know**.

> [!TIP]
> In the [course wizard](doc:DOC-LRN-002), the **Media** step picks the image from your academy's media library instead of uploading it from your computer.

## Limits on these fields

- Up to 20 lines each for **What learners will achieve** and **Requirements**, 300 characters per line.
- Short description up to 200 characters; description up to 5,000 characters.
