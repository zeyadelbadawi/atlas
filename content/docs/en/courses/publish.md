A course stays a **Draft** until you publish it. Only owners and managers can publish or unpublish a course.

## What "published" means

Publishing makes the course available to learners according to its **Visibility**:

- A **Public** course can appear on your academy website, where learners can discover it and enroll.
- A **Private** course is published but stays out of the public catalog.

Inside a published course, learners still only see lessons, quizzes and assignments whose own status is **Published**.

## What a course needs before it can be published

| Check | Required? |
|---|---|
| A title and a web address | Required |
| At least one published lesson, quiz or assignment (a published lesson needs a link or file to open) | Required |
| For a paid course: a price above zero | Required |
| Short description, full description, course image, learning outcomes | Recommended |
| No items left in draft, no empty units | Recommended |
| Payment collection set up (paid courses) | Recommended |

Atlas refuses to publish a course that misses a required item, from the wizard and from Course Settings alike.

## Publish from the wizard

1. Open the course's **Review** step. Fix everything under **Required before publishing**, using the **Go to** buttons to jump to the right step. Look through **Recommended** and **Good to know**, then choose **Continue**.

   ![The Review step with required, recommended and good-to-know checks](shot:courses/wizard-review)

2. On **Publish**, choose **Publish course**, then **Publish** to confirm.

   ![The Publish step with the Publish course button](shot:courses/wizard-publish)

## Publish from Course Settings

3. Open **Courses**, open the course's **Actions** menu and choose **Settings**. In the **Publishing** card, choose **Publish Course**, then **Publish** to confirm. If something required is missing, the card lists it under **Before you can publish:** with a link to **Open the curriculum**, and the button stays inactive.

   ![The Publishing card on Course Settings with the Publish Course button](shot:courses/settings-publish)

## Unpublish a course

4. On **Course Settings**, choose **Unpublish Course**, then **Unpublish** to confirm. The course returns to draft and is no longer visible to learners according to its visibility.

   ![The Unpublish this course dialog with the Unpublish button](shot:courses/settings-unpublish-dialog)

## What happens next

The course status changes to **Published** in the course list and on **Course Settings**. If a public course doesn't show on your website, check that your website is published too: see [Website builder](category:website).

> [!TIP]
> Publish a course only when its first lessons are ready. Learners can enroll as soon as it is published, and they see every item you set to **Published** from then on.
