Owners, managers and the course's assigned instructors can create and grade assignments.

## Create an assignment

1. Open the course and choose the **Assignments** tab, then **Add Assignment**.

   ![The Assignments page of a course with the Add Assignment button](shot:courses/assignments-list)

2. Fill in the **Title**, **Description** and **Instructions**, and choose the **Status** (a **Draft** assignment is visible only to staff). Set the **Due date** and the **Late policy**, and choose whether to **Allow resubmission** and whether it is **Required for course completion**. Choose **Save**.

   ![The Add Assignment dialog with the due date and late policy](shot:courses/assignment-dialog)

3. Place it in the course: in the **Course Builder**, choose **Add content** in a unit, then **Add existing assignment**, and choose **Add**.

   ![The Add an existing assignment dialog](shot:courses/attach-assignment)

## Due dates and late work

The late policy only matters when a due date is set, and the server enforces it:

| Late policy | After the due date |
|---|---|
| **Do not accept late submissions** | The submit action is refused, and the learner is told the deadline has passed. |
| **Accept late submissions and flag them** | Submissions are accepted and marked as late for reviewers. |

With **Allow resubmission** on, learners may submit again after their first submission.

## What learners can submit

Learners write a response of up to 5,000 characters and can attach one file: a PDF or an image (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP) up to 10 MB. Word documents need to be saved as PDF first. Learners must write a response or attach a file before they can submit. See [For learners](category:for-learners).

## Grade a submission

4. Open the **Teaching Dashboard**, open the course, choose **Assessments**, then the assignment. **Submissions** lists every learner's submission with its grading state. Choose a submission. (Owners and managers can also use **Review submissions** from the assignment's menu on the **Assignments** tab.)

   ![The Submissions page listing learners' submissions](shot:courses/submissions-list)

5. On **Review Submission**, read the answer and open the attachment if there is one. Enter a **Score** from 0 to 100 and optional **Feedback**, then choose **Save Grade**.

   ![The Review Submission page with score, feedback and the Save Grade button](shot:courses/submission-grade)

## What happens next

The submission shows as **Graded**, and the learner sees the grade and your feedback. A graded assignment counts towards completion if it is required. Files learners upload are stored privately: the link you open expires after a short time, and the page shows when.
