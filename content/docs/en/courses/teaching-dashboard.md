Sign in to the Atlas dashboard with your instructor account. You only see the courses an owner or manager has assigned to you; if none are assigned yet, ask them to [assign you to a course](doc:DOC-LRN-012). Owners and managers can open these pages too.

## Find your way around

1. In the sidebar, open **Teaching Dashboard**. The cards show your **Assigned Courses**, **Active Courses**, **Total Students**, **Pending Submissions** and **Pending Grading**. **Courses Requiring Attention** lists courses with submissions awaiting grading, low student engagement or no recent activity.

   ![The Teaching Dashboard with its summary cards](shot:courses/teaching-dashboard)

2. Open **My Courses** to see every course you teach, with its status, visibility, enrolled learners and average progress. Choose a course to open it.

   ![The My Courses list of assigned courses](shot:courses/teaching-courses)

3. **Course Overview** shows enrolled students, average progress, pending submissions and pending grading, with quick links to **Students**, **Assessments** and **Announcements**. Choose **Edit curriculum** to open the Course Builder.

   ![The Course Overview with the Edit curriculum button and quick links](shot:courses/teaching-course-overview)

4. **Students** lists the learners enrolled in the course with their enrollment, progress, completion and last activity. Choose a learner to see their progress in detail.

   ![The Students list of a course with progress and completion](shot:courses/teaching-students)

5. **Assessments** lists the course's quizzes and assignments. Choose a quiz to review attempts, or an assignment to grade submissions.

   ![The Assessments page listing quizzes and assignments](shot:courses/instructor-assessments)

## What you can do from here

- Build lessons and sections: [Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005).
- Create quizzes and assignments from the **Quizzes** and **Assignments** tabs of the Course Builder: [Create a quiz](doc:DOC-LRN-014), [Assignments](doc:DOC-LRN-019).
- Grade: [Review attempts and grade essay answers](doc:DOC-LRN-017).
- Post a course announcement from **Announcements**: see [Notifications and messages](category:notifications).

## What instructors can't do

Instructors can't create courses, change course details, prices or visibility, publish courses, change completion rules or certificates, upload lesson files, or approve payments. Ask an owner or manager for these.
