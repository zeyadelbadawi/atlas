The academy owner and managers can write and publish announcements. Instructors can read them but can't post them.

## Post an academy announcement

1. In the sidebar, open **Announcements**.

   ![The Academy announcements page with its entry in the sidebar highlighted](shot:notifications/announcements-page)

2. Choose **New Announcement**.

   ![The New Announcement button](shot:notifications/announcements-new)

3. Write the **Title** and the **Message**. To publish it later, set **Schedule for**. Then select **Save**.

   ![The new announcement form with the Save button highlighted](shot:notifications/announcements-form)

4. On the saved announcement, choose **Publish**. (If you set **Schedule for**, you can skip this: Atlas publishes it within about 15 minutes after that time.)

   ![The Publish button on a draft announcement](shot:notifications/announcements-publish)

5. Confirm with **Publish** in **Publish this announcement?**.

   ![The publish confirmation dialog](shot:notifications/announcements-publish-confirm)

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, not visible to learners. You can edit or publish it |
| **Scheduled** | Saved with a date in **Schedule for**. Atlas publishes it within about 15 minutes after that time |
| **Published** | Visible to learners |
| **Archived** | Removed from learners' feeds |

To take a published announcement down, choose **Archive** and confirm. It no longer appears in learners' feeds.

## Schedule or unschedule a saved announcement

You can add, change or remove the date of a **Draft** or **Scheduled** announcement:

- Choose **Edit**, set **Schedule for**, then **Save**. A draft becomes **Scheduled** and is published automatically.
- To cancel the schedule, choose **Edit**, clear **Schedule for**, then **Save**. The announcement goes back to **Draft** and is published only when you choose **Publish**.

## Announcements for one course

To reach only the learners of one course, open the **Teaching Dashboard**, then **My Courses**, open the course and choose **Announcements**. The **Course Announcements** page works the same way: **New Announcement**, **Save**, then **Publish**. Every learner enrolled in that course sees it.

![The Course Announcements page](shot:notifications/course-announcements)

## Where learners see it

Learners see published announcements under **Announcements** on the overview of My Learn, and get an in-app notification.

![Announcements on a learner's My Learn overview](shot:notifications/learner-announcements)

> [!LIMITATION]
> Announcements are not emailed and don't appear on your public website. To email your learners, use [Messages](doc:DOC-MSG-003).
