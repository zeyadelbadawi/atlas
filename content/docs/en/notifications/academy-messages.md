The academy owner can send messages. Managers and instructors can't open **Messages**.

## Send a message

1. In the sidebar, open **Messages**.

   ![The Messages page with its entry in the sidebar highlighted](shot:notifications/messages-page)

2. Under **Recipients**, choose who gets it: **All active learners**, **Learners in selected courses** (then tick the courses) or **Academy staff** (then tick the roles).

   ![The recipient choices](shot:notifications/messages-recipients)

3. Under **Send as**, tick **Email**, **In-app notification**, or both.

   ![The Send as options](shot:notifications/messages-channels)

4. Write the **Subject** (up to 150 characters) and the **Message**. Choose the language you wrote it in under **Written in**.

   ![The Subject field filled in](shot:notifications/messages-write)

5. Choose **Preview recipients**. **Who will receive this** shows how many people it reaches, how many emails will be queued, and who is left out.

   ![The preview of who will receive the message](shot:notifications/messages-preview)

6. Choose **Review and send**, check the numbers in **Send this message?**, then select **Send now**.

   ![The Send this message confirmation](shot:notifications/messages-confirm)

The message appears under **Sent messages**, with its delivery progress.

## Your monthly email allowance

Each academy can send a limited number of emails per month from **Messages**. The **Monthly email allowance** meter shows how many you have used and when it resets.

![The Monthly email allowance meter](shot:notifications/messages-quota)

- Each email queued counts once. In-app notifications don't count.
- Emails that fail before reaching the email provider are given back.
- If a message needs more emails than you have left, nothing is sent: send it as an in-app notification only, or choose fewer recipients. A message is never sent partially.

## Who is left out, and why

The preview tells you who won't get an email:

- People who turned these emails off in their preferences (they still get the in-app notification if it is on).
- Addresses that can't receive email because messages bounced or were reported as spam.
- Blocked learners, and learners waiting for approval.

## Good to know

- **Preview again if things changed.** If the number of recipients changed since your preview, nothing is sent. Review the new numbers and send again.
- **Large messages** ask you to tick an extra confirmation before sending.
- **A sent message can't be recalled.**
- **Unsubscribe link.** Every email includes a link that lets the person turn these emails off. See [Email preferences and unsubscribing](doc:DOC-MSG-002).

> [!TIP]
> For a short notice that stays visible in My Learn, post an [announcement](doc:DOC-MSG-004) instead. Announcements don't use your email allowance.
