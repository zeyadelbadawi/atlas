Everyone with an Atlas account has email preferences: team members in the dashboard, and learners in My Learn on their academy's site. The preferences belong to you, not to an academy.

## Change your email preferences

1. In the dashboard, open the account menu at the top, choose **Profile**, then the **Preferences** tab.

   ![The Profile page with the Preferences tab highlighted](shot:notifications/profile-preferences-tab)

2. Under **Email preferences**, choose your **Email language**: the language your emails are written in.

   ![The Email language setting](shot:notifications/email-language)

3. For **Academy updates**, use the switch to turn these emails on or off, and choose how they arrive: **As it happens**, **Daily digest** or **Off**.

   ![The Academy updates switch highlighted](shot:notifications/academy-updates)

4. Team members also have **Operations** (support tickets, website messages and academy health). Turn it on or off and choose **As it happens** or **Daily digest**.

   ![The Operations switch highlighted](shot:notifications/operations)

5. Under **Account and courses**, use **Reminders** to turn reminders about your trial and subscription renewal on or off.

   ![The Reminders switch highlighted](shot:notifications/reminders)

Every change is saved straight away, and you see "Email preferences saved".

## If you're a learner

On your academy's site, open **Profile** in My Learn, then the **Preferences** tab. You'll find the same **Email preferences**. Learners don't have the **Operations** category.

![The Email preferences in a learner's profile](shot:notifications/learner-preferences)

## The categories

| Category | What it covers | Can you turn it off? |
|---|---|---|
| **Security** | Sign-ins from new devices, password changes, two-step verification | No, always on |
| **Receipts and orders** | Purchases, payments and refunds | No, always on |
| **Account and courses** | Enrollments, grades, certificates, academy setup | No, always on (except **Reminders**) |
| **Academy updates** | Messages from your academy, grades and results, course completion | Yes |
| **Operations** (team only) | Support tickets, website messages, academy health | Yes |

Turning an email off doesn't stop the matching in-app notification: you still see it in Atlas.

## Unsubscribe from a message

Emails sent from an academy's **Messages** page, and messages from the Atlas team, include an unsubscribe link.

- Select the link. A page opens and asks you to confirm; nothing changes until you select **Unsubscribe · إلغاء الاشتراك**.
- This turns off that kind of email only, for you only. It doesn't delete your account and never stops security emails.
- You still see those messages as notifications in Atlas.
- To turn the emails back on, use your **Preferences** tab as above.

If the page says the link is invalid or has expired, change the setting from your **Preferences** tab instead.
