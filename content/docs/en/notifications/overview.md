Atlas has two channels: **in-app notifications** and **email**. There are no SMS, WhatsApp or push notifications.

## Where you see notifications

**In the dashboard (owners, managers and instructors).** Select the bell in the top bar to see your latest unread notifications, then **View All** to open the **Notifications** page. You can also open it from the account menu. Filter by **All** or **Unread**, open a notification, or choose **Mark all as read**.

![The notification bell open in the dashboard](shot:notifications/dashboard-bell)

![The Notifications page in the dashboard](shot:notifications/dashboard-page)

**On your academy's site (learners).** Learners open **Notifications** in My Learn, or the bell at the top. A small badge shows how many are unread.

![The Notifications page in My Learn](shot:notifications/learner-page)

**Each notification has its place.** Notifications about running an academy (a payment proof to review, a new website message) appear in the dashboard. Notifications about your own learning (a grade, a certificate) appear in that academy's My Learn. If you teach in one academy and learn in another, you see each set in its own place. A few account notices, such as a password change, appear in both.

## What you get notified about

| You are | Examples |
|---|---|
| Learner | Enrollments, payment approved or rejected, grades, certificates, announcements, messages from your academy |
| Owner or manager | Payment proofs to review, learners waiting for approval, new website messages, setup finished or failed, plan limits, replies to your support tickets and service requests |
| Everyone | Security events such as a password change, two-step verification turned on or off, or being added to an academy |

The full list is in [Emails Atlas sends](doc:DOC-REF-005).

## Emails you can't turn off

Security alerts, receipts and account changes are always emailed. You need to know when they happen. Everything else follows your [email preferences](doc:DOC-MSG-002), where you can turn emails off or choose a daily digest.

## Daily digests and limits on email volume

- **Daily digest.** For some categories you can choose one daily email instead of one email per event. Digests are sent according to the academy's time zone.
- **Volume limit.** To avoid flooding your inbox, Atlas limits how many non-essential emails one person gets in a day. Extra ones are moved into the next daily digest. Security and purchase emails are never held back.

## Announcements are not emailed

Academy and course announcements appear only as notifications, in My Learn. They are not sent by email, even though the academy settings show an "Announcements by email" option: that card is read-only. To email your learners, use [Messages](doc:DOC-MSG-003).

## Academy email settings

In **Academy Settings**, in the **Emails to students** section, the **Email & sign-in codes** card shows how your academy uses email, such as when learners are asked for a code at sign-in. These settings follow Atlas's configuration and can't be changed per academy yet.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas does not send SMS, WhatsApp or push notifications. To reach learners outside Atlas, use the email channel in [Messages](doc:DOC-MSG-003).
