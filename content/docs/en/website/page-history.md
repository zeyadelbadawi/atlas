The academy owner and managers can see a page's history and restore a version. Restoring replaces your **draft** only: visitors keep seeing the live page until you publish.

## Restore an earlier version

1. Open the page in the visual editor and choose **History** at the top. (The classic editor has the same **History** button.)

   ![The History button at the top of the visual editor](shot:website/history-button)

2. **Page history** lists the kept versions, newest first, with who made each one and when.

   ![The Page history dialog listing a Published version and an Auto-saved draft](shot:website/history-list)

3. Choose a version to preview it. You can check it on **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** and in each language. When it's the one you want, choose **Restore to draft**.

   ![A version previewed in Page history, with the Restore to draft button highlighted](shot:website/history-version)

4. Read what will happen in **Restore this version to the draft?**, then choose **Restore to draft** to confirm. Atlas tells you when it's done.

   ![The Restore this version to the draft? dialog](shot:website/history-confirm)

5. Check the page, then publish it when you're ready for visitors to see it.

   ![The Publish button at the top of the visual editor](shot:website/ve-publish)

## What Atlas keeps

| Version | When it's kept |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Every time the page is published, on its own or with the whole site |
| **Auto-saved draft** | About once per editing session, as you save |
| **Before restore** | Your draft just before you restored another version |

Atlas keeps the 30 most recent versions of each page. Older ones are removed.

## What a restore changes

- It replaces the page's whole draft: its title, address, visibility, search-engine text and sections.
- Your draft from just before is kept as **Before restore**, so you can switch back.
- Unsaved changes are saved first, so they are part of the draft that is kept. If they can't be saved (a section needs attention), Atlas tells you they will be discarded.
- **Undo** in the visual editor starts again from the restored version.

## If a restore is refused

- **Someone else saved the page since you opened it**: nothing is restored. The page and the list refresh; check the version and restore it again.
- **The version's address now belongs to another page**, or is now reserved: nothing is changed.
- **Parts of the version no longer pass today's checks** (for example a text over a newer limit): nothing is changed. Atlas lists those parts; you can still look at the version and copy what you need by hand.
- **That version is no longer kept**: the list refreshes.
