The visual editor is the main way to edit your website. It shows a page as visitors will see it, and you edit it right there. The academy owner and managers can use it; instructors don't see the website screens.

The screen has three parts: **Sections** (the page's sections, in order) on one side, your page in the middle, and the settings of what you selected on the other side.

## Edit a page

1. Open **Website** in the sidebar and choose **Edit website**. Your home page opens in the visual editor. To edit another page, choose it from the page list at the top of the editor, or open it from the **Pages** tab.

   ![The Website overview with the Edit website button highlighted](shot:website/overview-edit)

2. Look at the editor: the **Sections** list, your page, and a tip that says **Click a section on your page, or here, to edit it.**

   ![The visual editor with the Sections list, the page and the empty settings panel](shot:website/ve-editor)

3. Click a section on the page, or its name in **Sections**. It's outlined on the page and its settings open in the side panel, on the **Content** tab. Every change you make shows on the page as you type.

   ![The Hero section selected in the Sections list, with its settings open on the Content tab](shot:website/ve-select-section)

4. To change a text, edit its field in the panel, or double-click the text on the page and type there. Press Enter to keep the change and Esc to cancel. In a text with several lines, Shift+Enter starts a new line.

   ![The tip about double-clicking text at the top of the Content tab](shot:website/ve-inline-hint)

5. To add a section, choose **Add section** (or the **+** between two sections in the list) and pick one from the library. Many sections then offer ready-made looks: see [Section looks and page templates](doc:DOC-WEB-016).

   ![The Add a section library with its search box and section groups](shot:website/ve-library)

6. Check the page on other screens with **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile**, and switch the **Preview language** to check your other language.

   ![The Mobile button and the preview language list above the page](shot:website/ve-devices)

7. Your changes save to your draft by themselves a moment after you stop. The bar at the top says where things stand, for example **All changes saved**. Use **Undo** and **Redo** to step back and forward.

   ![The Undo and Redo buttons and the All changes saved status](shot:website/ve-undo-status)

8. To change things that apply to every page, such as the theme, the menu, the header and the footer, choose **Site**. The panel is described below.

   ![The Site panel open beside the page, with the theme list](shot:website/ve-site-panel)

9. When the page is ready, choose **Publish**. Atlas first checks the page and lists anything worth a look; then the page goes live. Other pages and the site settings stay as they are published.

   ![The Publish button at the top of the visual editor](shot:website/ve-publish)

## Type text on the page

Double-click a text in the **selected** section, or move to it with Tab and press Enter, to type on the page itself. A badge at the top reads **Editing text · Enter saves · Esc cancels**.

- **Enter** keeps your change, **Esc** puts the text back as it was, and clicking elsewhere also keeps the change.
- The field in the panel follows what you type, with its character count. A text stops at its limit.
- You edit the language you are previewing. To type the Arabic text, switch **Preview language** to Arabic first.
- Some texts can't be edited in place, for example a text that is still empty in the other language and shows the main-language text. Atlas then opens the right field in the panel instead.
- The whole edit counts as one step for **Undo**.

## Work with sections

- In **Sections**, drag a section by its handle to move it, or use its menu (**More actions**): **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate**, **Hide on the page** and **Delete**. The same buttons are at the top of the settings panel. Atlas asks before it deletes a section.
- Under **Display** on the **Content** tab, the **Shown** switch hides a section without deleting it, and **Show on** chooses the screen sizes it appears on.
- The **Design** tab sets the section's background, spacing, alignment and width. See [Section design options](doc:DOC-WEB-018).
- A section marked **Needs attention** has a field to fix. Atlas doesn't save the page until it's fixed, and says so in the status bar.

## Saving, undo and leaving

- The visual editor saves your draft by itself, a moment after the last change, once every section is valid. Saving never publishes.
- **Undo** and **Redo** also work with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y). On a Mac, use Cmd.
- If a save fails, the status bar shows **Couldn’t save your changes** with **Try again**.
- To go back to an earlier version of the page, use **History**: see [Page history: go back to an earlier version](doc:DOC-WEB-019).

## The Site panel

**Site** opens the settings that apply to every page, beside your page, so you see each change on it:

- **Theme**: switch between the four themes. See [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002).
- **Logo and colours**: a link to **Visual Identity**, where your logo and colours are set.
- **Menu, header and footer**: the same settings as in Website settings. See [Navigation, header, footer and social links](doc:DOC-WEB-009).
- **Search engines**: how the current page appears in Google and when shared. See [SEO settings for your academy site](doc:DOC-WEB-012).
- The publish bar for the whole site. Theme, menu, header and footer changes reach visitors when you publish the website; the editor's **Publish** button publishes only the current page.

## When someone else is editing

If a colleague has the same page open, a banner at the top names them. If they saved first, Atlas offers to reload their version or keep your changes on top of it. See [Website save conflict](doc:DOC-TRB-013).

> [!NOTE]
> The visual editor opens every page except **Course Details**, which opens in the classic editor. The classic editor is still available for every page from the page's **More actions** menu on the **Pages** tab: see [Edit a page in the classic editor](doc:DOC-WEB-004).

> [!TIP]
> Images you upload are turned into smaller versions automatically, so your pages load faster on phones. You don't need to resize them yourself.
