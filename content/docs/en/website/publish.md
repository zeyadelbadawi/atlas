The academy owner and managers can preview and publish the site.

## Draft and live: two copies of your site

- Everything you save, pages and site settings, goes into your **working copy**.
- Visitors see the **published copy**. It changes only when you publish.
- Saved changes that aren't published can't be reached by visitors, even by guessing an address.

The publish bar at the top of the **Website** overview, **Settings** and the page editor shows the status (**Draft** or **Published**) and what's waiting:

- **Your website is not published yet. Visitors see a Coming Soon page.**
- **This website is live and shows everything you have saved.**
- A line such as **Site settings and 2 pages have unpublished changes.** when you have saved changes to publish.

## Preview and publish

1. Open **Website**, then the **Preview** tab, and choose a page from the page list.

   ![The Preview tab with the page list highlighted](shot:website/preview)

2. Choose **Desktop**, **Tablet** or **Mobile** to see the page at that width. Use the language list to check the other language.

   ![The Preview tab showing the draft site at Mobile width](shot:website/preview-mobile)

3. Go back to the **Website** overview and read the publish bar to see what is waiting to be published.

   ![The publish bar showing the site status and what is waiting](shot:website/publish-bar)

4. Choose **Publish changes** and confirm in **Publish your changes?**. The first time, the button is **Publish** and the dialog is **Publish this website?**. Every saved page and your site settings become live for every visitor.

   ![The publish bar on the Website overview with Publish changes highlighted](shot:website/overview-publish)

5. To take the site offline, choose **Unpublish** and confirm in **Take the website offline?**. Visitors see a Coming soon page until you publish again.

   ![The Take the website offline? dialog with the Unpublish button](shot:website/unpublish-dialog)

6. Open your site in a private browser window to check it as a visitor sees it.

   ![The live academy website as a visitor sees it](shot:website/public-home)

## Publish only one page

While your site is live, the page editor has **Publish page**. It saves your changes to that page and makes only that page live; other pages and the site settings stay as they are published. See [Edit a page with sections](doc:DOC-WEB-004).

## What goes live without publishing

- Your logo, favicon and colours from **Visual Identity** go live as soon as you save them.
- Deleting a custom page removes it from the live site at once.
- Edits to published entries in your content library (questions and quotes) show on live pages that already use them.

## Sample quotes don't block publishing

If your starter site still has sample quotes, Atlas lists them before publishing and lets you choose **Publish anyway**. Samples never appear on your live site. See [Replace sample content before you launch](doc:DOC-WEB-007).

## Why visitors might still see Coming soon

- The site isn't published yet, or it was unpublished.
- Your subscription no longer allows the site to be shown. Visitors see Coming soon even though the site is published, and they aren't told why. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

Step-by-step checks are in [My website shows "Coming soon"](doc:DOC-TRB-005). The page itself is described in [The Coming Soon page](doc:DOC-ACD-009).
