The academy owner and managers can change the theme. A theme sets the fonts, layout and style of every page. Your sections, text and images stay the same when you switch.

## The four themes

| Theme | How Atlas describes it |
|---|---|
| **Modern Education** | Friendly and approachable, with a split hero and elevated cards. This is the default theme. |
| **Atelier** | An editorial, art-directed site with a serif display face, a warm paper canvas and numbered chapters. |
| **Manara** | Bold and kinetic, built for teacher-led academies with big audiences: a dark stage and brand-coloured blocks. |
| **Riwaq** | Measured and clear, built for professional institutes and career academies, with a visible column grid. |

Each theme uses your brand colours and logo from **Visual Identity**.

## Change your theme

1. In the sidebar, open **Website**, then the **Settings** tab. The **Theme** tab opens first.

   ![The website settings with the Theme tab selected](shot:website/theme-tab)

2. Look through the theme cards. Each card shows a preview, the theme's name and a short description. The theme your site uses now is marked **Current theme**.

   ![The theme cards, with the current theme's button marked Current theme](shot:website/theme-current)

3. On the theme you want, choose **Select theme**. Atlas saves the new theme to your draft right away.

   ![The Select theme button on another theme's card](shot:website/theme-select)

4. Open the **Preview** tab and check your pages at **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** widths.

   ![The Preview tab showing the draft site at Mobile width](shot:website/preview-mobile)

5. When you're happy with it, choose **Publish changes** in the publish bar and confirm.

   ![The publish bar on the Settings page with Publish changes highlighted](shot:website/settings-publish)

## What changes and what stays

- Switching changes the **draft only**. Your live site keeps its current theme until you publish.
- Your sections, text and images are kept. Atlas doesn't rewrite or translate your content when you switch.
- Some details are drawn differently by each theme, so a section can look different after a switch. Check every page in **Preview** before you publish.
- The starter content was written once, when your academy was created. A later switch doesn't add new starter content.

> [!NOTE]
> If your site still uses an older theme that is no longer offered, the Theme tab tells you so. Moving to one of the four current themes can't be undone, because the older theme can't be chosen again. Atlas asks you to confirm with **Switch theme** first.

> [!TIP]
> The owner can also ask the Atlas team for a theme designed around your brand from the card below the themes. See [Request a service from the Atlas team](doc:DOC-SUP-002).
