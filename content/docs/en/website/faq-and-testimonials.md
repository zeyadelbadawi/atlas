The academy owner and managers can manage the content library. A library entry is written once and can appear in sections on several pages; edit it in one place and every page that uses it shows the change.

## Create entries

1. Open **Website**, then the **Content** tab. The **FAQs** tab opens first.

   ![The Content tab with the FAQs tab selected](shot:website/content-faqs)

2. Choose **New FAQ entry**.

   ![The New FAQ entry button](shot:website/content-faq-new)

3. Fill **Question (English)**, **Question (Arabic)**, **Answer (English)** and **Answer (Arabic)**, then choose **Save changes**. Both languages are required. The entry is saved as **Draft**.

   ![The Create FAQ entry dialog with a demo question in English and Arabic](shot:website/content-faq-dialog)

4. For a testimonial, open the **Testimonials** tab and choose **New testimonial**.

   ![The Testimonials tab with the New testimonial button](shot:website/content-testimonials)

5. Fill **Quote (English)** and **Quote (Arabic)**, the **Author name**, and optionally **Author role (English)**, **Author role (Arabic)** and an **Avatar**, then choose **Save changes**.

   ![The Create testimonial dialog with its empty fields](shot:website/content-testimonial-dialog)

6. To show entries on a page, open the page and its **FAQ** or **Testimonials** section (in the visual editor, select the section; in the classic editor, open it), and add entries under **From your content library**. Set their order there. The visual editor saves by itself; in the classic editor, choose **Apply changes** and **Save draft**.

   ![The FAQ section dialog with the From your content library area](shot:website/editor-faq-library)

7. Choose **Publish** in the visual editor or **Publish page** in the classic editor (or publish the whole site) so the section shows your entries.

   ![The Publish page button next to Save draft](shot:website/editor-publish-page)

## Publish, hide, order and archive entries

Each entry in the list has these controls:

- **Publish**: makes a draft entry available to your site. Only **Published** entries appear.
- **Visible**: a switch that hides a published entry from your site without unpublishing it.
- **Move up** / **Move down**: set the order of the list.
- **Archive**: removes the entry from every page that uses it, after you confirm in **Archive this entry?**. The entry isn't deleted and stays in your list as **Archived**, but archiving can't be undone: an archived entry can't be published again.

To edit an entry, choose its text in the list. Edits to a published entry show on your live pages without publishing those pages again.

> [!WARNING]
> Archiving is final. You can still edit an archived entry's text, but it stays archived and off your site. Archive only entries you no longer need; to take one off your site for a while, switch off **Visible** instead. To show archived content again, create a new entry.

## Library entries and section items

FAQ and testimonials sections can hold both library entries and items typed directly into the section. Library entries show first, and a section can show up to 12 of them. In the section, an entry that won't show is marked: **Not published — won't show.**, **Hidden — won't show.** or **Archived — won't show.**

## Sample testimonials in your starter site

Your starter site comes with sample testimonials typed into its testimonials section. They are marked **Sample** in the section dialog and never appear on your live site. For each one, either replace the quote, name and role with a real testimonial and tick **This is a real testimonial**, or choose **Remove**. Ticking the box is your confirmation that the testimonial is real and that you may publish it.

> [!WARNING]
> Never publish invented quotes or names. Add only testimonials from real learners who agreed to be quoted.

## Testimonials and course reviews

Testimonials here are quotes you enter. They are separate from **course reviews**, which learners write on a course page and you approve. See [Course reviews and moderation](doc:DOC-LRN-023).

The third tab on the **Content** page, **Announcements**, opens your announcements. See [Post announcements](doc:DOC-MSG-004).
