Your academy's public website is where visitors find your courses, read about you and create their learner account. You manage it from **Website** in the **Academy** section of the dashboard sidebar.

![The Website overview with the Edit website button, the publish bar and the launch checklist](shot:website/overview)

The website screens sit on tabs at the top: **Website** (the overview), **Pages**, **Content**, **Settings** and **Messages**. On the overview:

- **Edit website** opens your home page in the visual editor, the main way to edit your site. See [Edit your website in the visual editor](doc:DOC-WEB-015).
- **Preview draft** shows your saved draft, and **Visit website** opens your live site.
- The publish bar shows whether your site is live and what is waiting to be published.

## Where to change what

The **Where to change what** card on the overview gives each part of your website one place to edit it:

![The Where to change what card on the Website overview](shot:website/overview-where)

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Page content and sections | The visual editor. The classic editor is in each page's **More actions** menu under **Pages**. |
| Theme, menu, header and footer | The visual editor's **Site** panel, beside your page. Also under **Settings**. |
| Logo and colours | **Visual Identity**, once for your whole academy |
| Search engines, language and domain | **Settings** |
| Reusable questions and quotes | **Content** |
| Messages from visitors | **Messages** |

## What you get when your academy is created

- **Six core pages**: Home, About, Courses, FAQs, Contact and the course details page that shows each course. Core pages can't be deleted. You can hide any of them except the course details page.
- **Starter content** written for the theme you chose, using your academy's name and description. Rewrite it before you launch: see [Replace sample content before you launch](doc:DOC-WEB-007).
- **A theme** that sets the look of every page.
- A **Launch checklist** on the overview: confirm your brand colours, replace the sample quotes, and publish.

## Pages, sections and themes

- A **page** has an address on your site, such as `/about`. You can add your own custom pages next to the core ones. See [Manage your website pages](doc:DOC-WEB-003).
- A page is a stack of **sections**: a hero banner, featured courses, a class schedule, pricing plans, a countdown, a map, questions and answers, a contact form and more. Many sections come in ready-made looks, and a new page can start from a template. The custom section lets you lay out your own columns. There is no HTML or custom code. See the [Section types reference](doc:DOC-WEB-005), [Section looks and page templates](doc:DOC-WEB-016) and [Build your own layout with the custom section](doc:DOC-WEB-017).
- Each section has design options, such as its background and spacing, and phones can have their own spacing. See [Section design options](doc:DOC-WEB-018).
- The **theme** decides fonts, layout and style for the whole site. You can switch between four themes: Modern Education, Atelier, Manara and Riwaq. See [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002).
- Your logo, favicon and colours come from **Visual Identity**: see [Brand Studio: logo, colours and favicon](doc:DOC-ACD-004).

## Who can edit the website

The academy owner and managers can open every website screen, edit pages and settings, and publish. Instructors don't see **Website** in their sidebar, and Atlas refuses their requests to the website even through a direct link. The owner can also lock the header, menu and footer so nobody changes them: see [Lock the header, menu and footer](doc:DOC-WEB-021) and [Roles and permissions](doc:DOC-ROLE-005).

## Draft and live: when visitors see your changes

Atlas keeps two copies of your site: the working copy you edit and the published copy visitors see. The visual editor saves your working copy by itself; saving never publishes. Visitors see your changes when you publish the whole site, or one page with its **Publish** button. Before a page is published, Atlas checks it for things worth a look. Details are in [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008) and [Check a page before you publish it](doc:DOC-WEB-020).

Atlas also keeps earlier versions of each page, so you can go back to one: see [Page history: go back to an earlier version](doc:DOC-WEB-019).

One exception: logo, favicon and colour changes made in **Visual Identity** go live as soon as you save them.

## Arabic and English

Every text a visitor reads has a box for each language. Your site has a main language, English or Arabic, that is required; the other language is optional, and where it's empty visitors see the main-language text. The main language opens at your site's main address, and the other one lives under `/ar` or `/en`. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

## What visitors see before you publish

Until you publish, every page of your site shows a **Coming soon** page with your academy's name and logo. Visitors also see it if you unpublish the site, or if your subscription no longer allows the site to be shown. See [The Coming Soon page](doc:DOC-ACD-009) and [My website shows "Coming soon"](doc:DOC-TRB-005).

> [!LIMITATION]
> The website builder has no field for custom code, HTML, analytics tags or tracking pixels. Each section shows only the fields the editor offers.

> [!TIP]
> Images you upload are turned into smaller versions automatically, so your pages load faster on phones.
