Use the custom section when none of the ready-made sections fits what you want to say. The academy owner and managers can add it. Your theme still draws it, in its own fonts and colours.

## Add and set up a custom section

1. In the visual editor, choose **Add section**, then **Custom section** under **Build your own**. It's added to your page and its settings open.

   ![The Add a section library](shot:website/ve-library)

2. Choose a **Layout** (**One column**, **Two columns** or **Three columns**) and, with more than one column, the **Column widths** (**Equal**, **First wider** or **Second wider**). Optionally add a **Section title (optional)** and an **Introduction (optional)** above the columns.

   ![The custom section's settings with the Layout and Column widths choices](shot:website/editor-custom-section)

3. In each column, choose **Add element** and pick what to add. Use each element's menu to **Duplicate** it, move it to another column, or **Remove** it, and the arrows to change the order.

   ![The Add element menu of a column, listing the element types](shot:website/editor-custom-elements)

## The elements

| Element | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Heading** | A heading, as a **Main heading (H2)** or a **Subheading (H3)**; up to 120 characters |
| **Text** | Plain text, up to 1,500 characters; an empty line starts a new paragraph |
| **Buttons** | Up to three buttons, with the same links as other sections (a page, a course, a web address, WhatsApp, a phone call, sign up or sign in) |
| **Image** | An image from your media library, a description for screen readers and a shape: **Landscape 4:3**, **Wide 16:9**, **Square** or **Portrait 4:5** |
| **YouTube video** | A YouTube link and an optional title; the video loads only when a visitor presses play |
| **Checklist** | 1 to 10 short items, each up to 120 characters |
| **Icon with text** | An icon, a title and optional text |
| **Space** | Empty space: **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** |
| **Divider** | A thin line; nothing to fill in |

## Limits and good to know

- A column holds up to 12 elements.
- On phones the columns always stack, one under the other. To show the last column first on phones, turn on **Swap the order on phones** on the section's **Design** tab. See [Section design options](doc:DOC-WEB-018).
- If you change to fewer columns, the elements of the removed columns move into the last remaining column, so nothing is lost.
- The first column is on the left in English and on the right in Arabic.
- An image element stays hidden on your site until you add an image.
- If the section has a title, use subheadings for the headings inside it, so the page keeps one clear outline.

> [!LIMITATION]
> The custom section has no field for HTML, scripts or embed codes. Videos come from YouTube links only.
