The academy owner and managers can change how each section looks. The choices are named options, not colour pickers or numbers: your theme draws each one with your brand colours, so a later change of brand colour still updates every section.

## Change a section's design

1. In the visual editor, click the section on the page or in **Sections**, then open the **Design** tab in the settings panel.

   ![The Design tab of a selected section in the visual editor](shot:website/ve-design-tab)

2. Choose the options you want. The page shows each change straight away, and it saves to your draft like any other change. Publish the page when you're ready.

   ![The On phones spacing choice on the Design tab](shot:website/ve-design-phones)

## The options

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Background** | **Automatic**, **Light**, **Soft**, **Brand** or **Dark**. Text, links and buttons switch to colours that stay readable on it. |
| **Space above and below** | **Compact**, **Normal** or **Roomy** |
| **On phones** | Phones follow the spacing of larger screens until you choose a different one there; **Match larger screens** goes back to following it. |
| **Alignment** | **Start** or **Center** |
| **Width** | **Normal** or **Wide** |
| **Swap the order on phones** | For sections with two columns, such as an image beside text: on phones the columns stack, and this shows the second one first. |

**Back to the theme's design** clears every choice for the section.

## Why some options are missing

A theme offers only what it can draw well for that section. A background the theme has no style for, or one the section doesn't read well on, isn't offered, so the list changes from one section or theme to another. When a section has no choices, the tab says **Your theme sets this section's design; it has no choices to make here.**

> [!NOTE]
> The Design tab is in the visual editor only. The classic editor keeps a section's design as it is.
