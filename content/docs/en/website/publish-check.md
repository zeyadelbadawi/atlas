The academy owner and managers can publish a page. Each time you choose **Publish** in the visual editor or **Publish page** in the classic editor, Atlas looks through the page first. If it finds nothing, you confirm as usual. If it finds something, the check takes the place of the confirmation.

## Review and publish

1. Choose **Publish** in the visual editor (or **Publish page** in the classic editor).

   ![The Publish button at the top of the visual editor](shot:website/ve-publish)

2. If **Check this page before publishing** opens, read the list. Items are grouped by section, numbered by their place on the page. Choose an item to go straight to that section and field, fix it, then publish again. Or choose **Publish anyway** to publish the page as it is.

   ![The Check this page before publishing dialog listing three images without a description, with Publish anyway highlighted](shot:website/publish-check)

**Review first** closes the check without publishing.

## What the check looks for

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| An image has no description (alt text) in a language | Screen readers and search engines get nothing for it. |
| A button doesn't link anywhere yet | It stays hidden on your site until it has a link. |
| A button links to a page that no longer exists | Visitors would have nowhere to go. |
| A button links to a page that is hidden from visitors | The link would lead to a page visitors can't open. |
| Another main title (a second hero or page title) | Only the first is the page's main heading; the next one shows as a sub-heading. |
| Placeholder text, such as text in [brackets] | Template and look wording still waiting for your details. |
| Sample items | Samples never appear on your public site, so the section may look emptier than in the editor. |

> [!NOTE]
> The check looks only at the page you publish. **Publish changes** in the publish bar publishes the whole site without it, so open each page you changed and use its own **Publish** when you want the check.
