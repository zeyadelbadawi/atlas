You add sections from **Add section** in the visual editor (see [Edit your website in the visual editor](doc:DOC-WEB-015)) or the classic editor. The visual editor's library groups them under **Introduce your academy**, **Courses**, **Build trust**, **Explain and answer**, **Get in touch** and **Build your own**, with a search box. Many sections offer ready-made looks: see [Section looks and page templates](doc:DOC-WEB-016). Every text field has a box for your main language and an optional box for the other language.

![The Add a section library with its search box and section groups](shot:website/ve-library)

## At a glance

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | The large banner at the top of a page: headline, text, image and buttons |
| **Page header** | A smaller heading for inner pages, with an optional course or FAQ search |
| **About** | A title, body text and an image |
| **Featured Courses** | A selection of your courses |
| **Course Catalog** | Your full course list with search, filters and sorting |
| **Statistics** | Numbers with labels, typed by you or counted live |
| **Features** | A list of features, each with an icon, title and description |
| **Image with benefits** | An image beside a list of benefits |
| **Course in focus** | One course presented in detail |
| **How it works** | Numbered steps |
| Quotes section | Learner quotes from your library or typed in the section |
| **Instructors** | Your academy's instructors |
| **FAQ** | Questions and answers |
| **Gallery** | Images with captions |
| **Contact** | Your contact details and a contact form |
| **Call to Action** | A closing message with up to three buttons |
| **Logos and accreditations** | Partner and accreditation logos in a calm grid |
| **Student results** | Your top students and their results |
| **Video reviews** | Short video reviews from students and parents, played on request |
| **Certificates** | Images of your certificates, awards and accreditations |
| **Class schedule** | Your weekly timetable, by day and time |
| **Learning path** | Your learning path, stage by stage |
| **Comparison table** | A table comparing your academy or your plans, with ✓, ✗ or short text |
| **Video** | A YouTube video that loads only when visitors press play |
| **Downloads** | Notes, revision sheets and other files visitors can download |
| **Talk to us bar** | A short strip with WhatsApp, call or page buttons |
| **Countdown** | A live countdown to a registration deadline |
| **Register your interest** | A short form visitors fill in so you can call them back; it arrives in your Messages |
| **Map and directions** | Your address, a directions link and a map visitors can open |
| **Pricing plans** | Your plans or packages, with prices and what each includes |
| **Notice strip** | A slim strip with one short notice, such as "Registration is open" |
| **Custom section** | Your own layout: one to three columns of headings, text, buttons, images, videos and lists |

## Hero

Fields: **Eyebrow**, **Title**, **Highlighted phrase**, **Subtitle**, **Description**, **Image** with its alt text, up to three **Buttons**, **Show course search** and up to 4 **Highlights**.

Limits per language: eyebrow 60 characters, title and highlighted phrase 70, subtitle 140, description 280, button labels 40.

## Page header

Fields: **Eyebrow**, **Title**, **Description**, **Image** and **Search**: **No search**, **Course search** or **FAQ search**.

## About

Fields: **Title**, **Body**, **Image** and its alt text, and up to three **Buttons**.

## Featured Courses

Fields: **Title**, **Description**, **Source** (**Latest courses** or **Selected courses**), **Layout** (**Grid** or **Carousel**), **Number to show**, **Show price** and **Show instructor**. With **Selected courses**, you pick the courses and their order. Only published, public courses appear on your site; a picked course that stops being published or public is left out.

## Course Catalog

Fields: **Title**, **Description**, **Courses per page** (6 to 48), **Default sort order**, **Show search**, **Show level filter**, **Show pricing filter** and **Show sort control**. See [Course catalogue and course pages on your site](doc:DOC-WEB-014).

## Statistics

Each item has a **Data source**: **Custom text (below)** with your own **Value** and **Label**, or a live count: **Live course count**, **Live student count** or **Live instructor count**.

> [!NOTE]
> A live student count is public: every visitor sees your number of learners.

## Features

A **Title**, **Description** and a list of features, each with a **Title**, **Description** and an **Icon** from a fixed set.

## Image with benefits

Fields: **Eyebrow**, **Title**, **Description**, **Image**, **Image position** (**Before the text** or **After the text**), up to three **Buttons** and a list of up to 6 **Benefits**.

## Course in focus

Fields: **Eyebrow**, **Title**, **Description**, **Show what learners will be able to do**, **Show the syllabus**, **Syllabus sections to list** and a **Primary button**. Choose the course under **Course**; left on **Automatic: newest published course**, it shows your newest published course. If the chosen course is no longer published and public, the section is hidden on your site.

## How it works

A **Title**, **Description**, an optional **Image** and a list of **Steps**, each with a title and description. This section holds up to 6 steps.

## Quotes section

In **Add section** it's listed between **How it works** and **Instructors**. It shows quotes from your learners. Pick entries from your content library under **From your content library**, or type items in the section: quote, author name and role, photo and a rating. Library entries show first. Sample quotes from your starter site never appear on your live site. See [Reusable FAQs and quotes](doc:DOC-WEB-010).

## Instructors

A **Title**, **Description** and **Number to show**. The section lists your academy's instructors.

## FAQ

A **Title**, **Show at most**, **Link to all questions** and a list of **Questions**, each with a question and answer. You can also pick entries from your content library; they show before the questions typed in the section.

## Gallery

A **Title** and a list of **Images**, each with alt text and a **Caption**.

## Contact

Fields: **Title**, **Description**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Address** and **Show contact form**. Leave email, phone or address empty to use the contact details from your academy profile. Messages from the form arrive in the **Messages** tab of **Website**: see [Contact form and website messages](doc:DOC-WEB-013).

## Call to Action

A **Title**, **Description**, up to three **Buttons** and an **Image**.

## Logos and accreditations

A **Title** and a list of **Logos**, each with a **Logo image**, the **Organisation name (read aloud by screen readers)** and an optional **Link**.

## Student results

A list of **Results**, each with the student's name, the **Result**, an optional photo, a **Grade, year or exam** and an optional **Top student** mark. Sample results are placeholders: they never appear on your live site until you replace them and mark each one as real.

## Video reviews

A list of **Video reviews**, each with a YouTube link, the person's name and role, and an optional short quote. Each video loads only when a visitor presses play. Sample reviews never appear on your live site; replace them with real reviews, shared with permission, and mark each one as real.

## Certificates

A list of **Certificates**, each with a **Certificate image**, **Issued by (optional)** and **Year (optional)**.

## Class schedule

**Timetable rows**, each with a **Day**, **Start time**, **End time** (later than the start), **Course or group**, **Teacher** and **Place**. Sample rows never appear on your live site until you mark them as real.

## Learning path

A list of **Milestones**, each with a title, an optional **Period** (such as "Term 1"), a description, and an icon or, with no icon, its number.

## Comparison table

**Columns and rows**: compare your academy or your plans with ✓, ✗ or short text in each cell.

## Video

A **YouTube link**, an optional title, description and caption, and a **Poster image (optional)**. The video loads only when a visitor presses play.

## Downloads

A list of **Files**, each with a **Link or file** (a link, or a PDF from your media library), an optional **File type** and **File size**.

## Talk to us bar

A short line and up to three buttons, such as a WhatsApp chat, a phone call or one of your pages. The first button is required.

## Countdown

A title, a **Closes on** date and time and up to three buttons. When the date has passed, visitors see your **Message after it ends** instead of the countdown.

## Register your interest

A short form: visitors give their name and phone number, and, if you turn them on, an **Email field**, a **Choice list** (for example grades or courses) and a **Message field**. You can set the **Button text (optional)** and the **Message after sending (optional)**. Each submission arrives in **Messages**: see [Contact form and website messages](doc:DOC-WEB-013).

## Map and directions

Your **Address**, a **Landmark note** and an optional **Map search** (an address or coordinates). Visitors get a directions link and a map they can open.

## Pricing plans

A list of **Plans**, each with a name, a price and its period, a description, a list of what is or isn't included, an optional badge and a button. You can highlight one plan.

## Notice strip

One short **Notice**, a **Colour** and a **Button (optional)**.

## Custom section

One to three columns of elements you choose. See [Build your own layout with the custom section](doc:DOC-WEB-017).

## Buttons

Hero, About, Image with benefits, Call to Action, Countdown, Talk to us bar and the custom section's **Buttons** element hold up to three buttons. Choose **Add a button**, then open each button to set its label, where it goes and its **Look** (**Automatic**, **Bold**, **Outline** or **Text link**). Use the arrows to change their order.

![The Add a button control in the Hero section's settings](shot:website/editor-buttons)

For each button you choose where it goes:

- **Internal page**: one of your website pages;
- **Course**: one of your courses;
- **External URL**: a full web address;
- **WhatsApp chat**: a **WhatsApp number** (pick the country, then type the mobile number) and an optional **First message (optional)**;
- **Phone call**: a **Number to call**: a mobile, a landline or a short hotline such as 19999;
- **Sign-up page** or **Sign-in page**: your academy's own pages.

A button that isn't linked yet stays hidden on your site until it is.

## Limits for every section

- Lists hold up to 12 items, and a section can show up to 12 library entries.
- Short texts are limited to 100 characters and long texts to 2,000, unless a section has a tighter limit shown under the field.
- A section can be hidden by screen size or switched off. See [Edit your website in the visual editor](doc:DOC-WEB-015).
- A section's background, spacing, alignment and width are set on its **Design** tab. See [Section design options](doc:DOC-WEB-018).

> [!NOTE]
> Themes draw some sections differently, so a section can look different after you change theme. See [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002).

A page created before may also contain a **Course categories** section. It stays on the page but shows nothing on your site, and it isn't offered when you add a section, because academies can't create course categories.
