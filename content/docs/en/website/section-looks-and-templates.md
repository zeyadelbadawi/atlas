The academy owner and managers can add sections and pages. Looks and templates save time: they arrive designed for your theme and filled with sample wording in English and Arabic. Everything stays editable.

## Add a section from a look

1. In the visual editor, choose **Add section** and pick a section. A section that has looks shows how many, for example **4 looks**.

   ![The Add a section library](shot:website/ve-library)

2. Choose a look. Each preview uses your current theme. **Default** adds the section with its standard starting content. Then choose **Add section**. The section is added to your page, ready to edit.

   ![The Hero: choose a look step with four looks and the Add section button](shot:website/ve-looks)

In the classic editor, **Add section** offers the same looks in a dialog. Closing the dialog adds nothing.

## Start a new page from a template

1. Open **Website**, then the **Pages** tab, and choose **New page**. Under **How do you want to start?**, choose **Blank page** or one of the templates. The preview on the right shows the page with your theme, and Atlas suggests a title and an address.

   ![The Create a custom page dialog with the Course landing page template selected and its preview](shot:website/pages-templates)

2. Change **Page title** and **URL slug** if you like, then choose **Create page**. Atlas creates the page with all of the template's sections in one step and opens it in the visual editor.

   ![The Create a custom page dialog with a page title and a URL slug filled in](shot:website/pages-create-dialog)

### The four templates

| Template | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Course landing page** | One course: what it covers, its fee, its deadline and a register button. |
| **Teacher profile** | One teacher: introduction, class times, results and courses. |
| **Offers and registration** | Your offer, plans, a deadline and a registration form. |
| **About the academy** | Your story, live numbers, teachers and what parents say. |

## Make it your own

- Text in **[brackets]**, such as **[Course name]**, is a placeholder. Replace it with your own details. The check before publishing lists any placeholder still on the page: see [Check a page before you publish it](doc:DOC-WEB-020).
- Sample quotes and sample student results are marked **Sample**. They never appear on your public site until you replace them and mark them as real.
- Buttons that point to one of your pages only link to pages visitors can reach.

> [!NOTE]
> If a template's sections don't pass your site's checks (for example a page a button links to no longer exists), Atlas doesn't create the page and says so. Choose **Blank page** or another template.
