Your site shows your courses in three places: the **Courses** page, the course sections you add to other pages, and a page for each course at `/courses/<course address>`.

![The Courses page of the academy website as a visitor sees it](shot:website/public-catalog)

## Which courses are listed

A course appears on your site only when it is both:

- **published**: see [Publish your course](doc:DOC-LRN-011);
- **public**: private courses never appear in the catalogue. See [Course details: visibility, level, language, outcomes and thumbnail](doc:DOC-LRN-004).

The same rule applies to **Featured Courses** and **Course in focus** sections.

## The catalogue

The Courses page uses a **Course Catalog** section. In its settings you choose how many courses show per page, the default order (**Newest first**, **Title (A–Z)**, **Price: low to high** or **Price: high to low**) and whether visitors see a search box, a level filter, a pricing filter and a sort control. See the [Section types reference](doc:DOC-WEB-005).

## The course page

The course details page is a core page that works for every course. It shows the course's title, description and level, **What you'll learn**, **Requirements** and **Course content** (sections and lessons), plus **Learner reviews** and **Related courses**.

![A course page on the academy website with the Sign in to enroll button](shot:website/public-course)

## The button a visitor sees

| Visitor | Button |
|---|---|
| Not signed in | **Sign in to enroll** |
| Signed in, paid course | **Buy this course**: see [Buy a course](doc:DOC-COM-010) |
| Signed in, course with no price | The enroll button, which enrolls them straight away |
| Enrolled, not started | **Start course** |
| Enrolled, in progress | **Continue Learning** |
| Finished the course | **Course completed · Review** |

## Reviews and ratings

Only reviews you have approved appear on the course page. See [Course reviews and moderation](doc:DOC-LRN-023).

## If a course is missing from your site

- Check that the course is published and public.
- Check that your site itself is published: see [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).
- In a **Featured Courses** section set to **Selected courses**, check that the course is picked.

> [!NOTE]
> Academies can't create course categories, so category filters don't apply to your site, and a Course categories section shows nothing on it.
