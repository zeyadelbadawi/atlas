The academy owner and managers can change the menu, header and footer. On the **Navigation** tab there's no Save button: each change saves to your draft as soon as you make it (text fields save when you leave the box).

## Edit your menu, header and footer

1. Open **Website**, then **Settings**, then the **Navigation** tab.

   ![The Navigation tab with the Primary navigation card](shot:website/settings-navigation)

2. Under **Primary navigation**, tick each page you want in the main menu. The Course Details page isn't offered, because it shows individual courses.

   ![The page checkboxes under Primary navigation](shot:website/nav-menu-pages)

3. Under **Order**, edit each **Menu item label** in both languages, and use **Move up** and **Move down** to order the items.

   ![The Order list with a menu item label and the move buttons](shot:website/nav-menu-order)

4. Under **Header call-to-action**, set the **Button label**, then the **Link type**: **Internal page**, **External URL** or **Sign In / Sign Up**, and choose where it goes in **Links to**.

   ![The Header call-to-action card](shot:website/nav-header-cta)

5. Under **Footer**, fill the **Copyright text** in both languages.

   ![The Footer card with the Copyright text field](shot:website/nav-footer)

6. Under **Social links**, choose **Add link**, pick the network in **Choose a platform**, and paste the address in **Link**.

   ![The Social links area with the Add link button](shot:website/nav-social)

7. To change the headings of your Sign In and Sign Up pages, open the **Pages** tab and choose **Sign In** or **Sign Up**. Fill **Heading** and **Subheading**, or leave them empty to keep the default wording.

   ![The Sign In page dialog with the Heading and Subheading fields](shot:website/pages-auth-dialog)

8. Publish your site: in the publish bar, choose **Publish changes** and confirm.

   ![The publish bar on the Settings page with Publish changes highlighted](shot:website/settings-publish)

## Rules and limits

- The main menu holds up to 12 items, and each item opens one of your own pages. To link to another website, use the header button or a section button.
- You can add up to 12 social links. The supported networks are Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat, Threads, Discord and GitHub. Each network's icon shows in your site's footer.
- The same link can't be added twice. A link saved before networks could be chosen asks you to pick its platform so its icon shows.
- Sign In and Sign Up are fixed pages that already use your logo, colours and footer. Only their heading text can change.
- Fill the labels in both languages: where the other language is empty, visitors see the main-language label. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

> [!NOTE]
> Your site's header always has a **My Learn** item, and signed-in learners also get their account menu there. Atlas adds these, and they can't be removed.

## If someone else changed these settings

If a colleague changed the navigation while you had the tab open, Atlas shows **Someone else changed these settings in the meantime. Their version is now shown — make your change again.** Make your change again on top of their version. See [Website save conflict](doc:DOC-TRB-013).
