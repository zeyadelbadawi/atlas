The academy owner and managers can change the website settings.

## The settings tabs at a glance

| Tab | What it holds | How it saves | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | The look of your whole site | Right away, to your draft | [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002) |
| **SEO** | Site title, description, share image, indexing | **Save changes**, to your draft | [SEO settings for your academy site](doc:DOC-WEB-012) |
| **Navigation** | Main menu, header button, footer and social links | Each change, to your draft | [Navigation, header, footer and social links](doc:DOC-WEB-009) |
| **Language** | Your site's main language | **Save changes**, to your draft | [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006) |
| **Domain** | Your Atlas address and your own domain | See the guide | [Connect your own domain](doc:DOC-ACD-006) |

## Change and save a setting

1. Open **Website**, then the **Settings** tab, and choose the tab you need.

   ![The website settings with the Theme, SEO, Navigation, Language and Domain tabs](shot:website/settings-tabs)

2. Make your change. On the **SEO** and **Language** tabs, choose **Save changes** when you're done.

   ![The Save changes button at the bottom of the SEO tab](shot:website/seo-save)

3. If you open another tab or leave the page with unsaved changes, Atlas asks **Leave without saving?**. Choose **Stay on this page** to go back and save.

   ![The Leave without saving? dialog with the Stay on this page button](shot:website/settings-unsaved-dialog)

4. Your logo, favicon and colours are on a separate page: open **Visual Identity** in the sidebar. These changes go live on your site as soon as you save them, without publishing.

   ![The Visual Identity page with the Logo and colours card](shot:website/visual-identity)

5. Publish your other settings: in the publish bar, choose **Publish changes** and confirm.

   ![The publish bar on the Settings page with Publish changes highlighted](shot:website/settings-publish)

Visual Identity is covered in [Brand Studio: logo, colours and favicon](doc:DOC-ACD-004). If a colleague saved the same settings first, Atlas shows their version and asks you to make your change again.

## The cookie banner on your academy site

Every academy site shows a cookie banner to new visitors, titled **Cookies and similar technologies**. It uses your site's colours and names your academy.

### What visitors can choose

- **Accept all**, **Reject optional**, or **Manage preferences**.
- There are two categories: **Strictly necessary**, which keeps visitors signed in and is always on, and **Preferences**, which remembers choices such as language. Visitors can turn **Preferences** off.
- The choice is stored in the visitor's browser, so the banner doesn't come back on later visits from that browser.

### What you can and can't change

> [!LIMITATION]
> The cookie banner has no settings. You can't change its text, hide it, or add categories. It has no analytics or marketing category, because academy sites don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

## Can I add Google Analytics or a tracking pixel?

No. The website builder has no field for scripts, analytics tags or tracking pixels. See [What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004) for privacy details.
