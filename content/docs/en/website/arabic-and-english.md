The academy owner and managers set up the languages of the site. Every text a visitor reads, in sections, menus and SEO fields, has two boxes: one for the main language and one for the other language.

## How languages work on your site

- **Main language**: English or Arabic. It opens at your site's main address, and every text needs it.
- **Other language**: optional. With English as the main language, the Arabic pages live under `/ar` (for example `/ar/courses`). With Arabic as the main language, the English pages live under `/en`.
- **Missing translations**: where a box in the other language is empty, visitors on that version see the main-language text. An unfinished translation gives a page in two languages, so fill every box before you publish.
- **Switching**: visitors can switch to the other language from the site's header at any time.
- Arabic pages are shown right to left.

## Set up both languages

1. Open **Website**, then **Settings**, then the **Language** tab, and choose your **Main language**.

   ![The Language tab with the two main language options](shot:website/settings-language)

2. Choose **Save changes**. The new main language goes live when you publish your site, so you can add your text in that language first.

   ![The Save changes button on the Language tab](shot:website/settings-language-save)

3. In the page editor, open each section and fill the box for the other language. It shows **Not translated yet** until you fill it, then **Complete**.

   ![The section dialog for the Hero section, with English and optional Arabic boxes](shot:website/editor-section-dialog)

4. On the **Navigation** tab of **Settings**, fill both languages for your menu labels, the header button and the copyright text.

   ![The Navigation tab of the website settings](shot:website/settings-navigation)

5. On the **SEO** tab, fill both languages for **Site title**, **Meta title** and **Meta description**.

   ![The SEO tab with the title and description fields](shot:website/settings-seo)

6. Open the **Preview** tab, choose the other language from the language list, and check each page.

   ![The Preview tab with the language list highlighted](shot:website/preview-language)

7. On the **Website** overview, choose **Publish changes** and confirm. Then open your site and its other-language address to check both versions.

   ![The publish bar on the Website overview with Publish changes highlighted](shot:website/overview-publish)

## Reusable questions and quotes

Entries in your content library on the **Content** tab need both languages: Atlas doesn't save an entry until both boxes are filled. See [Reusable FAQs and quotes](doc:DOC-WEB-010).

## Changing the main language later

- Old links keep working: they forward to the new address. Switching back restores the previous addresses.
- After a switch, the new main language becomes the required one. A section whose text exists only in the other language must be completed before that page can be saved.

## Language hints under a box

If a box looks like it holds the wrong language (for example English text in the Arabic box), Atlas shows a hint. It's not an error, and you can still save: brand names, technical terms, numbers and links are fine.

> [!TIP]
> Each published page tells search engines where its version in the other language is. See [SEO settings for your academy site](doc:DOC-WEB-012).
