The academy owner and managers can edit pages. The **Page Editor** shows the page's sections under **Composition** on one side and a live preview of the page on the other.

## Edit a page

1. In **Website**, open the **Pages** tab and choose the page you want to edit, for example Home.

   ![The Pages tab with the Home page row highlighted](shot:website/pages-open-row)

2. To add a section, choose **Add section** under the section list and pick a type. The new section is added at the end of the page, and its settings open.

   ![The Add section menu listing the section types](shot:website/editor-add-section)

3. To edit a section, choose its name or the pencil button (**Edit section**). Its settings open in a dialog with a preview of the section.

   ![The section dialog for the Hero section, with English and optional Arabic boxes](shot:website/editor-section-dialog)

4. Fill in the fields, then choose **Apply changes**. Each text has a box for your site's main language and an optional box for the other language.

   ![The Apply changes button at the bottom of the section dialog](shot:website/editor-section-apply)

5. To show a section only on some screen sizes, use the **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** buttons on the section. A button that is switched off hides the section at that size.

   ![The Desktop, Tablet and Mobile buttons on a section in the Composition list](shot:website/editor-visibility)

6. Choose **Save draft** to save your changes. Visitors don't see them yet.

   ![The Save draft button at the top of the page editor](shot:website/editor-save)

7. To put only this page live, choose **Publish page** and confirm. Other pages and your site settings stay as they are published.

   ![The Publish page button next to Save draft](shot:website/editor-publish-page)

## More things you can do with a section

- **Move up** and **Move down** change the order of sections.
- **Duplicate section** adds a copy right after it.
- **Delete section** removes it after you confirm.
- The **Enabled** switch hides a section on every screen size without deleting it.

## Text in two languages

The main language box is required; the other language is optional and shows **Not translated yet** until you fill it, then **Complete**. Where the other language is empty, visitors see the main-language text. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

Under a box, Atlas may warn that the text doesn't look like the expected language. It's a hint, not an error: brand names, technical terms, numbers and links are fine.

Text fields have character limits. A counter under the field shows how much you've used, and Atlas asks you to shorten text that is too long. Lists in a section hold up to 12 items. Section limits are in the [Section types reference](doc:DOC-WEB-005).

## Save draft or publish

- **Save draft** (shown as **Save changes** while your site isn't published yet) saves the page to your working copy.
- **Publish page** appears only while your site is live. It saves your changes and makes this one page live.
- **Publish changes** in the publish bar publishes every saved page and your site settings together. See [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).

> [!WARNING]
> Changes are never saved automatically. If you try to leave the editor with unsaved changes, Atlas asks you first: **Stay on this page**, **Leave and discard**, or **Save and leave**.

## When someone else is editing

If a colleague has the same page open, a banner at the top names them. If they save first, Atlas shows **This page changed while you were editing** with two choices: **Reload latest** to start again from their version, or **Keep my changes** to apply yours on top of theirs. Their work is never discarded.

## If saving is blocked

- **Shorten text before saving**: a section has text over its current limit. Your live site still shows it, but the page can't be saved until you shorten it. Use the button for each named section.
- **These sections need attention before this page can be saved**: open each named section and fix the fields it marks.
