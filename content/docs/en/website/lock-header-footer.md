Use the lock when your menu, header and footer are final and you don't want them changed by mistake, for example while a manager works on your pages.

## Turn the lock on or off

1. Open **Website**, then **Settings**, then the **Menu, header & footer** tab. Turn on **Lock the header, menu and footer**. To unlock, turn the same switch off.

   ![The Lock the header, menu and footer switch on the Menu, header & footer tab](shot:website/settings-lock)

The lock takes effect at once; there is nothing to publish.

## Who can lock and unlock

- Only the academy owner (or an academy administrator) can turn the lock on or off. They see the switch in Website settings and in the visual editor's **Site** panel.
- Managers don't see the switch. While the lock is on, they see **The header, menu and footer are locked** with the fields read-only, and they ask the owner to unlock it if something needs updating.

## What the lock covers

- The main menu: which pages it shows, their labels and their order.
- The header button.
- The footer copyright text and your social links.
- The **Heading** and **Subheading** of the Sign In and Sign Up pages.

While locked, these fields are read-only for everyone, the owner included: turn the lock off to edit them. Pages, sections, the theme, Visual Identity and search settings save as usual.

> [!NOTE]
> If a change to these areas is refused, Atlas says **The header, menu and footer are locked, so this change wasn't saved.** Ask the owner to unlock them first.
