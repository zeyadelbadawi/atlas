The activity log answers "who changed this, and when?" for one academy. Only the organization owner (or the academy's owner) can open it. Managers and instructors can't.

## Open the log

1. In the academy sidebar, select **Activity log**. The page reads **Who changed what in this academy, and when.**

   ![The Activity log link in the sidebar and the list of changes](shot:analytics/activity-log-open)

2. To narrow the list, use **Search**, **Category**, **From** and **To** at the top. Select **Clear filters** to start again.

   ![The Search, Category, From and To filters](shot:analytics/activity-log-filters)

3. Select an entry to open **Activity details**.

   ![The Activity details panel with Who, When and What changed](shot:analytics/activity-log-entry)

## Read an entry

Each line is a sentence such as who created a course, published it or removed a team member. **Activity details** shows **Who**, **When**, **What happened**, their **Role**, the **Category** and the **Item**. When Atlas recorded the individual fields, **What changed** lists each **Field** with its **Before** and **After** values.

To see only one person's changes, select their name on a line. The list then shows a **Person** filter you can remove.

Changes made by the Atlas team appear under the name **Atlas**. Older entries load when you select **Load more** at the bottom.

## Categories

The **Category** filter groups changes, for example **Courses**, **Quizzes & assignments**, **Website**, **Academy settings**, **Team**, **Students**, **Certificates**, **Media**, **Domains** and **Payments**.

## What isn't in the log

- Sign-ins, passwords, two-step verification and other security events.
- Email addresses: people appear by name.
- Actions that only the Atlas platform team can see.

> [!LIMITATION]
> The activity log isn't a complete security audit trail, and it can't be exported.

The owner also sees the latest few changes on the **Academy home** page, under **Recent Activity**, with a link to **View full activity log**. See [Dashboards overview](doc:DOC-ANL-001).
