Atlas has a few places that show numbers. This page explains what each one counts, who sees it, and where to go for more detail. Every figure comes from what really happened in your academy; nothing is estimated.

## Your Dashboard

**Dashboard** is the first page in the sidebar.

- The **owner** sees the whole organization: every academy together.
- A **manager** sees only the academy they work in.

![The owner's Dashboard with the counts, Revenue and Plan usage cards](shot:analytics/dashboard-owner)

### What each card counts

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Academies** (owner) | Academies in your organization. |
| **Published courses** (manager) | Published courses in the academy. |
| **Courses** | All courses, whether published or not. |
| **Students** | Everyone on your academies' Students lists, whatever their status. A learner in two of your academies counts in each. |
| **Instructors** | Active instructors on your team. |

### The Revenue card

What the **Revenue** card shows depends on who you are and how you collect payments.

- **Owner, with manual payment methods** (bank transfer, mobile wallet, InstaPay): the card shows **Sales recorded in Atlas (manual payments)**. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** to see the total of the payments you approved, and how many approved orders it covers. Your academy received this money directly. A payment you record as refunded (**Record refund…**) drops out of the total; money you return without recording it isn't subtracted.
- **Owner, with no payment method set up yet**: **Not tracked here**, with a prompt to set up how you collect payments. See [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).
- **Manager**: always **Not tracked here**. Sales figures are for the owner only.

![The Revenue card with the 7, 30 and 90 day choices](shot:analytics/dashboard-revenue-owner)

For each order and payment one by one, the owner uses **Course orders**. See [Track course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).

![The Dashboard as a manager, with Revenue showing Not tracked here](shot:analytics/dashboard-manager)

### Plan usage and Recent activity

**Plan usage** shows what you use against your plan's limits, such as academies, courses, students, instructors and storage. See [The Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006).

**Recent activity** lists the latest changes, such as courses created or published, lessons added and team changes, with who made them. A manager sees their own academy's changes only.

## The Academy Overview page

**Academy Overview** in the sidebar opens one academy's page. Its cards show:

- **Total Members**: people on the academy's team, including the owner. Learners aren't counted here.
- **Active Staff**: active members of the academy's team, whatever their role: the owner, managers and instructors. Team members you removed aren't counted.
- **Active Instructors** and **Published Courses**.

The owner also sees **Recent Activity** for this academy, with **View full activity log**.

![The Academy Overview page with its cards and recent activity](shot:analytics/academy-overview)

## Other reports

| Page | Who sees it | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [Student Analytics](doc:DOC-ANL-002) | Owner | How many enrolled students started and finished, monthly trends, and students who may need help. |
| [Reports](doc:DOC-ANL-003) | Owner, managers | Quiz integrity events, lesson-access sharing signals and plan usage for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. |
| [Activity log](doc:DOC-ANL-004) | Owner | Who changed what in the academy, and when. |
| [Student details](doc:DOC-ORG-009) | Owner, managers | One learner's enrollments, progress, quiz and assignment results. |

Instructors don't see these pages. They follow their own courses from the **Teaching Dashboard**: students, progress, quiz results and submissions to grade. See [Your teaching dashboard](doc:DOC-LRN-024) and [Review attempts and grade essay answers](doc:DOC-LRN-017).

> [!LIMITATION]
> These pages have no export or download, and no custom date range: you choose from the periods each page offers.
