Student Analytics answers two questions: how many of your students finish, and who needs a nudge. It covers every academy in your organization. Only the organization owner sees it.

## Open Student Analytics

1. In the sidebar, select **Student Analytics**.

   ![The Student Analytics link in the sidebar and the page it opens](shot:analytics/student-analytics-open)

2. Scroll down to **Students who may need help** to see who is falling behind.

   ![The Students who may need help list with a reason next to each student](shot:analytics/student-analytics-at-risk)

## The numbers at the top

Four cards sum up the page: **Enrolled**, **Started**, **Completed** and **Students at risk**.

## Completion funnel

**Completion funnel** shows three counts, each with its share of enrolled students:

- **Enrolled**: course enrollments that are active or completed.
- **Started**: of those, enrollments where the student has finished at least one thing, such as a lesson, a passed quiz or a submitted assignment. Opening a course without finishing anything doesn't count.
- **Completed**: enrollments where the student completed the course.

![The Completion funnel with Enrolled, Started and Completed](shot:analytics/student-analytics-funnel)

## Cohort trends

**Cohort trends** is a table with one row per month for the last six months: **New enrolments** and **Completions** in that month. A month with nothing shows 0.

![The Cohort trends table by month](shot:analytics/student-analytics-trends)

## Students who may need help

This list shows students on a course they haven't finished, with the course, how many lessons or activities they have done, and the reason they were flagged:

| Reason | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Hasn't started** | Enrolled for more than 14 days and has finished nothing yet. |
| **No recent progress** | Started, but has completed nothing in the last 14 days. |
| **Hasn't passed a quiz** | Has a scored attempt on a quiz in this course and hasn't passed it yet. |

The page states the number of days in the description above the list. It shows up to 100 students; the **Students at risk** card gives the full count.

## What to do next

- Open the student on **Members** → **Students** to see their enrollments and results. See [Student list and student details](doc:DOC-ORG-009).
- Send a message or reminder to your learners. See [Message your learners](doc:DOC-MSG-003).

> [!LIMITATION]
> Student Analytics is built only from enrollments, completed lessons and quiz results. It has no watch time, page views or engagement score. There is no date filter, per-course view or export on this page.
