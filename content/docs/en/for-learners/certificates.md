Certificates are issued automatically when you meet the course's completion
rules, if the academy awards a certificate for that course. Nothing to
request.

## Find your certificates

1. In My Learn, open **Certificates**. After you finish a course you can also choose **View certificate** on the **Course complete** screen.

   ![The Certificates page in My Learn](shot:for-learners/certificates-page)

2. To see what someone checking your certificate sees, open your verification link. The academy's **Verify a certificate** page shows **Valid certificate** and the certificate details.

   ![The academy's Verify a certificate page showing a valid certificate](shot:for-learners/certificate-verify)

## Download and share

On each certificate card:

- **Download PDF** gets the file. "Each download fetches a fresh link that
  stays valid for one hour." If a link stops working, press **Download PDF**
  again.
- **Copy verification link** copies a link to the academy's verification
  page for this certificate. Send it to an employer or school.
- **Copy code** copies the **Verification code** if someone prefers to type
  it on the verification page.

If the card says **Preparing your certificate…**, the PDF is still being
made: choose **Refresh** in a moment.

> [!NOTE]
> Anyone with the link or code sees the name on the certificate, the course,
> the academy and the dates. Share it only with people who need it.

## Why you might not have a certificate

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Certificates are not being issued by this academy yet** | No course in this academy awards certificates at the moment. |
| **No certificates yet** | You haven't completed a course that awards one. Check what is left in **Course progress**. |
| **Revoked on …** | The academy withdrew the certificate. It can't be downloaded, and the verification page shows it as revoked. |

Questions about a missing or wrong certificate go to the academy:
[How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003). How the check works:
