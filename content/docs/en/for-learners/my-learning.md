**My Learn** is where everything you do in one academy lives: your courses,
quizzes, assignments, certificates, purchases and devices. You need to be
signed in on the academy's own website ([Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030)).

## Open My Learn and continue a course

1. On the academy's website, choose **My Learn** at the top of the page. If you are signed out, you are asked to sign in first.

   ![The academy home page with the My Learn link highlighted in the header](shot:for-learners/academy-home-my-learn)

2. Start on **Overview**: **Continue learning** takes you to your next lesson, **Due soon** lists work with a deadline, and **Recent results** and **Announcements** show what's new.

   ![The Overview page of My Learn](shot:for-learners/my-learn-overview)

3. Open **My Courses** and use the **All**, **In progress** and **Completed** tabs. Each course card shows where you are, **Not started**, **In progress** or **Completed**, and one button: **Start course**, **Continue learning** or **Review course**.

   ![My Courses with the All, In progress and Completed tabs highlighted](shot:for-learners/my-courses)

4. Choose a course title to open **Course progress**. It lists every lesson, quiz and assignment with its state, such as **Completed**, **In progress** or **Locked**.

   ![The Course progress page listing each activity and its state](shot:for-learners/course-progress)

5. Open a lesson. Use **Contents** to jump around the course, **Previous** and **Next** to move on, and **Mark complete** when you finish a lesson.

   ![A lesson in the player with the Next button highlighted](shot:for-learners/lesson-player)

## What the course buttons mean

| Button | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Start course** | You are enrolled and haven't started yet. |
| **Continue learning** | Opens the lesson you reached. |
| **Review course** | You finished the course; you can still open it. |
| **Access ended** | Your access to this course has ended (for example, the academy ended your enrollment). |

When you finish the last activity, choose **Finish course**. You then see
**Course complete**, and **View certificate** if the course awards one
([Download and share your certificate](doc:DOC-LRN-035)).

## Why a lesson is locked

The course outline says why, in one line:

- **Finish the activity before this one first**: the academy wants you to go in order.
- **Pass the required quiz before this one first**: pass that quiz to continue ([Take a quiz or exam](doc:DOC-LRN-033)).
- **Your access to this course has ended**: contact the academy.

Some lessons only let you choose **Mark complete** after you have watched
enough of the video. On a YouTube lesson Atlas can't see how much you
watched, so the page tells you if the lesson can't be completed
automatically.

## Watching on one device at a time

Lessons play on one device at a time. If you open a lesson on a second
device, you are asked whether to **Continue here**. Each academy also limits
how many devices you can use: see [Manage your devices](doc:DOC-MED-010).

## If your connection drops

- Videos always need a connection and are never saved on your device.
- If you choose **Mark complete** while offline, the page says it is saved on
  this device and sends it when you are back online.
- If you submit an assignment while offline, it waits to send. It hasn't
  reached your instructor until you are back online, and the due date is
  checked when it arrives.
- Quizzes need a connection to submit.

## Your profile and your data

Open **Profile** in My Learn to change your details. On the **Account** tab,
**Your data** lets you ask for a copy of your personal data or ask for your
account to be deleted. Atlas confirms it's you with a code sent by email, and
emails you when the request is done.

![The Account tab of Profile with the Your data card highlighted](shot:for-learners/profile-your-data)

Need help with a course? Use **Contact your academy** in My Learn, or see
[How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003).
