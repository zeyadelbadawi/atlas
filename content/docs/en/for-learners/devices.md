A "device" here is a browser you have used to learn at this academy: Chrome
on your laptop and Safari on your phone are two devices. A new browser, or a
browser where you cleared the site's data, counts as a new device.

## See and remove your devices

1. In My Learn, open **Devices**.

   ![My Learn with Devices highlighted in the section list](shot:for-learners/devices-nav)

2. Read the page: **Registered devices** shows how many of your device slots you use and marks the one you are on as **This device**. **Active learning sessions** shows which device is learning right now.

   ![The Devices page with This device highlighted](shot:for-learners/devices-page)

3. Next to a device you no longer use, choose **Remove**, then confirm in **Remove this device?**.

   ![The Remove this device? confirmation dialog](shot:for-learners/devices-remove-confirm)

## What happens next

The slot is free and you can open lessons on another device. A removed
device has to register again the next time you learn on it, and then takes a
slot again.

> [!WARNING]
> If you remove the device you are using right now, your current learning
> session ends and this browser registers again the next time you open a
> lesson.

## "You have reached your device limit"

This appears when you open a lesson on one device too many. You can still
sign in and see your courses, progress and payments; only lessons are
refused. Remove an old device from the same dialog or from **Devices**,
then open the lesson again.

## "You are already learning on another device"

Lessons play on one device at a time. If you open a lesson while another
device is learning, choose **Continue here** to move to this device, or
**Keep learning there**. The other device pauses with "Paused — you started
learning on another device"; nothing you did is lost.

> [!NOTE]
> The academy can't remove or reset your devices for you. Only you can, from
> this page. Each academy counts your devices separately.
