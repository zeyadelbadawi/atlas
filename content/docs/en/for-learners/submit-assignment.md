Assignments are written answers you send to the academy for grading,
optionally with one file.

## Steps

1. In My Learn, open **Assessments** and choose the **Assignments** tab. You can also open the assignment from the course outline.

   ![The Assessments page with the Assignments tab highlighted](shot:for-learners/assessments-assignments)

2. Open the assignment and read **Instructions**, the due date, **After the due date** and **Resubmission**.

   ![An assignment page showing its instructions and rules](shot:for-learners/assignment-page)

3. Write your answer in **Your Response**. The page shows **Draft saved** as you type.

   ![The assignment form with the Your Response box highlighted](shot:for-learners/assignment-response)

4. To add a file, choose **Upload File** under **Attachment**. The form says: "Optional. PDF or image (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP), max 10MB. Save Word documents as PDF first."

   ![The Attachment section of the assignment form with its file rules highlighted](shot:for-learners/assignment-attach)

5. Press **Submit Assignment** and confirm in **Submit this assignment?**. (When you change a submission, the button reads **Resubmit**.)

   ![The assignment form with the submit button highlighted](shot:for-learners/assignment-submit)

## What happens next

The page shows **Your submission** with the date you sent it and "Waiting
for a reviewer to grade it. You will be notified." When it is graded you see
your score and **Feedback** on the assignment and in **Assessments**.

![The submitted assignment with its submission details highlighted](shot:for-learners/assignment-submitted)

## Late work

Each assignment says what happens **After the due date**:

- **Submissions are not accepted**: you can't submit after the due date.
- **Submissions are accepted and marked late**: you can still submit, and
  your submission shows **Submitted late**.

## Changing your submission

If **Resubmission** says **Allowed until graded**, choose **Resubmit**, edit
your answer and confirm **Replace your submission?**. The new version
replaces the old one. If it says **Not allowed**, you can't change it after
submitting.

> [!TIP]
> Word files are not accepted. In Word or Google Docs, save or download your
> file as PDF, then attach the PDF.

> [!NOTE]
> If you lose your connection, text you wrote stays on this device, and a
> submission you send while offline waits until you are back online
> ([Find your way around My Learn](doc:DOC-LRN-031)).
