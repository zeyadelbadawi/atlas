Free courses open as soon as you enroll. You only need an account on this
academy ([Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030)).

## Steps

1. On the academy's website, open the course. If you are signed out, the button on the course page reads **Sign in to enroll**: choose it.

   ![A free course page for a signed-out visitor with Sign in to enroll highlighted](shot:for-learners/free-course-signed-out)

2. Sign in with your email and password. You come back to the same course page.

   ![The academy's sign-in page with the Sign In button highlighted](shot:for-learners/sign-in)

3. Press the enroll button on the course page. On a free course it says the course is free.

   ![A free course page for a signed-in learner with the enroll button highlighted](shot:for-learners/free-course-enroll)

## What happens next

You are taken to the course's **Course progress** page and can open the
first lesson. The course is now listed in **My Courses** with a
**Start course** button, and the course page shows **Start course** or
**Continue Learning** instead of the enroll button.

## If the button says something else

| Button | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Buy this course** | The course is paid. See [Buy a course: pay and upload your proof](doc:DOC-COM-010). |
| **Sign in to enroll** | You are signed out. Sign in on this academy's site. |
| **Start course** or **Continue Learning** | You are already enrolled. |

If you see "We couldn't enroll you in this course. Please try again." and it
keeps happening, contact the academy. Enrolling a second time does no harm.
If the message adds that the academy didn't approve your registration, you
can apply again ([Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030)).

> [!NOTE]
> Your account must belong to this academy. If you learn at another academy
> on Atlas, join this one first with the same account
> ([Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030)).
