Each academy keeps its own purchases and payments. Sign in on the academy's
website to see the ones you made there.

## Steps

1. In My Learn, open **Purchases**. Each order shows the course, the date and its status.

   ![The Purchases page in My Learn](shot:for-learners/purchases-page)

2. Open **Payments**. The **My payments** page lists every payment you sent to this academy, with its method, date and status.

   ![The My payments page with a payment and its status](shot:for-learners/payments-page)

3. To check what you sent, choose **View proof** on a payment.

   ![The proof you uploaded, open from the My payments page](shot:for-learners/payment-proof)

## What each status means

**Purchases**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | The order is open; send your payment and proof. |
| **Paid** | The payment was approved and you have the course. |
| **Expired** | The order wasn't paid in time. Start again from the course page. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was closed. |
| **Refunded** | The academy recorded a refund. Your access to the course has ended. |

**My payments**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for your proof** | Send the payment, then upload your proof to finish. |
| **Under review** | The academy is checking your payment. You'll get an email when it's reviewed. |
| **Approved** | Your payment was approved and you have access to the course. |
| **Rejected** | This payment was not approved, so you don't have access yet. |
| **Closed** | This payment is no longer open. |

## Your payment was rejected

A rejected payment shows **Reason from the academy**. If there's no reason,
you see "No reason was given. Contact the academy if you have questions."
Fix what the academy asked for, then choose **Submit a new payment** (or
**Continue payment**) on that payment. After a rejection the order stays
open for another 72 hours.

## Refunds and receipts

You paid the academy directly, so refunds are handled by the academy:
contact it ([Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005)). When the
academy records the refund, the order shows **Refunded** with the amount and
date, and you get an email. Atlas doesn't
offer a downloadable receipt; the emails you receive are your record
([Receipts and purchase emails](doc:DOC-COM-006)).

Still waiting or confused about a status? See
[Payment proof pending or rejected](doc:DOC-TRB-008).
