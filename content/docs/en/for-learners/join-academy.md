You sign up on **your academy's own website** (the address your teacher or
academy gave you), not on the Atlas dashboard. The account you create there
is a student account for that academy.

## Before you start

- Have your email address and mobile number ready. The form says how the
  number is used: if you join an academy as a student, its owners and
  managers can see it, and so can Atlas platform administrators.
- Choose a password of at least 8 characters.

## Create a new account

1. On the academy's website, choose **Sign up** at the top of the page.

   ![The academy home page with the Sign up link highlighted in the header](shot:for-learners/academy-home-sign-up)

2. Fill in **Full Name**, **Email**, **Mobile number**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.

   ![The academy sign-up form with the Full Name field highlighted](shot:for-learners/sign-up-form)

3. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**, then choose **Create Account**.

   ![The bottom of the sign-up form with the Create Account button highlighted](shot:for-learners/sign-up-submit)

4. Choose **Go to sign in** and sign in with your email and password.

   ![The academy's sign-in page with the Sign In button highlighted](shot:for-learners/sign-in)

## What happens next

After you sign in, you land in **My Learn**, your learning area on this
academy's site. See [Find your way around My Learn](doc:DOC-LRN-031).

Atlas makes sure the email is really yours. Depending on how the academy is
set up, you either get a verification link by email, or the first time you
sign in on a browser you are asked for a code sent to your email
([Verify your email address](doc:DOC-IAM-004)).

## Already have an Atlas account from another academy?

You don't need a second account. Under the form, next to **Already have an
Atlas account?**, choose **Join with it**, enter your email and the password
you already use, and choose **Continue with my Atlas account**. If you type
an email that already has an Atlas account into the normal form, the page
offers the same step.

![The join form for an existing Atlas account with the Continue with my Atlas account button highlighted](shot:for-learners/sign-up-join-existing)

The academy is added to your account. Your courses, progress and
certificates stay separate in each academy, and you sign in on each
academy's own website. More in [How learner accounts work across academies](doc:DOC-ORG-011).

## If the academy approves new students first

Some academies review every new student. After you sign up you see
"Your request to join … has been sent. You'll be able to sign in once the
academy approves it." Wait for the academy, or contact it.

If the academy doesn't approve you, you get an email saying so. You can sign
up again later with the same account and password, and the academy decides
again.

## If the academy uses invitations

Some academies only accept students with an invitation link. Without one,
the form shows **An invitation is required**. Open the link the academy sent
you and sign up from there. If the link no longer works you see
**This invitation cannot be used**: ask the academy for a new one.

> [!NOTE]
> If you are blocked by an academy, you can't sign in to it. Contact the
> academy directly.

Still stuck? See [A learner can't join the academy](doc:DOC-TRB-014) or
[How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003).
