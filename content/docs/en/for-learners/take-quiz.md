Read the quiz page before you start: it tells you exactly how this quiz
works. The academy sets the rules, so they can differ from one quiz to the
next.

## Open and start the quiz

1. In My Learn, open **Assessments**. The **Quizzes** tab lists the quizzes from all your courses, with your attempts and due dates. You can also open a quiz from the course outline.

   ![The Assessments page with the Quizzes tab](shot:for-learners/assessments-quizzes)

2. Open the quiz and read the intro: **Questions**, **Time limit**, **Attempts**, **Passing score**, **Which attempt counts**, and any **Opens**, **Due** or **Closes** dates.

   ![A quiz intro page showing the number of questions, attempts and passing score](shot:for-learners/quiz-intro)

3. If the page shows **What is recorded during this quiz**, read it and tick **I understand what is recorded during this quiz**. Then press **Start Quiz** (or **Resume Quiz** if you already started).

   ![The quiz intro page with the Start Quiz button highlighted](shot:for-learners/quiz-start)

## While you answer

- **The timer.** On a timed quiz the page says: "The clock starts when you
  press Start and keeps running even if you close this page. When it reaches
  zero, the answers you have saved are submitted automatically." The header
  shows **Time left**.
- **Saving.** Your answers save as you go: the header shows **Saved just
  now**. If it shows **Offline — will retry**, check your connection.
- **Flag** a question to come back to it; the question list shows which ones
  are answered.
- **Essay questions** are graded by a person, so your final result can come
  later.

## Full screen

Some quizzes open in full screen. If you leave full screen, the questions
are hidden until you choose **Enter full screen**; your answers and the timer
are kept. If your browser can't use full screen (common on phones), you see
**Full screen isn't available in this browser** and you can still take the
quiz. Your instructor sees that full screen wasn't available.

## What is recorded, and what never is

If the academy records quiz events, the intro lists them before you start:
switching to another tab or window, leaving full screen, copying, pasting and
printing on the quiz page, plus a connection check. Depending on the quiz you
are warned when an event is recorded, and some quizzes are submitted
automatically after a set number of events: the intro tells you if so.

Atlas never uses your camera or microphone, never records your screen or
what you type, and sees nothing outside the quiz page.

## Submit

Choose **Submit Attempt** and confirm in **Submit this attempt?**. If some
questions are still unanswered, the dialog says which ones: unanswered
questions count as incorrect. After submitting you can't change your
answers. Your grades appear in **Assessments**.

Problems during a quiz? See [Quiz timer, autosave and full-screen problems](doc:DOC-TRB-011)
and the [Quizzes and exams FAQ](doc:DOC-FAQ-006).
