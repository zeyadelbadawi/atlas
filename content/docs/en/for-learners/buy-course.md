You pay the academy directly, outside Atlas, with one of the methods the
academy accepts: bank transfer, e-wallet (such as Vodafone Cash) or
InstaPay. Then you upload proof of the payment, and the academy reviews it.

## Before you start

- Sign in on the academy's website. Signed out, the course page shows
  **Sign in to enroll**.
- Have your bank, wallet or InstaPay app ready.

## Steps

1. Open the course on the academy's website and choose **Buy this course**.

   ![A paid course page with the Buy this course button highlighted](shot:for-learners/paid-course-buy)

2. On **Checkout**, check the **Order summary**. Under **Payment method**, choose how you'll pay, then choose **Continue**.

   ![The checkout page with the payment methods and the Continue button highlighted](shot:for-learners/checkout-method)

3. Under **Where to send the money**, read the academy's details and the amount next to **Send exactly**. Send that exact amount from your app.

   ![The academy's payment details and the exact amount to send](shot:for-learners/checkout-instructions)

4. Under **Proof of payment**, attach a screenshot or receipt of the transfer ("PNG, JPG or PDF, up to 10 MB."). If you have one, add the **Transfer reference (optional)** from your bank or wallet.

   ![The proof upload field on the checkout page](shot:for-learners/checkout-proof)

5. Choose **Submit for review**.

   ![The checkout page with the Submit for review button highlighted](shot:for-learners/checkout-submit)

## What happens next

You see **Payment submitted**: "Your payment is under review. You'll be
enrolled once it's approved." You get an email when the academy has reviewed
it. Follow it any time in My Learn under **Payments**
([Your purchases and payments](doc:DOC-COM-011)).

How long the review takes depends on the academy.

## Good to know

- **The price is fixed when you start.** The amount on your order doesn't
  change if the academy changes the price later.
- **Don't pay twice.** If you already sent a payment, checkout shows
  **Your payment is under review**. Wait for the academy's decision.
- **Orders expire.** You have 30 minutes to choose a method, then 72 hours to
  send your proof. If the order expires, start again from the course page.
- **Not available for purchase yet** means the academy hasn't finished
  setting up payments. Try again later or contact the academy.
- **You already own this course** means you are already enrolled: choose
  **Go to course**.
- **You can't buy courses here yet** means the academy didn't approve your
  registration. You can apply again
  ([Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030)).
- **Waiting for the academy to approve you?** You can still buy. You get
  into the course once the academy has approved both your payment and your
  membership.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't take card payments or coupon codes for these courses, and it
> doesn't move the money: you pay the academy directly, and the academy checks
> that your transfer arrived.

Payment pending for a long time or rejected? See
[Payment proof pending or rejected](doc:DOC-TRB-008).
