You can review a course you are enrolled in, once per course. Every review
is checked by the academy before it appears on the course page.

## Steps

1. Open the course page on the academy's website while signed in. Scroll to **Write a review**. (After you finish a course, **Rate this course** takes you there too.)

   ![The Write a review section on a course page](shot:for-learners/review-form)

2. Under **Your rating**, choose the number of stars. Optionally write in **Your review (optional)**.

   ![The review form with the star rating highlighted](shot:for-learners/review-rating)

3. Press **Submit review**.

   ![The review form with the Submit review button highlighted](shot:for-learners/review-submit)

## What happens next

You see "Thanks! Your review is awaiting moderation before it appears
publicly." and your review is marked **Awaiting review**. When the academy
approves it, it changes to **Published**. If the academy doesn't approve it,
it shows **Not approved**.

## Edit or delete your review

Your review stays in the same section of the course page under
**Your review**. Choose **Edit** to change it and **Update review** to save,
or **Delete** to remove it. An edited review goes back to
**Awaiting review** until the academy approves it again.

> [!NOTE]
> If you choose **Submit review** without stars, the form asks you to choose
> a star rating first.
