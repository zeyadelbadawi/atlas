## What you need

- An up-to-date web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Keep it updated to the latest version.
- JavaScript turned on. The dashboard and the learner area don't work without it.
- Cookies allowed for the Atlas site and your academy's site. Atlas uses them to keep you signed in and, for learners, to recognize each device.

There is no Atlas app to install. You and your learners use Atlas in the browser, and the screens adapt to phone size.

## Each browser is a learning device

Atlas recognizes a learner's device by a cookie it stores in that browser. It does not fingerprint the device.

- Each browser on each phone or computer counts as a separate device. Chrome and Safari on the same phone are two devices.
- A private or incognito window, or clearing the browser's cookies, makes the browser look new, so it takes a new device slot.
- Each academy allows a set number of devices, and limits how many learning sessions can run at the same time. Learners see their own numbers on the **Devices** page in **My Learn**, and can remove a device they no longer use.

![The Devices page in My Learn with registered devices and the active session](shot:reference/learner-devices)

Reaching the device limit never stops a learner from signing in. It only stops lessons from opening on the new browser until they remove a device. See ["Device limit reached"](doc:DOC-TRB-003) and [Manage your devices](doc:DOC-MED-010).

## Quizzes in full screen

Some quizzes open in full screen. Safari on iPhone can't do that. The learner sees "Full screen isn't available in this browser", can still take the quiz, and the instructor sees that full screen wasn't available. See [Take a quiz or exam](doc:DOC-LRN-033).

## Watching lessons

- Videos play inside the page. They can't be popped out into a floating window or cast to another screen, and full screen keeps the watermark.
- Videos need an internet connection. They are never saved on the device for offline watching.

> [!TIP]
> If something looks broken, update your browser, allow cookies for the site, turn off extensions that block scripts, and try again. See [A lesson won't open or play](doc:DOC-TRB-004).
