## 20 October 2026: the Atlas Help Center opens

**New.** Step-by-step guides for running your academy on Atlas, in English and Arabic, with a screenshot for each step. Start at [Getting started with Atlas](doc:DOC-START-001).

On that date, Atlas offers:

- **Your academy website** on its own Atlas subdomain, with your own domain if you connect one, and pages in English and Arabic. You choose which language opens at the main address. See [Academy site URLs](doc:DOC-REF-004).
- **Courses** with YouTube and link lessons, lesson files, quizzes with time limits and attempt settings, assignments and certificates that anyone can verify. See [Courses overview](doc:DOC-LRN-001).
- **Quiz integrity settings** that record events such as leaving full screen or switching tabs for the reviewer, and per-learner overrides for extra time or attempts. See [Exam integrity and full-screen exams](doc:DOC-LRN-016).
- **Learner payments** by bank transfer, mobile wallet or InstaPay, which the organization owner approves. See [How selling courses works](doc:DOC-COM-001).
- **Content protection** with a visible watermark on every video and a device limit for each learner. See [Content protection: what Atlas does and does not do](doc:DOC-MED-001).
- **Your data** on the learner's profile, where learners can request a copy of their data or the deletion of their account. See [What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

What Atlas does not offer yet is listed in [Integrations](doc:DOC-INT-001) and the [FAQ](doc:DOC-FAQ-001).
