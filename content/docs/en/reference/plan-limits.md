The numbers for each plan come from the live plan catalog and can change, so this page does not repeat them. See the current limits for every plan in the **Compare plan limits** table on the [pricing page](site:/pricing). Every plan includes the same features; plans differ only in these limits.

## What each limit counts

| Limit | What counts toward it |
|---|---|
| **Academies** | The academies in your organization. An archived academy no longer counts. |
| **Students** | Learners who hold a current enrollment in at least one course in your academies. A learner enrolled in several courses counts once. |
| **Instructors** | People with the instructor role in your academies. Owners and managers do not count here. |
| **Courses** | Every course in your academies, published or draft. Archived courses do not count. |
| **General storage** | The files you upload to Atlas: images, documents and attachments. |
| Emails to students | Emails you send to learners from **Messages**, counted per academy each month. Your remaining allowance shows in **Messages**. |

All limits except emails to students are shared by the whole organization, across all its academies.

## See your usage

The **Usage** page shows each limit with what you use now, and marks a limit you have reached with **Limit reached**.

![The Usage page with academies, students, instructors, courses and storage](shot:reference/usage-page)

Atlas also sends an email when you get close to a limit and again when you reach it. See [The Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006).

## What happens at a limit

- Nothing is deleted. Your courses, learners and files stay as they are.
- Adding one more of that item is refused with the message "You've reached your plan's limit for this. Upgrade your plan to continue."
- To add more, move to a bigger plan, or remove items you no longer need (for example, archive an academy or delete unused files). See [You've reached a plan limit](doc:DOC-TRB-010).

## When your limits change

- During the trial ({trialDuration}), you get the full limits of the plan you picked.
- When you upgrade, the new limits start once your payment is approved. See [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).
- When a trial or subscription is no longer active, you can't add anything until you subscribe again.
