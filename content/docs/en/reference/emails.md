Emails are written in the language each person chooses under **Email language** in their email preferences. Many events also appear in the in-app notifications.

## Account and security emails (everyone)

Always sent. You can't turn these off.

- Email address verification, and sign-in codes when you sign in from a new device.
- Password reset links, and a confirmation after a password reset or change.
- Two-factor authentication turned on or off, or a sign-in method linked or unlinked.
- Your account joined a new academy.
- An invitation to join an academy as a team member or a learner.
- Codes and updates for a privacy request (a copy of your data, or account deletion).

## Emails to learners

| Email | Can it be turned off? |
|---|---|
| Your payment proof was received | No |
| Your payment was approved, or rejected | No |
| You were enrolled in a course (or enrolled yourself), your enrollment was removed, or its end date changed | No |
| Your request to join was approved or rejected; your access was blocked or unblocked | No |
| You were added to the academy | No |
| A certificate was issued or revoked | No |
| A device was removed from your account | No |
| A quiz or assignment was graded | Yes |
| A quiz was submitted automatically, or an attempt was voided | Yes |
| A quiz exception was given, became active or was removed | Yes |
| You completed a course | Yes |
| A message from your academy (**Messages**) | Yes |

Announcements appear in the learner's notifications, not by email.

## Reminders about your plan (organization owners)

Atlas reminds the organization owner before anything changes with their plan, so a slow bank transfer never takes the site offline by surprise. A reminder that would arrive too late to be true is not sent.

| When | Email | Can it be turned off? |
|---|---|---|
| Trial, 3 days before it ends (trials of 7 days or more) | Your trial ends in 3 days | No |
| Trial, 1 day before it ends | Your trial ends tomorrow | No |
| When the trial ends | Your trial has ended | No |
| 3, 14 and 45 days after the trial ended | Your site is still offline, Your courses are waiting, Still here whenever you're ready | Yes (**Account and courses**) |
| Yearly plan, 30 days before the period ends | Your yearly plan renews in 30 days | No |
| 7 days, then 1 day, before the period ends | Your plan renews soon; Your plan renews tomorrow | No |
| Payment late: grace period starts, and 1 day before it ends | Payment is late — your site is still online; Your site goes offline tomorrow | No |
| When the subscription expires or ends | Your subscription has expired; Your subscription has ended | No |
| 7 and 30 days after it expired | Your site is still offline; Your academy is still here | Yes (**Account and courses**) |
| Before a renewal at a new price | Your plan changes at renewal; for Founding Academies, Your next renewal is at the catalog price | No |

**Setup tips.** In the first days after you sign up, Atlas sends a few short tips: if you haven't created your academy after a day, if your site isn't published after two days, and if payments or your first course aren't set up after four days. You also get one when your first payment proof arrives. These follow your **Account and courses** preference.

## Which emails you can turn off

Open your email preferences and switch a category off, or choose a daily digest where offered. Security alerts, receipts and account changes stay on: the app marks them **Always on**. See [Email preferences and unsubscribing](doc:DOC-MSG-002).

## Is this email really from Atlas?

- Never send your password or a sign-in code to anyone, including someone who says they work for Atlas or your academy.
- Type a sign-in code only on the sign-in page you opened yourself.
- If you get an email about a sign-in, password change or new academy that wasn't you, reset your password straight away. See [Reset or change your password](doc:DOC-IAM-003).

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas sends email and in-app notifications only. It does not send SMS or WhatsApp messages.
