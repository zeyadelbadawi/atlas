Everything you do as a learner happens on your academy's website, in the **My Learn**
area. Your teacher's academy sets the rules: who can join, the course prices, how you
pay and how many devices you can use.

![The Overview page of My Learn](shot:roles/learner-overview)

## Join an academy

Sign up on the academy website, or join with an Atlas account you already use at
another academy. See [Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030) and
[How learner accounts work across academies](doc:DOC-ORG-011).

## Enrol in a free course or buy a paid one

- Free course: [Enroll in a free course](doc:DOC-LRN-032).
- Paid course: you pay the academy directly by bank transfer, a mobile wallet or
  InstaPay, then upload a proof. The academy checks it and opens the course for you.
  See [Buy a course: pay and upload your proof](doc:DOC-COM-010) and
  [Your purchases and payments](doc:DOC-COM-011).

## Watch lessons and keep track

**My Courses** lists your courses with your progress. Open one to continue where you
stopped. See [Find your way around My Learn](doc:DOC-LRN-031).

## Quizzes, exams and assignments

**Assessments** gathers your quizzes and assignments. See
[Take a quiz or exam](doc:DOC-LRN-033) and [Submit an assignment](doc:DOC-LRN-034).

## Certificates

When you finish a course that gives a certificate, it appears in **Certificates**, where
you can download and share it. See [Download and share your certificate](doc:DOC-LRN-035).

## Your devices

Your academy may limit how many devices you use, and lessons play on one device at a
time: if you open a lesson on another device, Atlas asks you to move playback there.
Videos show a visible code that belongs to your account. See
[Manage your devices](doc:DOC-MED-010).

## Your account, your data and getting help

- For help with courses, payments or your account, use **Contact your academy** in My Learn.
  Learners get help from their academy; they can't open Atlas support tickets.
- The academy's owner and managers can see your mobile number, and so can Atlas
  platform administrators.
- See [What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

> [!LIMITATION]
> You can't delete your learner account yourself. Ask your academy for help.
