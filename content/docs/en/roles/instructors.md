The academy owner adds you as an **Instructor**, and the owner or a manager assigns you
to courses. New here? Start with the [Quick start for instructors](doc:DOC-START-007).

## What instructors can do

On the courses you are assigned to:

- Build the curriculum: sections and lessons (YouTube videos or links to other sites).
  See [Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005).
- Create and edit quizzes and assignments. See [Create a quiz](doc:DOC-LRN-014) and
  [Assignments](doc:DOC-LRN-019).
- Review quiz attempts, grade written answers and give a learner a quiz exception.
  See [Review attempts and grade](doc:DOC-LRN-017) and [Quiz exceptions](doc:DOC-LRN-018).
- Review assignment submissions and grade them.
- See the course's students and their progress, read-only.
- Open a support ticket with Atlas from **Support**.

## What instructors can't do

- See or work on courses they aren't assigned to.
- Change a course's details, price or visibility, publish it, or manage certificates.
- Edit the website, branding or academy settings, or open the media library.
- Upload the file of a **File** lesson. The lesson dialog says **Only the academy owner or a manager can upload or replace the lesson file.**
- See payments, orders or the Atlas subscription.
- Add, approve, block or enrol learners. When you open a student, Atlas reminds you:
  **You are viewing this student through the courses you teach. Managing their
  membership and enrollments is done by the academy's owner or a manager.**

## Getting assigned to a course

Your course list is empty until the owner or a manager assigns you. Each instructor
counts toward your academy plan's instructor limit, so the owner may need room on the
plan to add you.

## Your Teaching Dashboard

**Teaching Dashboard** gives an overview of the courses you teach and what needs your
attention. **My Courses** lists every course you are assigned to. See
[Your teaching dashboard](doc:DOC-LRN-024).

## Grading, feedback and exam reviews

Open a course and its **Assessments** to find its quizzes and assignments, their
attempts and submissions.
