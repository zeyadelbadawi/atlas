The owner adds you as a **Manager** of one or more academies. You sign in on the
[Atlas dashboard](app:/auth/sign-in), and you only see the academies you were added to.
If you manage more than one, switch between them with the academy switcher
([Switch between academies](doc:DOC-ACD-008)).

## What a manager can do

| Area | You can |
|---|---|
| Courses | Create, edit, publish and archive courses; build lessons, quizzes and assignments; grade; assign instructors who are already on the team to a course ([Assign instructors](doc:DOC-LRN-012)) |
| Certificates | Design certificate templates and issue or revoke certificates |
| Website | Edit pages, theme, navigation and SEO, publish the website, read website messages |
| Academy | Edit the academy profile, settings and visual identity, connect your own domain |
| Learners | See the student list and details, approve or reject pending learners, block and unblock, enrol a learner in a course, set or end course access, create invite links |
| Communication | Post announcements |
| Reports | Read the academy reports, including sharing signals |

## What only the owner can do

- Add anyone: managers, instructors or students. The **Members** page shows **Only the
  account owner can add people.**
- Remove a staff member.
- Set up payment methods, review learners' payments and see course orders. These pages
  aren't in your menu; if you open one from a link, Atlas shows **You do not have access**.
- Anything about the Atlas subscription: plans, billing, usage.
- Change the registration policy (**Only the account owner can change the registration
  policy.**), content protection or device limits.
- Create, delete or change the Atlas address of an academy.
- Send Academy Messages and read the activity log.

## Your first 30 minutes

1. Accept your invitation and sign in ([Accept an invitation](doc:DOC-IAM-012)).
2. Open **Courses** to see the academy's courses, or create one ([Create a course](doc:DOC-LRN-002)).
3. Open **Website** to review the site before it goes live ([Website builder overview](doc:DOC-WEB-001)).
4. Open **Members** and the **Students** tab to see who has joined ([Manage learners](doc:DOC-TASK-005)).

![The You do not have access page a manager sees when opening Course orders](shot:roles/manager-orders)

> [!NOTE]
> If the owner removes you from an academy, you lose access to it right away.
