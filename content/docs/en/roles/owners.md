When you sign up for Atlas you become the **owner** of your organization and of every
academy you create in it. You sign up and sign in on the Atlas dashboard at
 `dashboard.atlass.dpdns.org`. The team list shows you with the role **Owner**.

## Your first steps

1. [Create your account and start a trial](doc:DOC-START-002).
2. [Complete the setup steps](doc:DOC-START-003) and [create your academy](doc:DOC-START-004).
3. Work through the [launch checklist](doc:DOC-START-005): website, first course,
   payment methods and who can join.
4. Add the people who help you: [Add a manager or instructor](doc:DOC-ORG-002).

## Things only you can do

| Area | What only the owner can do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas subscription | Start a trial, pay for a plan, renew or change plan, see billing and usage | [Your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-001) |
| Money from learners | Set up payment methods, review payment proofs, see course orders | [Review payment proofs](doc:DOC-COM-003) |
| Team | Add managers, instructors and students; remove staff | [Add a manager or instructor](doc:DOC-ORG-002), [Add a student yourself](doc:DOC-ORG-004) |
| Academies | Create a new academy, change its Atlas address, delete it | [Create another academy](doc:DOC-ACD-007), [Delete an academy](doc:DOC-ACD-010) |
| Who can join | Change the registration policy | [Choose who can join](doc:DOC-ORG-005) |
| Protection | Change content protection and learner device limits | [Content protection settings](doc:DOC-MED-004) |
| Communication | Send Academy Messages to your learners | [Message your learners](doc:DOC-MSG-003) |
| Records | Read the academy activity log | [Academy activity log](doc:DOC-ANL-004) |

## Things you share with managers

Managers can do the day-to-day work with you: courses and their content, the website,
branding and academy settings, your own domain, announcements, and the learner list
(approving, blocking and enrolling learners and creating invite links). See the
[Guide for academy managers](doc:DOC-ROLE-002).

## Limits of the owner role

- Ownership can't be handed to someone else, and there is only one owner per organization.
- The organization name can't be changed yet.
- Roles can't be changed in place. To give someone another role, remove them and add
  them again with the new role.
- Payment pages and billing can't be delegated: managers never see them.

See the full comparison in [Roles and permissions](doc:DOC-ROLE-005).
