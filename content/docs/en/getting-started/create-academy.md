Only the organization owner can create academies. You need an active trial or paid
plan, and your plan must allow one more academy.

## Before you start

- Your first academy is created in the **Academy** step of
  [the setup steps](doc:DOC-START-003), with the same form described below.
- For another academy later, check your plan's academy limit on the
  [pricing page](site:/pricing). If you've reached it, the page shows **Academy limit
  reached** with **View plans**.

## Fill in the academy form

1. In the sidebar, open **New Academy**.

   ![The New Academy page with the academy form](shot:getting-started/new-academy-page)

2. Type the **Academy name**, up to 100 characters. It must not already be used by
   another academy on Atlas.

   ![The Academy name field filled in](shot:getting-started/academy-name)

3. Check the **Atlas address**. Atlas suggests one from the name; you can edit it.
   Use 3 to 50 lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens, and wait until it says
   **Available**. **Already taken** or **This address is reserved** means you need
   another address.

   ![The Atlas address field showing that the address is available](shot:getting-started/academy-address)

4. Pick a **Theme** for your website. You can change it any time from Website Settings.

   ![The theme choices on the academy form](shot:getting-started/academy-theme)

5. Optional: open **Logo, colours & favicon (optional)** to add your logo, colours
   and the small icon shown in browser tabs. You can also do this later in Visual Identity.

   ![The logo, colours and favicon section opened](shot:getting-started/academy-brand)

6. Under **How would you like to start?**, choose **Start with a ready-made website**
   (pages and starting content in English and Arabic, which you then edit) or
   **Start from a blank page** (the pages and structure, with nothing filled in).

   ![The two choices for how to start the website](shot:getting-started/academy-start-mode)

7. Under **Payment methods**, choose **Set up later**, or **Accept payments now** and
   turn on at least one method: bank transfer, InstaPay or a mobile wallet. These are
   the ways learners will pay you for paid courses.

   ![The payment methods choices on the academy form](shot:getting-started/academy-payment-methods)

8. Choose **Start provisioning** (in the setup steps the button is **Create academy**).
   Keep the page open until it says **Your Academy is ready**, then choose **Continue setup**.

   ![The Start provisioning button at the end of the form](shot:getting-started/academy-submit)

## What Atlas sets up for you

Your academy gets its own dashboard, its Atlas address and a website built from the
theme and starting option you picked, with your logo and colours if you added them.
The website starts unpublished: visitors see a Coming Soon page until you publish it
(see [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008)).

## What you can change later

- Name, logo, colours, theme and website content: any time, by you or a manager.
- The Atlas address: only the owner, once every 60 days. Your website moves to the new address and the old one redirects to it. See [Your academy web address](doc:DOC-ACD-005).
- Payment methods: from **Payment methods** in the academy sidebar. See
  [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).
- Your own domain: after the academy is ready, from the website settings.

> [!NOTE]
> Paid courses can't be bought until you turn on at least one payment method.

> [!WARNING]
> If the browser tab is closed before setup finishes, the favicon you chose may not
> be saved. Add it again later in Visual Identity.

## If setup stops or fails

If you open the same address in a second tab, Atlas says **This address is already
being set up from another tab or an earlier attempt.** Choose **Follow that setup**
instead of starting again. If setup can't be completed, the page offers **Retry**.
See [Academy creation status, retries and failures](doc:DOC-ACD-011).
