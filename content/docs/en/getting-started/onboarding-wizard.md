Only the organization owner sees these setup steps. Managers and instructors go
straight to the dashboard.

## What the wizard asks for

| Step | Tag | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | Required first | Your trial or paid plan is active. Shown only while you have no active plan. |
| **Academy** | Required | Your academy and its website have been created. |
| **Website** | Required | Your academy website is published. |
| **First course** | Recommended | Your academy has at least one course (a draft is enough). |
| **Summary** | | You choose **Finish**. |

The rail on the side shows where you are ("Step 2 of 4"), and each step is marked
**Done**, **In progress**, **To do** or **Locked**. A logo for your academy is also
recommended; you can add it in the academy form or later in Visual Identity.

## If Atlas asks you to choose a plan first

If you started a trial when you signed up, this step is skipped. Otherwise the
**Plan** step comes first and the other steps stay locked until a plan is active:

- If a trial is available, pick a plan and choose **Start trial**.
- If not (for example, your email already had a trial), choose **Choose** followed by
  the plan's name, and pay for it. The step then shows **Payment submitted — waiting
  for confirmation** and updates on its own once Atlas confirms the payment. See
  [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).

## Go through the steps

1. Sign in. Atlas opens **Set up your academy** with the steps listed on the side.

   ![The setup screen with the progress rail and the Academy step](shot:getting-started/onboarding-academy)

2. In the **Academy** step, fill in the academy form (name, Atlas address, theme and
   how you want to start) and choose **Create academy**. Keep the page open while
   Atlas builds your academy; it updates on its own. The details of each field are in
   [Create your academy](doc:DOC-START-004).

   ![The academy form with name, Atlas address and theme](shot:getting-started/academy-form)

3. In the **Website** step, choose **Preview website** to look at it first, then
   **Publish website**. The step reads **Your website is live** when it's done.
   **Customize website** opens the website builder if you want to change it first.

   ![The Website step after the website is published](shot:getting-started/onboarding-website)

4. In the **First course** step, type a **Course Title** and choose **Create Course**
   to save a draft, or choose **Skip for now**. After you create it, **Open course builder** takes you to
   its lessons.

   ![The First course step](shot:getting-started/onboarding-course)

5. On the **Summary**, check the list of required and recommended steps and choose
   **Finish**. Finish becomes available once every required step is done.

   ![The setup summary with the Finish button](shot:getting-started/onboarding-summary)

## Leave and come back later

**Finish for now** is on every step. It closes the setup screens and takes you to your
dashboard. The **Set up your academy** card on the dashboard lists what is still open;
choose **Continue setup** to go back to the next open step. Your progress is saved on
Atlas, so you can continue on any device.

## What happens next

Your academy is created and its website is live. Before your first learner joins,
work through the [launch checklist](doc:DOC-START-005): add lessons to your course
and publish it, turn on payment methods if the course is paid, and decide who can join.

> [!NOTE]
> The animation you see while the academy is built is a presentation of what is being
> set up. Keep the page open until it says your academy is set up.

> [!WARNING]
> If your trial ends before you finish setup, the **Plan** step appears again so you
> can choose a plan. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).
