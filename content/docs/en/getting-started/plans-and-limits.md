Every plan gives you the same Atlas features. What changes from one plan to another
is the size of your limits. The current plans, prices and limit values are on the
[pricing page](site:/pricing) and in the [Plan limits reference](doc:DOC-REF-001).

## What counts toward each limit

| Limit | What is counted |
|---|---|
| **Academies** | Academies in your organization that haven't been deleted. |
| **Courses** | Courses in those academies that aren't archived, drafts included. |
| **Instructors** | Different people who are active instructors in at least one of your academies. Someone who teaches in two academies counts once. |
| **Students** | Different learners who have course access (an enrolment that hasn't been revoked or refunded) in one of your academies. A learner in several courses counts once. |
| **General storage** | The files you store on Atlas, such as images and PDFs. |

You, the owner, and your managers don't count toward any limit.

## What happens when you reach a limit

- Atlas checks a limit when you add something: a new academy, course or instructor,
  or a learner getting access to a course.
- At the limit, the new item is refused with a message such as **You've reached your
  plan's limit for this. Upgrade your plan to continue.**
- Nothing you already have is removed or switched off. Existing courses, learners and
  instructors keep working.
- When the student limit is full, a learner who has no course access yet can't enrol
  or buy a course; they see that the academy isn't accepting new enrolments right now.

## Where to see your usage

The owner opens **Usage** in the sidebar to see current usage against each limit.
See [The Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006).

## When your limits change

Your trial uses the limits of the plan you chose to try. When you move to another
plan, the new limits apply once your payment is approved. If you move to a smaller
plan, items above the new limit stay, but you can't add more until you are under it.
See [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).

> [!LIMITATION]
> Videos can't be stored on Atlas yet, so no video storage is used: video lessons use
> YouTube links or links to other sites.
