This page is for the person who will own the academy: a teacher, a centre owner or
whoever pays for Atlas. Learners don't use this form; they sign up on their academy's
own website (see [Quick start for learners](doc:DOC-START-008)).

## Before you start

- An email address you can open now. Temporary or disposable addresses are refused.
- A mobile number. One number can belong to only one Atlas account.
- A name for your organization: the school, company or brand your academies belong to.
  It must not already be used on Atlas, and it can't be changed after sign-up, so
  choose carefully.

## Fill in the sign-up form

1. Open the [Atlas sign-up page](app:/auth/register) on `dashboard.atlass.dpdns.org`. The page is titled **Create Account**.

   ![The Create Account page on Atlas](shot:getting-started/sign-up-page)

2. Enter your **Full Name** and your **Email**.

   ![The Full Name and Email fields on the sign-up form](shot:getting-started/sign-up-name-email)

3. Under **Mobile number**, check the country code and type your number.

   ![The Mobile number field with its country code](shot:getting-started/sign-up-phone)

4. Choose a **Password** of at least 8 characters and type it again under **Confirm Password**.

   ![The Password and Confirm Password fields](shot:getting-started/sign-up-password)

5. Type your **Organization name**.

   ![The Organization name field](shot:getting-started/sign-up-organization)

6. Under **Choose a plan to try**, select the plan you want. Each card shows the
   length of the trial and **No card required**.

   ![The plan cards to choose a trial from](shot:getting-started/sign-up-plan)

7. Tick the box to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then choose **Create Account**.

   ![The terms checkbox and the Create Account button](shot:getting-started/sign-up-submit)

8. Atlas shows **Next, sign in** and opens the sign-in page with your email filled in.
   Type your password and choose **Sign In**. If Atlas asks for a code, open the email
   from Atlas with your 6-digit code, type it under **Check your email** and continue.
   Tick **Remember this device** if you don't want to be asked again on this browser for a while.

   ![The Atlas sign-in page](shot:getting-started/sign-in)

## What the trial includes

- The trial lasts {trialDuration} on the plan you picked, with everything that plan includes.
- No card is needed, and Atlas never charges you automatically. When the trial ends,
  you decide whether to pay for a plan. See [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002).
- One trial per person. If your email address already had a trial, the account and
  organization are still created, and you choose a plan to pay for in the first setup step.
- If trials are switched off, the form says **You'll choose a plan after signing in.**

## What happens next

After you sign in, Atlas opens **Set up your academy**, a short series of steps for
your academy, website and first course. See
[Complete the onboarding wizard](doc:DOC-START-003).

> [!NOTE]
> Atlas gives the same answer whether or not your email already has an account. If it
> does, sign in with that account instead: Atlas emails you how to continue.

> [!LIMITATION]
> The organization name can't be changed after sign-up yet. If the name is already
> used on Atlas, the form says **This organization name isn't available. Please try
> another.**

## If something goes wrong

- **No code arrived**: check spam, then use **Send a new code**. See
  [I didn't get an email](doc:DOC-TRB-009).
- **Email refused**: use a permanent address you can receive mail at.
- **Phone number already used**: the number belongs to another account. Enter a
  different number, or contact support if it's yours.
- More help with codes: [Sign-in codes by email and remembered browsers](doc:DOC-IAM-005).
