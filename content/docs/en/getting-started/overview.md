Atlas gives a teacher or an education business its own online academy: a website
with your name and brand, courses with lessons and quizzes, and a place where your
learners sign up, pay you and study.

## How Atlas is organised

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Your account on Atlas: the school, company or brand your academies belong to. It holds your plan and subscription. You create it when you sign up. |
| **Academy** | One academy inside your organization. It has its own Atlas address, website, courses, payment methods, team and learners. Your plan decides how many academies you can have. |
| **Team** | The people who help you run an academy: managers and instructors. You add them; they sign in on the Atlas dashboard. |
| **Learners** | Your students. They sign up and sign in on your academy's own website, not on the Atlas dashboard. One Atlas account can join several academies, and each academy's courses, progress and certificates stay separate. |

## Your first steps, in order

1. [Create your account and start a trial](doc:DOC-START-002). One form creates your account and your organization.
2. [Complete the setup steps](doc:DOC-START-003). Atlas walks you through your academy, your website and a first course.
3. [Create your academy](doc:DOC-START-004): its name, Atlas address, theme and starting content.
4. [Follow the launch checklist](doc:DOC-START-005): publish your website and a course, turn on payment methods and decide who can join.

## Who does what

The person who signs up is the **owner** of the organization and of every academy in
it. Only the owner pays for Atlas, sees the money pages, adds people and creates or
deletes academies. Managers run courses, the website and the learner list.
Instructors teach the courses they are assigned to. See
[Roles and permissions](doc:DOC-ROLE-005).

## Plans and the trial

Your trial lasts {trialDuration} and needs no card. Plans differ in their limits,
such as how many academies, courses, instructors and learners you can have. See
[Plans, limits and what counts toward them](doc:DOC-START-006) and the
[pricing page](site:/pricing).

## What Atlas does not offer yet

> [!LIMITATION]
> Video lessons use YouTube links or links to other sites; videos are not stored on
> Atlas yet. Lessons that are only text, live sessions, an academy blog and a forum
> are not available. Learners pay you by bank transfer, mobile wallet or InstaPay and
> upload a proof; there is no card payment.
