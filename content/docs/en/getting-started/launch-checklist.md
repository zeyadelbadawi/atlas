Use this checklist after the [setup steps](doc:DOC-START-003). The owner can do
every stage; managers can help with the website and courses, but payment methods and
the registration policy are owner-only.

## The checklist

1. **Publish your website.** In the academy sidebar, open **Website**. Work through
   the **Launch checklist** on that page, replace the sample content with your own,
   then choose **Publish** and confirm **Publish** in **Publish this website?**. Until
   then, visitors see a Coming Soon page. Details:
   [Replace sample content](doc:DOC-WEB-007) and [Publish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).

   ![The Website overview with its launch checklist](shot:getting-started/launch-website)

2. **Create your first course.** Open **Courses** and choose **Create Course**. The
   guided wizard takes you through the basics, details, image, curriculum and price.
   Add at least one lesson: a [YouTube video](doc:DOC-LRN-006) or a
   [link to another site](doc:DOC-LRN-007). See [Create a course](doc:DOC-LRN-002).

   ![The Courses page with the Create Course button](shot:getting-started/launch-courses)

3. **Publish the course.** On the wizard's **Publish** step, choose **Publish course**
   and confirm. Learners can then find it according to its visibility. See
   [Publish your course](doc:DOC-LRN-011).

   ![The Publish step of the course wizard](shot:getting-started/launch-course-publish)

4. **Turn on payment methods (paid courses only).** In the academy sidebar, open
   **Payment methods** and set up bank transfer, InstaPay or a mobile wallet. See
   [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).

   ![The Payment methods page](shot:getting-started/launch-payment-methods)

5. **Choose who can join.** Open **Academy Settings** and, under **Registration**,
   choose a **Registration policy**: **Open**, **Approval required** or **Invite only**.
   Then choose **Save policy**. See [Choose who can join](doc:DOC-ORG-005).

   ![The Registration policy card in Academy Settings](shot:getting-started/launch-registration)

6. **Test it as a learner.** Open your academy's address in a private browser window,
   choose **Sign up** and create a test learner account with another email address.
   Enrol in a free course, or buy a paid one and approve the payment yourself, then
   open a lesson. See [Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030).

   ![The sign-up page of an academy website](shot:getting-started/launch-test-sign-up)

## Who can do each stage

| Stage | Owner | Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Publish the website | Yes | Yes |
| Create and publish a course | Yes | Yes |
| Payment methods | Yes | No |
| Registration policy | Yes | No (sees it, can't change it) |
| Approve payments | Yes | No |

## Common launch blockers

- **The site still shows Coming Soon**: the website isn't published, or your
  subscription has lapsed. See [My website shows "Coming soon"](doc:DOC-TRB-005).
- **A paid course can't be bought**: no payment method is turned on.
- **Learners wait after signing up**: the policy is **Approval required**. Approve
  them on the **Students** tab of **Members** ([Approve pending learners](doc:DOC-ORG-007)).
- **A course shows no content**: check that it has at least one lesson learners can open.

> [!TIP]
> Do the test on a phone too: most learners will open your academy there.
