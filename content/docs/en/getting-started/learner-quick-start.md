Each academy on Atlas has its own website. Everything you do as a learner, from
signing up to watching lessons, happens there.

## Use your academy's website

Open the academy address your teacher or centre gave you. If you try to sign in on
the Atlas dashboard instead, Atlas tells you **You are a student. Please sign in
through your academy's website.** and lists the academies linked to your account.

## Create your account

1. On the academy website, choose **Sign up**.
2. Fill in your name, email, mobile number and a password of at least 8 characters,
   accept the terms and choose **Create Account**.
3. Already learning at another Atlas academy? Under **Already have an Atlas account?**,
   choose **Join with it** and use the password you already have. The academy is added
   to your account; your courses, progress and certificates stay separate in each academy.

![The sign-up page of an academy website](shot:getting-started/launch-test-sign-up)

What happens next depends on the academy:

- You can sign in right away.
- Your request waits for the academy to approve it: **Your request to join … has been
  sent. You'll be able to sign in once the academy approves it.**
- The academy only accepts learners with an invite link. Ask the academy for one.

See [Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030).

## Sign in

Choose **Sign in** on the same academy website. If the academy asks for a code, check
your email for it.

## Get a course

- **Free course**: open it and enrol. See [Enroll in a free course](doc:DOC-LRN-032).
- **Paid course**: you pay the academy directly by bank transfer, mobile wallet such as
  Vodafone Cash, or InstaPay, then upload a photo or screenshot of the payment. The
  academy checks it and gives you access. See
  [Buy a course: pay and upload your proof](doc:DOC-COM-010).

## Find your courses in My Learn

Choose **My Learn**, then **My Courses**, and open a course to continue where you
stopped. See [Find your way around My Learn](doc:DOC-LRN-031).

![My Courses in a learner's My Learn area](shot:getting-started/learner-my-courses)

## If you can't get in

- The academy may limit how many devices you use. See [Manage your devices](doc:DOC-MED-010).
- Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the academy's sign-in page.
- For anything about your courses or payments, use **Contact your academy** in My
  Learn. Learners get help from their academy, not from Atlas support.

> [!NOTE]
> The academy's owner and managers can see the mobile number on your account.

> [!LIMITATION]
> You can't delete your learner account yourself. Ask your academy for help.
