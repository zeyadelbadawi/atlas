This page is for teachers and assistants who were added to an academy as an
**Instructor**. Only the academy owner can add you.

## Accept your invitation

Atlas sends you an email when the owner adds you.

- **New to Atlas?** The email has a link to set up your account. It opens **Set your
  password**: choose a password of at least 8 characters, type it again and choose **Set
  password**. Then sign in with it.
  The link lasts 72 hours. If it has expired, choose **Get a new link**, or ask the
  owner to add you again. See [Accept an invitation](doc:DOC-IAM-012).
- **Already have an Atlas account?** The email tells you that you were added. Sign in
  with your usual password; nothing changes in your account.

## Sign in on the Atlas dashboard

Instructors sign in on the [Atlas sign-in page](app:/auth/sign-in), not on the
academy website, which is for learners. If Atlas asks for a code, check your email
for it. See [Where to sign in](doc:DOC-IAM-002).

## Find your courses

Open **Teaching Dashboard** in the sidebar. It gives an overview of the courses you
teach, and **My Courses** lists them. The list stays empty until the owner or a
manager assigns you to a course.

![The Teaching Dashboard of an instructor](shot:getting-started/instructor-teaching-dashboard)

## Build lessons, quizzes and assignments

Open one of your courses and choose **Edit curriculum** to add sections and lessons.
Lessons can be a YouTube video or a link to another site. You can also create quizzes
and assignments for the courses you teach. See
[Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005), [Create a quiz](doc:DOC-LRN-014) and
[Assignments](doc:DOC-LRN-019).

![A course overview for an instructor with the Edit curriculum button](shot:getting-started/instructor-course-overview)

## Grade and give feedback

From your course, open its assessments to review quiz attempts, grade written
answers and review assignment submissions. See
[Review attempts and grade essay answers](doc:DOC-LRN-017).

## What instructors can't do

- Work on courses they aren't assigned to.
- Change a course's title, description, price or visibility, or publish it. The owner
  or a manager does that.
- Edit the website or branding, see payments, or add people to the academy.
- Change student details: you can see the students of your courses, read-only.
