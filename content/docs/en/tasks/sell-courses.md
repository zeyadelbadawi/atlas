Atlas doesn't take card payments and doesn't handle the money. Each learner sends the
payment straight to you, using the details you set up, and you confirm it in Atlas.
See [How selling courses works](doc:DOC-COM-001).

## Stages

1. **Set a price.** Open the course and its **Pricing** step in the course wizard, choose
   **Paid** and enter the price. The owner or a manager can do this. See
   [Set a course price](doc:DOC-LRN-003).

   ![The Pricing and access step of the course wizard](shot:tasks/sell-pricing)

2. **Turn on your payment methods.** In the academy sidebar, open **Payment methods** and
   set up bank transfer, InstaPay or a mobile wallet with the details learners should pay
   to. See [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).

   ![The Payment methods page with the methods you can set up](shot:tasks/sell-payment-methods)

3. **Learners pay and upload a proof.** A learner opens the paid course, picks one of
   your methods, pays you from their bank or wallet app, then uploads a screenshot or
   photo of the payment. Their purchase waits in **Purchases** in their My Learn area. See
   [Buy a course: pay and upload your proof](doc:DOC-COM-010).

   ![A learner's Purchases page in My Learn](shot:tasks/sell-learner-purchases)

4. **Review the proof and approve.** You get an email and a notification when a proof
   arrives. Open **Payments**, open the payment, check in your bank or wallet that the
   money arrived, then choose **Approve** or **Reject payment**. Approving gives the
   learner the course. See [Review payment proofs](doc:DOC-COM-003).

   ![A pending payment opened for review, with the Approve button](shot:tasks/sell-review-payment)

5. **Track orders.** Open **Course orders** to see every purchase and its status. See
   [Track course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).

   ![The Course orders list](shot:tasks/sell-orders)

## Who can do each stage

| Stage | Owner | Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Set a course price | Yes | Yes |
| Payment methods | Yes | No |
| Review payments, see orders | Yes | No |

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't check your bank or wallet: you confirm each payment yourself. There is no
> card payment and no automatic confirmation. If you refund a learner, you send the
> money back yourself, then record it with **Record refund…** on the payment. See [Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005).

> [!TIP]
> Check new payments often: learners wait for your approval before they can start.

If a learner says their payment is stuck, see
[Payment proof pending or rejected](doc:DOC-TRB-008).
