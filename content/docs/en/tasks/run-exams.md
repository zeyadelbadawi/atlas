The owner, managers and the course's instructors can do every stage. Instructors work
only on the courses they are assigned to.

## Stages

1. **Create the quiz.** Open the course's **Quizzes** page and choose **Add Quiz**. Add
   your questions. See [Create a quiz](doc:DOC-LRN-014).

   ![The Quizzes page of a course with the Add Quiz button](shot:tasks/exams-quizzes)

2. **Choose time, attempts and results.** In the quiz settings, pick a **Mode**:
   **Practice** or **Exam**. Exam sets a time limit, one question per page and hides the
   answers; you can change each setting, such as **Time limit (minutes)**, **Max
   attempts**, **Score to keep** and **Show the score**. See
   [Quiz settings](doc:DOC-LRN-015).

   ![The quiz editor with the quiz settings](shot:tasks/exams-quiz-settings)

3. **Turn on integrity options.** Under **Integrity**, choose an **Integrity mode** and,
   if you want, **Require full screen**. See
   [Exam integrity and full-screen exams](doc:DOC-LRN-016).

   ![The Integrity section of the quiz settings](shot:tasks/exams-integrity)

4. **Learners take the quiz.** Once the course and the quiz are published, learners find
   it in **Assessments** in their My Learn area, or in the course itself. See
   [Take a quiz or exam](doc:DOC-LRN-033).

   ![A learner's Assessments page in My Learn](shot:tasks/exams-learner-assessments)

5. **Review attempts and grade.** In **Teaching Dashboard**, open the course, its
   **Assessments** and the quiz results. Open an attempt to see the answers and grade
   written answers. See [Review attempts and grade essay answers](doc:DOC-LRN-017).

   ![The Quiz Results page](shot:tasks/exams-results)

6. **Give a learner an exception.** On the quiz results, choose **Overrides**, then use
   **Add or update an override** to give one learner more time, another attempt or other
   dates. See [Give a learner a quiz exception](doc:DOC-LRN-018).

   ![The Student overrides panel of a quiz](shot:tasks/exams-overrides)

7. **Check the integrity signals.** Choose **Export integrity CSV** on the quiz results,
   or open **Reports** in the academy sidebar and its **Integrity summary** (owner and
   managers). See [Integrity report and CSV export](doc:DOC-LRN-025).

   ![The Integrity summary in the academy Reports](shot:tasks/exams-integrity-report)

## Who can do each stage

| Stage | Owner | Manager | Instructor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create quizzes and change their settings | Yes | Yes | Assigned courses |
| Review attempts, grade, give exceptions, export the CSV | Yes | Yes | Assigned courses |
| Academy Reports | Yes | Yes | No |

> [!NOTE]
> The time limit, availability dates, shuffling, one-question-per-page layout and
> integrity recording are applied only where Atlas has switched these exam features on
> for your academy. The settings always appear in the quiz editor. Where the features
> are off, learners still take the quiz, but with no timer, all questions on one page
> and nothing recorded. If a learner sees no timer on a timed quiz, contact Atlas
> through **Support**.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Integrity signals are for your review. They record events such as leaving full
> screen; they don't prove that a learner cheated, and they can't stop screen recording
> or someone helping the learner. Full screen isn't available on some phone browsers,
> for example every browser on iPhone: learners there take the quiz as usual, and you
> see that full screen was unavailable.

Problems with timers or full screen? See
[Quiz timer, autosave and full-screen problems](doc:DOC-TRB-011).
