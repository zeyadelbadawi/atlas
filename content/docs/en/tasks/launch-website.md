The owner and managers can do every stage below. All the website pages are under
**Website** in the academy sidebar.

## Before you start

Your academy was created with a website in the theme you picked. Until you publish it,
visitors see a Coming Soon page. See [Website builder overview](doc:DOC-WEB-001).

## Stages

1. **Choose your theme.** Open **Website**, then **Settings**, and the **Theme** tab.
   Pick the theme that fits your academy. See [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002).

   ![The Theme tab of the website settings](shot:tasks/website-theme)

2. **Apply your logo, colours and favicon.** In the academy sidebar, open **Visual
   Identity**, add your logo and colours, and choose **Save visual identity**. See
   [Brand Studio: logo, colours and favicon](doc:DOC-ACD-004).

   ![The Visual identity page with the Save visual identity button](shot:tasks/website-visual-identity)

3. **Replace sample content and edit your pages.** Open **Pages** to edit each page and
   its sections. If you started with a ready-made website, replace every sample item
   with your own before you publish. See [Replace sample content](doc:DOC-WEB-007) and
   [Edit your website in the visual editor](doc:DOC-WEB-015).

   ![The Pages list of the website](shot:tasks/website-pages)

4. **Choose the languages.** In **Settings**, open the **Language** tab and choose the
   **Main language** your site opens in. Text in the main language is required; the
   other language is optional, and where a translation is missing visitors see the
   main-language text. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

   ![The Language tab with the main language choice](shot:tasks/website-language)

5. **Preview and publish.** Go back to the **Website** overview, check the **Launch
   checklist**, then choose **Publish** and confirm. After the first publish, later
   edits go live when you choose **Publish changes**. See
   [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).

   ![The Website overview with the publish bar and the launch checklist](shot:tasks/website-publish)

6. **Connect your own domain (optional).** In **Settings**, open the **Domain** tab and
   choose **Connect a domain**, then add the DNS records it shows at your domain
   provider. See [Connect your own domain](doc:DOC-ACD-006) and
   [Custom domain DNS records](doc:DOC-REF-003).

   ![The Domain tab of the website settings](shot:tasks/website-domain)

## Who can do each stage

| Stage | Owner | Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Theme, visual identity, pages, languages | Yes | Yes |
| Publish and unpublish | Yes | Yes |
| Connect your own domain | Yes | Yes |
| Change the academy's Atlas address | Yes | No |

## Common failure points

- **The site still shows Coming Soon**: it isn't published yet, or the subscription has
  lapsed. See [My website shows "Coming soon"](doc:DOC-TRB-005).
- **Some text shows in the other language**: the translation is missing for that
  field, so visitors see the main-language text. See
  [Arabic text appears in English on my site](doc:DOC-TRB-016).
- **The domain doesn't verify**: DNS changes can take time to spread. See
  [Custom domain not verifying](doc:DOC-TRB-006).
