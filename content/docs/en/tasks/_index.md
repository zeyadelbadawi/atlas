## Pick a task

| Task | What you get | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Launch your academy website](doc:DOC-TASK-001) | Your theme, brand and pages in place, the site published, and your own domain if you have one. | Owner, manager |
| [Sell courses and collect payments](doc:DOC-TASK-002) | A priced course that learners pay for by bank transfer, mobile wallet or InstaPay, and that you approve. | Owner |
| [Run online quizzes and exams](doc:DOC-TASK-003) | A quiz with time, attempts and integrity settings, then attempts reviewed and graded. | Owner, manager, instructor |
| [Protect your content from sharing](doc:DOC-TASK-004) | The watermark, device limits and sharing signals, and what to do if a copy leaks. | Owner |
| [Manage learners and enrollments](doc:DOC-TASK-005) | Who can join, approvals, course access with an end date, and blocking. | Owner, manager |

## Not sure where to start?

Follow the [launch checklist](doc:DOC-START-005): it takes a new academy from setup to
its first learner. To see who on your team can do what, read
