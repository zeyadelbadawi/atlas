How you start depends on whether you already had an Atlas account with the
email the academy used.

## If you are new to Atlas: set your password

The academy created an account for you, and Atlas emails you **Your account is
ready**: "An account was created for you at" followed by the academy's name.

Open the email and select its button. The **Set your password** page opens:
type a password of at least 8 characters in **New Password** and **Confirm
Password**, then choose **Set password**. Your account is now active, and your email address
counts as verified.

The setup link lasts 72 hours. If it has expired, the page says "This setup
link is invalid or has expired." Choose **Get a new link**, enter your email,
and use the newest email.

## If you already have an Atlas account

You get **You've been added to an academy** instead. Nothing to set up: sign in
with the email and password you already use.

## Team members: your first sign-in

1. Go to the [Atlas sign-in page](app:/auth/sign-in), enter your email and
   password, and choose **Sign In**.

   ![The Atlas sign-in page with the Sign In button highlighted](shot:account/sign-in-submit)

2. Open the account menu and choose **Profile** to check your name and add
   your phone number.

   ![The account menu of a manager with Profile highlighted](shot:account/invite-staff-profile-menu)

Atlas may ask for a code sent to your email after your password
([Sign-in codes by email](doc:DOC-IAM-005)).

## Learners: join with an invite link

An academy can also share an invite link that opens its sign-up page.

1. Open the invite link, fill in the form, and choose **Create Account**. If
   you already have an Atlas account, choose **Join with it** under "Already
   have an Atlas account?" instead.

   ![The academy sign-up page opened from an invite link, with Create Account highlighted](shot:account/invite-learner-sign-up)

2. Sign in on the academy's website with your email and password.

   ![The academy website's sign-in page with the Sign In button highlighted](shot:account/invite-learner-sign-in)

If an academy added you as a learner and you were new to Atlas, your setup
email works the same way as above, but you sign in on the academy's website,
not on the dashboard. See
[Where to sign in](doc:DOC-IAM-002).

> [!NOTE]
> If an academy adds an existing account whose email address was never
> verified, Atlas sends a fresh setup link to that address. The account's old
> password stops working until the new one is set.
