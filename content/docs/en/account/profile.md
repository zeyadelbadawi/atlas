Anyone can edit their own profile. These steps use the Atlas dashboard; the
learner version is described below.

## Change your name, language and theme

1. Open the account menu (your name at the top of the dashboard) and choose
   **Profile**.

   ![The account menu open with Profile highlighted](shot:account/profile-menu)

2. On the **Personal** tab, choose **Edit**, change your **First name** or
   **Last name**, and choose **Save changes**.

   ![The Personal Information card with the Edit button highlighted](shot:account/profile-personal)

3. Open the **Preferences** tab and choose your **Language** (English or
   Arabic) and **Theme** (**Light**, **Dark** or **System**).

   ![The Preferences tab with the Language and Theme fields highlighted](shot:account/profile-preferences)

4. Choose **Save changes**.

   ![The Preferences tab with the Save changes button highlighted](shot:account/profile-preferences-save)

Atlas shows "Preferences updated". The language and theme apply straight away
and are saved to your account, so your next sign-in starts the same way.
**System** follows your device's light or dark setting.

Your name appears across Atlas wherever your account is shown.

## Switch the theme from the account menu

For a quick change, open the account menu, choose **Appearance**, and pick
**Light**, **Dark** or **System**. The page changes as soon as you choose.

![The account menu with the Appearance options open and Dark highlighted](shot:account/appearance-menu)

## Emails you receive

The **Preferences** tab also lists the kinds of emails Atlas sends you, with a
switch for each. They save as soon as you change them. See
[Notifications](category:notifications).

## Learners: My Learn, Profile

On an academy's website, learners open **My Learn** and choose **Profile**. It
has three tabs: **Personal** (name and phone number), **Account** (when you
joined, and **Your data**) and **Preferences** (language, theme and emails).

![The learner Profile page with the Preferences tab highlighted](shot:account/learner-profile)

If another learner at the same academy already uses your name, the Personal
tab asks you to "Please choose a different display name". Add a middle or
family name and choose **Save changes**.

## The Account tab

On the dashboard, the **Account** tab shows your **User ID**, your **Role**,
**Member since** and the **Organizations** you belong to. It is also where you
can [delete your account](doc:DOC-IAM-010).

If you belong to two or more organizations, a button with the current
organization's name appears in the top bar of the dashboard. Open it to switch
organization. With only one organization, the button isn't shown.

## What you can't change

- Your email address: "Your email address can't be changed here yet."
- Your organization's name, once the organization is created.

Your phone number has its own card: see [Your phone number](doc:DOC-IAM-009).
