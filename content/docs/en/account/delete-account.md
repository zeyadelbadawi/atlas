This is for team accounts (owners, managers and instructors) signed in on the
Atlas dashboard.

## Before you start

- Your email address must be verified, because the confirmation code is sent
  there ([Verify your email address](doc:DOC-IAM-004)).
- Read what deletion does. The card lists it before you can continue:
  - "Your name, email address and profile are permanently removed. The
    account cannot be restored."
  - "You are signed out everywhere immediately, and you will not be able to
    sign in again."
  - "Any academy you own is archived, which takes its public website offline."
  - "Records that must be kept — billing history, audit entries, and content
    you posted — remain, but are no longer linked to you by name."
  - Forensic video watermark records are kept until 730 days after each code
    was last shown, so a leaked recording can still be traced.

If you only want to stop paying, cancel your subscription instead (see
[Billing](category:billing)). To remove one academy and keep your account, see
[Academy settings](category:academy).

## Delete your account

1. Open the account menu (your name at the top of the dashboard) and choose
   **Profile**.

   ![The account menu open with Profile highlighted](shot:account/profile-menu)

2. Open the **Account** tab. Under **Delete my account**, read the list, then
   choose **Delete my account**.

   ![The Delete my account card with its button highlighted](shot:account/delete-account-card)

3. In **Delete your account permanently?**, you can choose a reason and leave
   feedback (both optional). Type your email address in the confirmation field,
   then choose **Email me a confirmation code**.

   ![The deletion dialog with the email typed and Email me a confirmation code highlighted](shot:account/delete-account-dialog)

4. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and choose **Permanently delete**.

   ![The code step of the deletion dialog with Permanently delete highlighted](shot:account/delete-account-code)

"Nothing is deleted until you enter it here. It expires in 10 minutes." If it
expires, choose **Send a new code**.

## What happens next

Atlas deletes your account straight away, signs you out on every device and
takes you to the Atlas home page. Any academy you own is archived and its
website goes offline. Your organization's billing and audit history stay, no
longer linked to your name.

> [!WARNING]
> Deletion can't be undone, and an archived academy can't be brought back.

## Learners

Learners don't have a delete button. In **My Learn**, open **Profile**, then
the **Account** tab, and under **Your data** choose **Request deletion**. The
Atlas team handles the request and emails you when it is done. See
[Data retention and account deletion](doc:DOC-SEC-005).

## A copy of your data

There is no download button for team accounts. Learners can ask for a copy
under **Your data**. Team members can ask the Atlas team from the
[contact page](site:/contact?topic=support).
