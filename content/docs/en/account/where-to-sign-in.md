Atlas has two kinds of sign-in page. Which one you use depends on what you
do on Atlas, not on which email you have.

## Team members: the Atlas dashboard

Owners, managers and instructors sign in at the
[Atlas sign-in page](app:/auth/sign-in) with their email and password, then
choose **Sign In**. The page reminds you: "Students sign in on their own
academy website, not here."

![The Atlas dashboard sign-in page with the note that students sign in on their academy website](shot:account/sign-in-dashboard)

## Learners: your academy's website

Learners sign in on the website of the academy they study at. Open the
academy's address (the academy gives it to you) and choose **Sign in**. The
page is titled "Sign in to" followed by the academy's name. If you don't have
an account at that academy yet, choose **Sign up** there.

![An academy website's sign-in page with the Sign up link](shot:account/sign-in-academy)

You have one Atlas account for every academy you join. Your courses, progress
and certificates stay separate in each academy, and each academy's website
has its own sign-in. Being signed in at one academy does not sign you in at
another one, or on the dashboard.

## If the dashboard says you are a student

If your account is only a learner account and you try the dashboard, Atlas
shows "You are a student. Please sign in through your academy’s website."
Your password was correct. Under **Your academies**, choose your academy to
go to its website, then sign in there with the same email and password.

If no academy is listed, ask your academy for its website address.

## If you are on a team and also a learner

Sign in on the dashboard to manage your academy. To study at an academy,
sign in on that academy's website.

When a team member signs in on their own academy's website, they see the
site the way a learner would. Atlas does not add them to the academy's
learners.

## Other messages on an academy sign-in page

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "You are not a member of this academy yet." | The academy accepts new learners only with approval or an invitation. Use its **Sign up** page instead. |
| "Your access to this academy has been blocked. Please contact the academy." | The academy has blocked your access. Only the academy can change this. |
| "Invalid email or password." | The email or password is wrong. See [Reset or change your password](doc:DOC-IAM-003). |

If the academy lets anyone join, signing in on its website with an existing
Atlas account adds that academy to your account.

## After your password

Atlas may ask for one more step before you are in: a 6-digit code sent to
your email ([Sign-in codes by email](doc:DOC-IAM-005)) or, if you turned it
on, the code from your authenticator app
([Set up an authenticator app](doc:DOC-IAM-006)).

## Signing out

On the dashboard, open the account menu (your name at the top) and choose
**Sign out**.

![The account menu open with Sign out highlighted](shot:account/account-menu-sign-out)

On an academy's website, open the account menu with your name and choose
**Sign out**.

Signing out ends your session on this browser only. To sign out other
devices, see [See and end your active sessions](doc:DOC-IAM-007).
