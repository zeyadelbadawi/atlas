## Why Atlas asks for your number

The sign-up form asks for your **Mobile number**: "We use it to contact you
about your account." Choose the country from the list beside the number; Atlas
picks one from your device's time zone, and you can change it. You can type
the number with Arabic or Western digits.

![The sign-up form with the Mobile number field highlighted](shot:account/sign-up-phone)

Atlas accepts mobile numbers only, and the number must belong to the country
you chose. A landline number, or a number from another country than the one
selected, is refused.

## Who can see it

- **You**, on your profile.
- **If you are a student in an academy**: that academy's owners and managers,
  and Atlas platform administrators.
- **Instructors and other students can't see it.**

Your number is also stored, encrypted, in the forensic watermark records that
link the codes shown on lesson videos to accounts. See
[What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

## Add, change or remove it

Open your profile (team members: account menu, **Profile**; learners: **My
Learn**, **Profile**). On the **Personal** tab, the **Phone number** card has
**Add phone number**, **Change number** and **Remove**.

![The Phone number card on the Personal tab](shot:account/phone-card)

If you change your number many times in a short period, Atlas asks you to try
again later.

## If the number is already used by another account

Each mobile number can belong to one Atlas account only. If you see "This phone
number is already used by another account", Atlas won't say which account has
it. If it's your number, contact support to reclaim it, or enter a different
number.

## Verification is not available yet

Your number shows **Not verified**: "Verification by SMS or WhatsApp is coming
soon. There’s nothing you need to do for now."

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't check that the number is yours, and doesn't send sign-in codes
> by SMS or WhatsApp. Sign-in codes go to your email
> ([Sign-in codes by email](doc:DOC-IAM-005)).
