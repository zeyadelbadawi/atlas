Anyone can do this for their own account. Owners, managers and instructors use
their profile in the dashboard. Learners use **Security** in **My Learn** on
their academy's website, which has the same card.

## Sign out one device

1. Open the account menu, choose **Profile**, then open the **Security** tab.

   ![The Profile page with the Security tab highlighted](shot:account/security-tab)

2. Find **Active Sessions**. Each device shows **Last active**, the country it
   signed in from, the date it signed in and its **IP address**. The browser
   you are using is marked **This device**.

   ![The Active Sessions card listing signed-in devices](shot:account/sessions-card)

3. Choose **Sign out** next to the device you want to end.

   ![The Active Sessions card with a device's Sign out button highlighted](shot:account/session-sign-out)

4. In **Sign out this device?**, choose **Sign out** to confirm.

   ![The Sign out this device? confirmation dialog](shot:account/session-sign-out-confirm)

Atlas shows "Device signed out". That device has to sign in again. If you sign
out **This device**, you go back to the sign-in page.

## Sign out all other devices

When other devices are signed in, the card shows **Sign out all other
devices**. Choose it and confirm: every other device is signed out straight
away, and the browser you are using stays signed in.

Changing your password goes one step further: it signs out every device,
including this one ([Reset or change your password](doc:DOC-IAM-003)).

## If you don't recognise a session

1. Sign it out as shown above.
2. Change your password.
3. Turn on an [authenticator app](doc:DOC-IAM-006).

## Three different lists

| List | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Sessions** | Profile, **Security** | Devices signed in to your account right now. |
| **Remembered Devices** | Profile, **Security** | Browsers that can skip the emailed sign-in code. Forgetting one doesn't sign it out. See [Sign-in codes by email](doc:DOC-IAM-005). |
| **Devices** (learners) | **My Learn**, **Devices** | The devices a learner watches lessons on. Managed separately; see [For learners](category:for-learners). |

> [!NOTE]
> A session doesn't last forever. Even if you use it every day, Atlas asks you
> to sign in again after a while.
