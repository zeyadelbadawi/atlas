You pay for Atlas by transfer: there's no card payment and nothing is charged automatically. Only the **organization owner** can pay. Prices are on the [pricing page](site:/pricing); checkout shows the exact amount for the plan and billing cycle you choose.

## Start checkout

1. Open **Plans** from the sidebar. During a trial you can also choose **Subscribe to …** in the banner at the top of the dashboard.

   ![The dashboard with Plans highlighted in the sidebar](shot:billing/plans-open)

2. Under the plan you want, choose **Select this plan**.

   ![The plan comparison with Select this plan highlighted under a plan](shot:billing/plans-select)

3. On **Checkout**, choose the **Billing cycle**: **Monthly** or **Yearly**, when the plan offers both. Check the price shown.

   ![Checkout with the billing cycle choice highlighted](shot:billing/checkout-cycle)

4. Choose **Start checkout**.

   ![Checkout with the Start checkout button highlighted](shot:billing/checkout-start)

## Pay and send your proof

After you start checkout, Atlas shows the **Order summary**, **What changes** and **Payment method**:

- Under **Payment method**, pick bank transfer, e-wallet or InstaPay, then choose **Continue to payment**.
- Follow the **Transfer instructions**. Send the exact amount from your own bank, wallet or InstaPay app, and write the reference the instructions ask for.
- Under **Proof of payment**, choose **Choose file** and pick a screenshot or PDF of the transfer (PNG, JPG or PDF, up to 10 MB). You can add a note for the reviewer.
- Choose **Submit for review**. The payment shows **Awaiting review**, and you get an email confirming that your proof arrived.

## While your payment is reviewed

The Atlas team reviews each payment by hand. You don't need to do anything else. Find the payment any time under **Billing**: choose **View all payments** or **Payment history**.

Only one payment can wait for review at a time. If you start another checkout, Atlas shows "A payment is already awaiting review" with a link to it.

## When it's approved

- The payment page says "This payment succeeded. Your subscription has been updated."
- You get an email that the payment was approved, and a receipt email with your plan, its dates and its limits. See [Billing receipts and emails](doc:DOC-BIL-009).
- Your dates depend on when you pay:
  - **Before your paid period ends:** the new period starts when the current one ends, so you lose no days.
  - **During a trial:** the trial ends and the paid plan starts when the payment is approved.
  - **During the grace period or after it ended:** the new period starts when the payment is approved.
- Your first paid subscription may include [gifted setup days](doc:DOC-BIL-003).

## If it's rejected

You get an email with the reviewer's reason, and the payment page says "This payment failed. You can start a new checkout to try again." Fix what the reason says, then start a new checkout.

> [!WARNING]
> Checkout keeps the price for 30 minutes. If it runs out before you continue, start checkout again.

> [!NOTE]
> If checkout says "No payment method available", no payment method is set up for your organization yet. Choose **Contact support** and the Atlas team will set one up. See [Get help: open a support ticket](doc:DOC-SUP-001).

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't take card payments and doesn't renew your plan automatically. Every period is a new checkout and transfer. Atlas doesn't promise a review time.
