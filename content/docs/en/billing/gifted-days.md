Gifted setup days are extra days Atlas adds to your **first paid subscription**, to give you time to set up without using up the period you paid for.

## Who gets them

- Only on your organization's first paid subscription, and only once per person.
- Only when the plan and billing cycle you choose include them. Some plans and cycles have none. See the [pricing page](site:/pricing).
- If you're on a trial, you can still get them when you subscribe.
- You don't get them when you renew or extend a period that's already running.

## Where to see them

At checkout, if the plan and billing cycle you choose include gifted days, you see "Your first paid subscription includes" followed by the number of gifted setup days. Switching between **Monthly** and **Yearly** can change this.

![Checkout with the Yearly billing cycle chosen and the gifted setup days offer highlighted](shot:billing/checkout-gift)

After your payment is approved, the **Subscription** page shows **Gifted setup days** and **Paid period**, each with its dates, and how many gifted days are left. Your receipt email lists them too.

## How they're added to your dates

The gifted days start when your payment is approved. Your paid period starts when they end and then runs for the full month or year you paid for. For example, with a yearly plan that includes gifted days, you get the gifted days first, then a full year.

> [!NOTE]
> Gifted days come from paying for a plan, so you need a payment approved first. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).
