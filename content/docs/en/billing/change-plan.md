Every renewal and plan change is a new checkout and payment. Only the **organization owner** can do it. Compare plans and prices on the [pricing page](site:/pricing).

## Choose a plan

1. Open **Subscription** and choose **Compare plans**.

   ![The Subscription page with Compare plans highlighted](shot:billing/subscription-compare)

2. Under the plan you want, choose **Select this plan**. Your current plan is marked **Current plan**. To renew it, open **Plans** from the sidebar instead and choose **Select this plan** under your current plan.

   ![The plan comparison opened from Subscription, with Select this plan highlighted](shot:billing/compare-select)

3. On **Checkout**, choose the billing cycle and then **Start checkout**. Pay as described in [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).

   ![Checkout with the Start checkout button highlighted](shot:billing/checkout-start)

## What happens to your dates and limits

| You pay for | When the plan and its limits change | When the new paid period starts |
|---|---|---|
| Your current plan, before the period ends (renewal) | No change | When your current period ends |
| A bigger plan, while a paid period is running (upgrade) | As soon as the payment is approved | When your current period ends |
| A smaller plan, more than 7 days before the period ends (downgrade) | At the end of your current period | When your current period ends |
| A smaller plan, in the last 7 days of the period | As soon as the payment is approved | When your current period ends |
| Any plan during a trial, during the grace period, or after your subscription ended | As soon as the payment is approved | When the payment is approved |

Before you pay, checkout shows **What changes**, with **Takes effect**: "When your payment is confirmed". You keep your current plan until then.

When a downgrade is scheduled, the **Subscription** page tells you which plan you change to and on what date. Until then you keep everything in your current plan.

## Downgrading when you use more than the new plan allows

Nothing is deleted when you move to a smaller plan. If you already have more than the new limit (for example, more courses), everything stays, but you can't add more until you're back under the limit. Check your numbers on [the Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006) first.

## Annual plans

While more than 30 days of an annual plan remain, you can't switch to a different plan from checkout. You can still renew your current plan. To change plans earlier, contact Atlas support from **Support** in the dashboard.

## Prices at renewal

Each renewal is charged at the plan's current price. If a plan's price or limits change, the **Subscription** page and an email tell you what changes at your next renewal. Nothing changes for the period you already paid for.

> [!LIMITATION]
> There's no proration, credit or refund when you change plans: each payment buys a full new period, added after the current one. Nothing renews automatically.
