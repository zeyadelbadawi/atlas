This page lists the billing records Atlas keeps for your subscription and the emails it sends you about it. They go to the **organization owner**.

## Your receipt email

When your payment is approved, Atlas sends a receipt email with your plan, the start and end of your paid period and the limits you bought. On your first paid subscription, it also shows any [gifted setup days](doc:DOC-BIL-003). Each renewal sends its own receipt.

## Payment history

Open **Billing** from the sidebar. **Needs attention** shows payments that are waiting for you. Choose **View all payments** or **Payment history** to see every payment with its amount, method, status and review result.

![The Billing page with the Payment history link highlighted](shot:billing/billing-overview)

Open a payment to see its **Transfer instructions**, its status and, once you've uploaded one, **View submitted proof**.

## Invoices

Atlas doesn't issue invoices or PDF receipts yet. The receipt email and your payment history are your record.

## Billing emails Atlas sends

| When | Email |
|---|---|
| You start a trial | Your trial has started, with its end date |
| You upload a payment proof | Your proof arrived and is waiting for review |
| The Atlas team approves your payment | Payment approved, plus the receipt email |
| The Atlas team rejects your payment | Payment rejected, with the reviewer's reason |
| You cancel a paid subscription | Cancellation confirmed, with the date access ends |
| A plan's price or limits change at your next renewal | What changes and when |
| You get close to a plan limit, or reach it | Which limit, and how to raise it |

Atlas may also send reminders before your trial or paid period ends, and when a grace period starts or ends.

> [!NOTE]
> Receipt and payment emails can't be turned off. If one doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and see [I didn't get an email](doc:DOC-TRB-009).
