The trial lets you build your academy on a real plan before you pay. It lasts {trialDuration} and includes everything on the plan you choose.

## How long it lasts

The trial lasts {trialDuration}. Your exact end date is shown on the **Subscription** page and in the banner at the top of the dashboard.

![The dashboard banner during a trial, showing the plan, the days left and the Subscribe button](shot:billing/trial-banner)

## What's included

- Full access to everything on the plan you trial, within that plan's limits. See the [pricing page](site:/pricing) for what each plan includes.
- No credit card. You won't be charged when it ends: you choose whether to subscribe.

## One trial per email address

Each person gets one trial. A second account or a second organization with the same email address can't start another one, and a trial you cancel can't be started again.

## Start a trial

- **When you sign up:** choose the plan you want to try during sign-up. See [Create your account and start a trial](doc:DOC-START-002).
- **Later:** if your organization has no plan yet and you haven't used your trial, open **Plans** and choose the **Try** button under a plan (it shows the plan's name and the trial length). Check the dates in the dialog that opens and confirm.

## Continue after the trial

To keep going without a break, choose **Subscribe to …** with your plan's name in the banner, or open **Plans**, pick a plan and pay. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).

When the Atlas team approves your payment, the trial ends and your paid plan starts. You keep trial access until then. Days left in the trial aren't added to the paid period, but your first paid subscription may include [gifted setup days](doc:DOC-BIL-003).

## When the trial ends

- Your dashboard becomes read-only: you can see everything, but you can't make changes.
- Your academy website shows a Coming Soon page.
- Nothing is deleted. Choose a plan and pay, and everything is back as you left it.

There's no grace period after a trial. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

## Cancel early

You can cancel a trial from the **Subscription** page. Access ends as soon as you confirm, and you can't start another trial. See [Cancel your trial or subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-008).

> [!NOTE]
> There's no button to extend a trial. If you need more time, contact Atlas support from **Support** in the dashboard.
