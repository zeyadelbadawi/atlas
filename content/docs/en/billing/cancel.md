Only the **organization owner** can cancel. The steps are the same for a trial and a paid subscription, but the effect is different. The screenshots show a trial.

## Cancel

1. Open **Subscription** from the sidebar and choose **Cancel trial**. On a paid subscription, the button is **Cancel subscription**.

   ![The Subscription page with the Cancel trial button highlighted](shot:billing/cancel-button)

2. In the dialog, choose a reason under **Why are you cancelling?**. A reason is required. You can also tell us more in the optional box.

   ![The cancel dialog with the reason list highlighted](shot:billing/cancel-reason)

3. Choose **Confirm cancellation**. To back out, choose **Never mind, keep it**.

   ![The cancel dialog with a reason chosen and Confirm cancellation highlighted](shot:billing/cancel-confirm)

## What happens next

**A trial** ends as soon as you confirm. Your dashboard becomes read-only and your website shows Coming Soon. Trials are one per person, so you can't start another one.

**A paid subscription** doesn't renew. Everything keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and the dashboard shows "Your subscription is scheduled to end" with the date. You get an email confirming the cancellation and the date. When the period ends, the dashboard becomes read-only and your website shows Coming Soon. There's no grace period after a cancellation. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

## Changed your mind?

There's no undo button. To keep going, pay for a new period from **Plans**, before or after your current period ends. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004). Paying before the end adds the new period after the current one.

## Refunds

There's no refund button for subscription payments. Atlas's [terms](app:/terms) explain that refund requests are considered case by case. To ask, contact Atlas support from **Support** in the dashboard.

## Cancelling isn't deleting

Cancelling doesn't delete your academies, your content or your account. To delete them, see [Delete an academy](doc:DOC-ACD-010) and [Delete your account](doc:DOC-IAM-010).
