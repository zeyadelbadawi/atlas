Your Atlas subscription belongs to your organization and covers all its academies. It's separate from the payments learners make to your academy. Only the **organization owner** sees and manages it.

## Where to see your subscription

In the dashboard sidebar:

- **Subscription**: your plan, its status, dates and actions such as **Compare plans** and cancelling.
- **Plans**: every plan side by side, where you start a trial or choose a plan to pay for.
- **Usage**: what you use against your plan's limits. See [The Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006).
- **Billing**: payments that need your attention and your payment history.

![The Subscription page showing the plan, its status and description](shot:billing/subscription-status)

Prices and what each plan includes are on the [pricing page](site:/pricing).

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No plan yet** | Your organization is ready, but no plan is chosen. | Open **Plans** and start a trial or choose a plan. |
| **Trialing** | You're on a trial with full access to the plan you picked. | Subscribe before the trial ends to keep going. See [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002). |
| **Trial ended** | The trial is over. The dashboard is read-only. | Choose a plan and pay. |
| **Active** | Your paid period is running and everything works. | Renew before the period ends. |
| **Active**, scheduled to end | You cancelled, but everything keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. | Pay for a new period if you change your mind. |
| **Grace period** | Your paid period ended without a new payment. Everything still works for 7 more days. | Open **Plans**, choose your plan and pay before the 7 days run out. |
| **Cancelled** | A cancelled subscription reached the end of its period. | Choose a plan to start again. |
| **Expired** | The grace period ended without payment. The dashboard is read-only. | Choose **Reactivate subscription**, pick a plan and pay. |

A banner at the top of the dashboard explains the current state and gives you the next action, for example **Subscribe to** your plan during a trial, or **Complete payment** during the grace period.

## Paying and renewing

There's no card on file and nothing renews automatically. Each paid period is a separate payment: you start a checkout, transfer the amount by bank, e-wallet or InstaPay, and upload a proof. The Atlas team reviews it and activates the period. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004) and [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).

Paying before your period ends adds the new period after the current one, so you never lose days you already paid for.

## When access ends

When a trial ends, or a paid period and its grace period end, your dashboard becomes read-only and your academy website shows a Coming Soon page. Nothing is deleted. Pay for a plan and everything comes back as you left it. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

> [!NOTE]
> Status changes happen automatically shortly after a date passes, so a status can take a few minutes to update.
