The **Usage** page tells you how close you are to your plan's limits. Only the **organization owner** sees it. Your plan's limits are listed on the [pricing page](site:/pricing).

## Check your usage

1. Open **Usage** from the sidebar.

   ![The Usage page with Usage highlighted in the sidebar](shot:billing/usage-nav)

2. Read each row: what you use, your plan's limit, and a status such as **Within limit** or **Limit reached**. **Unlimited** means your plan has no limit for it.

   ![The usage rows with a Within limit status and the note under the list highlighted](shot:billing/usage-rows)

## What each row counts

| Row | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Academies** | Academies in your organization |
| **Instructors** | Instructors across your academies |
| **Students** | Learners across your academies |
| **Courses** | Courses across your academies |
| **General storage** | Files stored for your academies, in GB |

## When you reach a limit

The row shows "You've reached this limit. Upgrading your plan will raise it." and a **Compare plans** button. Choose it to see which plans have a higher limit, then follow [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).

Atlas also sends you a notification and an email when you're close to a limit and when you reach it.

## Good to know

- As the page says: "Limits are enforced by Atlas, not by this page." When you try to add something over a limit, Atlas stops you at that moment.
- The numbers update shortly after changes, so they can lag for a little while. Right after your subscription starts, the page may say "Usage data isn't ready yet". Check back in a few minutes.
- The email limit for **Messages** isn't shown here: it's shown where you write a message.
