Founding Academies are the first 20 academies to join Atlas. Each one keeps the **yearly** price of one plan for **24 months**, and gets its setup done together with the Atlas team. You apply from the contact form (topic **Founding Academies**) and the Atlas team adds your organization: there is nothing to switch on yourself.

## What is locked

- **One plan, on yearly billing.** The lock covers the plan you agreed with the Atlas team, paid yearly. Monthly billing and other plans are at the normal price.
- **The price on the day you joined**, for 24 months. Any yearly period that **starts** within those 24 months is at your founding price: your first year and the renewal after it.
- **Protection against increases only.** If the catalog price for your plan ever drops below your founding price, you pay the lower price.
- The price is applied by Atlas when it creates your checkout. You don't need a code, and no one else can get your price.

## Where to see your locked price

The **Subscription** page (**Plan & billing** in the sidebar) shows a **Founding Academy** notice with the date your lock ends, your founding price, and what your next yearly renewal will cost.

![The Subscription page with the Founding Academy notice highlighted](shot:billing/founding-notice)

At checkout, when you choose your founding plan and **Yearly**, Atlas shows your founding price and the date it is locked until. You pay for your plan the usual way: see [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).

![Checkout on the Yearly cycle with the Founding Academy price highlighted](shot:billing/founding-checkout)

## When the 24 months end

- The yearly period you already paid for keeps running to its end. Nothing changes mid-period.
- The renewal that starts on or after the end date is at the **current** yearly price. The notice on your Subscription page changes to say the price lock has ended and shows the renewal price.
- About 30 days before a renewal at the current price, Atlas emails the organization owner with the new price, so it never comes as a surprise.

> [!NOTE]
> If your founding membership is ended early by agreement with the Atlas team, the current paid period is untouched. The next renewal is at the current price.

> [!LIMITATION]
> The lock is for one plan only. If you move to a different plan, that plan is at its normal price. Ask the Atlas team before changing plans.
