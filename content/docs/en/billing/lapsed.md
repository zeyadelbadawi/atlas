A lapsed subscription locks changes, not your data. This page explains what stops, what keeps working and how to get everything back.

## When it happens

| Situation | When access is restricted |
|---|---|
| A trial ends | Right away. There's no grace period after a trial. |
| A paid period ends without a new payment | After a 7-day grace period. During those 7 days everything keeps working, and the dashboard shows "Your payment is late — your site is still online". |
| You cancelled a paid subscription | At the end of the period you paid for. There's no grace period after a cancellation. |

The change happens automatically shortly after the date passes.

## What stops

- Every change in the dashboard: editing and publishing your website, building courses, grading, announcements, uploading files, managing members and so on. Atlas refuses the change, and a banner explains why.
- Your academy website shows a Coming Soon page to visitors instead of your site.

## What keeps working

- You can still sign in and see everything in your dashboard: your academies, courses, students and content.
- **Subscription**, **Plans**, **Billing** and checkout keep working, so you can pay.
- **Support** keeps working, so you can still contact Atlas support.
- The organization owner can still approve or reject course payments that learners already sent to your academy (**Payments**), so no one who paid is left waiting, and can record a refund of one. Setting up or changing payment methods stays locked.

## Is anything deleted?

No. As the dashboard says: "Nothing has been deleted. Your academies, courses, students and content are all still here, exactly as you left them."

## Restore access

1. In the banner, choose **Continue with …** (after a trial) or **Reactivate subscription**, or open **Plans** from the sidebar.
2. Pay for a plan. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).
3. When the Atlas team approves the payment, your dashboard works again and your website comes back shortly after.

A new period starts on the day your payment is approved.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't promise how long the payment review takes. To avoid any interruption, pay before your trial or paid period ends.
