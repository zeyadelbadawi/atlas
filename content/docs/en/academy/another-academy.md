Only the organization owner can create academies. How many academies you can have depends on your plan: see [pricing](site:/pricing) for each plan's limit.

## Academy or organization?

- **Another academy** sits in the same organization and shares its subscription. Use it for another subject, brand or branch.
- **Another organization** has its own, separate subscription. See [Your organization and multiple organizations](doc:DOC-ORG-012).

## Create the academy

1. In the sidebar, open **New Academy**. The **Academy Provisioning** page lists every academy you have set up.

   ![The Academy Provisioning page with New Academy highlighted in the sidebar](shot:academy/provisioning-list)

2. Choose **New Academy**.

   ![The New Academy button on the Academy Provisioning page](shot:academy/provisioning-new-button)

3. Type the **Academy name**. Atlas suggests an **Atlas address** from it; edit the address if you want a different one.

   ![The Academy name field on the New Academy form](shot:academy/new-academy-name)

4. Choose a **Theme**, then choose how you would like to start: **Start with a ready-made website** or **Start from a blank page**.

   ![The choice between a ready-made website and a blank page](shot:academy/new-academy-setup-mode)

5. Optional: open **Logo, colours & favicon (optional)** to add your logo and favicon now. You can also do it later.

   ![The optional logo, colours and favicon section](shot:academy/new-academy-brand)

6. Under **Payment methods**, choose **Set up later**, or **Accept payments now** to turn on bank transfer, InstaPay or a mobile wallet.

   ![The payment methods choice with Set up later highlighted](shot:academy/new-academy-payments)

7. Select **Start provisioning**. Atlas opens the setup status page and builds the academy.

   ![The Start provisioning button at the bottom of the form](shot:academy/new-academy-submit)

## What happens next

The status page shows each stage of setup and updates by itself. When it says your academy is ready, choose **Continue setup**. If setup stops or fails, see [Academy creation status, retries and failures](doc:DOC-ACD-011).

## If you've reached the limit

If your plan's academy limit is reached, the New Academy page shows **Academy limit reached** with a **View plans** button instead of the form. To add an academy, move to a plan with more academies ([Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005)), or delete an academy you no longer need: deleting one frees its place.

## What's shared and what's separate

| Shared by all academies | Separate for each academy |
|---|---|
| Your organization and its subscription | Website, theme and address |
| The plan's limits | Courses, quizzes and certificates |
| | Managers, instructors and learners |
| | Payment methods and course orders |

Each academy needs its own name and address: names are unique across Atlas. Add your team to each academy separately.
