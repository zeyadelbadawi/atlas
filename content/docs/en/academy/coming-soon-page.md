Every academy has a Coming Soon page, ready from the minute the academy is created. Atlas shows it instead of your website when there is nothing it can show visitors yet.

## What visitors see

- Your academy's name, and your logo if you uploaded one.
- The heading **Coming soon** and the line "This academy's website hasn't been published yet. Please check back soon — there will be something here shortly."
- "Powered by Atlas" at the bottom.

There is no launch date, no contact details and no other content.

## When it appears

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| Your website has never been published, or you unpublished it | There is no published version to show |
| Your trial has ended | The organization has no active access |
| Your subscription has ended or was cancelled | The organization has no active access |

During the grace period after a paid period ends, your website stays online.

## It never mentions billing

The page looks the same whatever the reason. Visitors and learners are never told that a trial or subscription has ended: that stays between you and Atlas.

## How to remove it

- **Unpublished website:** publish it. See [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).
- **Trial or subscription ended:** pay for your plan. Once the payment is approved, your website comes back shortly. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

## Can I edit it?

No. The Coming Soon page is part of Atlas, not a page of your website, so it can't be edited or deleted. Your logo on it comes from [Visual identity](doc:DOC-ACD-004).
