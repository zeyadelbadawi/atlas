Every screen in the Atlas dashboard (`dashboard.atlass.dpdns.org`) works on one academy at a time. The academy switcher in the top bar tells you which one is open and lets you move to another.

## Switch academy

1. In the top bar, select the academy switcher. It shows the name of the academy that is open and your role in it.

   ![The academy switcher in the top bar](shot:academy/switcher)

2. In the **Switch Academy** list, choose the academy you want to open.

   ![The Switch Academy list open](shot:academy/switcher-open)

3. If you have many academies, choose **Search academies** and type part of the name to find one quickly.

   ![The search field in the academy switcher](shot:academy/switcher-search)

## What happens next

Atlas shows "Opening" with the academy's name and loads the same screen in the academy you chose. If you have unsaved changes, Atlas asks before leaving the page.

The academy is part of the page address, so a bookmark always opens the academy it was saved from.

## Which academies you see

| You are | You see |
|---|---|
| Organization owner | Every academy in your organization |
| Manager or instructor | Only the academies you were added to |

Your role in each academy (**Owner**, **Manager** or **Instructor**) is shown next to its name.

## Switch organization

If you belong to more than one organization, a **Switch organization** control appears in the top bar. Use it to move to the other organization; the academy switcher then lists that organization's academies. With only one organization, you won't see it.

## If you lose access to an academy

If your access is removed, or the academy is deleted, Atlas moves you to an academy you can open and tells you: "You no longer have access to that academy".

> [!NOTE]
> Learners don't use these switchers. A learner moves between academies by visiting each academy's website. See [How learner accounts work across academies](doc:DOC-ORG-011).
