Only the organization owner can delete an academy. Read what is permanent before you start.

## Before you delete: what is permanent

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting an academy cannot be undone, and there is no restore. The Atlas team can't bring it back either.

> [!LIMITATION]
> The academy's name and its Atlas address stay reserved after deletion. Nobody can use them again, including you. If you want to start over with the same name, don't delete: edit the academy you have.

When you delete an academy:

- Its public website stops responding immediately.
- Managers, instructors and students lose access to it.
- A custom domain connected to it is released, so you can connect it to another academy.
- Its place on your plan is freed, so you can create a replacement.
- Billing and payment records are kept, as they must be.

**Just want to go offline for a while?** Unpublish your website instead. It's reversible. See [Preview, publish and unpublish your site](doc:DOC-WEB-008).

## Delete the academy

1. Open the academy you want to delete with the academy switcher, open **Academy Settings** and scroll to **Delete this academy** at the bottom.

   ![The Delete this academy card with its list of consequences](shot:academy/delete-card)

2. Read the list, then choose **Delete academy**.

   ![The Delete academy button](shot:academy/delete-button)

3. Optional: choose a reason under **Why are you deleting it? (optional)** and add feedback for the Atlas team.

   ![The confirmation dialog with the reason list highlighted](shot:academy/delete-reason)

4. Type the academy's name exactly as shown to confirm.

   ![The field where you type the academy name](shot:academy/delete-type-name)

5. Select **Permanently delete**. You see that the academy has been deleted and its website is offline.

   ![The Permanently delete button in the dialog](shot:academy/delete-confirm)

## Deleting your account deletes your academies

If you delete your own Atlas account, every academy in the organizations you own is deleted too. See [Delete your account](doc:DOC-IAM-010).
