The academy owner and managers can edit the academy profile. Instructors can't open it.

## Edit the profile

1. In the sidebar, open **Academy home**, then choose **View Profile**.

   ![The academy overview with the View Profile button highlighted](shot:academy/overview-view-profile)

2. Under **Basic Information**, change the **Academy Name** or the **Description** if you need to.

   ![The Basic Information card with the Academy Name field](shot:academy/profile-basic-info)

3. Under **Contact Information**, fill in the **Contact Email**, **Contact Phone** and **Website** you want people to use.

   ![The Contact Information card with the Contact Email field highlighted](shot:academy/profile-contact)

4. Under **Address**, fill in the **Street Address**, **City**, **State/Province**, **Postal Code** and **Country**.

   ![The Address card with the Street Address field highlighted](shot:academy/profile-address)

5. Select **Save Changes**. You see "Profile updated successfully".

   ![The Save Changes button at the bottom of the profile](shot:academy/profile-save)

## Good to know

- **Names are unique.** Two academies on Atlas can't have the same name. If the name is taken, the name field tells you, and nothing is saved.
- **Where contact details appear.** Your contact details are shown on your academy's public pages, so use an email and phone number you are happy to share.
- **The name also lives in Visual Identity.** You can rename the academy there too. Change it in one place at a time, then reload the other page.
- **Unsaved changes.** If you leave the page before saving, Atlas asks whether you want to leave without saving.
- **Language, time zone and currency** are on this page as well, under **Localization**. See [Language, currency and time zone](doc:DOC-ACD-003).

> [!NOTE]
> The **Academy Slug** field is read-only unless you are the organization owner. It can change once every 60 days. Changing it moves your website to a new address, and the old address keeps working and sends visitors to the new one. See [Your academy web address](doc:DOC-ACD-005).
