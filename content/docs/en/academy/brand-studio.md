The academy owner and managers can change the visual identity. Your academy's name, logo, favicon and colours are saved together, and they are live on your website as soon as you save. There is no separate publish step for them.

## Before you start

- **Logo:** PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, up to 2 MB. A logo on a transparent background works best.
- **Favicon** (the small icon in the browser tab): PNG or ICO, up to 1 MB. Square works best.

## Change your logo, colours and favicon

1. In the sidebar, under **Website & brand**, open **Visual identity**.

   ![The Visual Identity page with its entry in the sidebar highlighted](shot:academy/visual-identity)

2. Under **Logo and colours**, choose **Choose logo** (or **Replace logo** if you already have one) and pick your file. Atlas reads the colours in your logo and suggests a palette.

   ![The Replace logo button in the Logo and colours card](shot:academy/visual-identity-logo)

3. Look at **Your palette**. Each colour shows whether it is readable. To see another suggestion from the same logo, choose **Try another combination**.

   ![The palette with the Try another combination button highlighted](shot:academy/visual-identity-palette)

4. Optional: to change a single colour, open **Advanced: adjust individual colours** and pick the colour you want. If a text colour is hard to read, Atlas tells you why and offers **Use the nearest readable colour**.

   ![The advanced colour settings opened](shot:academy/visual-identity-advanced)

5. Under **Name and favicon**, choose **Upload Favicon** (or **Change Favicon**) and pick your icon file.

   ![The Change Favicon button in the Name and favicon card](shot:academy/visual-identity-favicon)

6. Select **Save visual identity**. You see "Visual identity saved. Your website shows it now."

   ![The Save visual identity button at the bottom of the page](shot:academy/visual-identity-save)

## If Atlas won't save

- **"Fix the highlighted colours before saving."** A text or button colour is not readable on its background. Pick the suggested readable colour, or another colour, and save again.
- **"Someone else changed these settings in the meantime."** Another team member saved while you were editing. Reload the page to see their version, then make your change again.
- **The logo file is refused.** Check the type and size above. A very large image is refused too: use a logo under 8000 pixels on its longest side.

## Good to know

- **Black and white logos.** If your logo has no colour, Atlas uses a theme colour for accents. You can pick a brand colour yourself.
- **Changing the logo later** keeps your saved colours. Atlas offers **Preview matching palette** if you want colours from the new logo, or **Keep current colours**.
- **Logo for dark backgrounds.** Add a second version of your logo for themes that place it on a dark surface.
- **SVG logos** are cleaned before use, so some SVG features may be removed for safety.
