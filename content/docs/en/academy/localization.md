The academy owner and managers can change these settings. They apply to one academy: if you run several, set each one.

## What each setting changes

| Setting | What it changes | What it doesn't change |
|---|---|---|
| **Default Language** (English or العربية) | The language of your academy's emails and certificates | The language of your public website, or of the dashboard you see |
| **Timezone** | When email digests are sent | — |
| **Currency** | The default currency for new course prices | Prices you already set, and orders already placed. Nothing is converted |

Egyptian pound (EGP) is the currency Atlas offers for course prices.

A person who has chosen their own **Email language** in their email preferences gets emails in that language. **Default Language** is used for everyone else. See [Email preferences and unsubscribing](doc:DOC-MSG-002).

## Change the settings

1. In the sidebar, open **Academy Settings**. The **Academy details** section opens first; find the **Localization** card in it.

   ![The Localization card on the Academy Settings page](shot:academy/settings-localization)

2. Choose the **Default Language**.

   ![The Default Language list highlighted](shot:academy/settings-language)

3. Choose the **Timezone**. For Egypt, choose Africa/Cairo.

   ![The Timezone list highlighted](shot:academy/settings-timezone)

4. Choose the **Currency**.

   ![The Currency list highlighted](shot:academy/settings-currency)

5. As soon as you change something, a bar saying "You have unsaved changes" appears at the bottom of the page. Select **Save Settings** in it (or **Discard** to undo your changes). You see "Settings updated successfully".

   ![The unsaved-changes bar with its Save Settings button highlighted](shot:academy/settings-save)

## Your website's language is a separate setting

**Default Language** does not decide which language your public website opens in. You set the website's main language, and whether it also has Arabic or English pages, in your website settings. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

> [!TIP]
> The same three settings also appear on the **Academy Profile** page. Change them in either place: they are the same settings.
