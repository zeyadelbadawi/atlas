Atlas doesn't have a separate security mailbox yet. Use one of these channels:

- **Anyone**: the Atlas [contact page](site:/contact?topic=support). Choose the
  topic **Help with my account**.
- **Academy team members**: **Support** in the dashboard
  ([open Support](app:/dashboard/support)).

If your problem is with your own account (a sign-in you don't recognise, a
security email you didn't expect), first follow
[Account protection](doc:DOC-SEC-003).

## What to include

- What you found, and why you think it is a security problem.
- The page or address where it happens.
- The steps to make it happen again.
- How we can reach you.

## Please don't

- Open, change or delete anyone else's data, or keep any you came across.
- Do anything that slows down or interrupts Atlas for other people.
- Try to trick Atlas staff, academy owners or learners into giving you access.
- Send passwords, sign-in codes or recovery codes, yours or anyone else's.

## What happens next

The Atlas team reads the report and replies by email if they need more details.

> [!LIMITATION]
> Atlas doesn't promise a response time for security reports and doesn't run a
> reward programme.
