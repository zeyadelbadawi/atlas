## Owners, managers and instructors: delete your account

You delete your own account from the **Account** tab of your profile, after
confirming a code sent to your verified email. The full steps are in
[Delete your account](doc:DOC-IAM-010).

## Learners: send a request

Learners don't have a delete button. Instead, in **My Learn**, open
**Profile**, then the **Account** tab, and use **Your data**:

- **Request deletion** asks the Atlas team to delete your account.
- **Request a copy** asks for a file with your profile, academies, enrolments,
  progress, certificates, orders and payments.

![The Your data card on the learner's Account tab](shot:security/learner-your-data)

Atlas emails a 6-digit code to confirm it is you, then sends you a receipt.
**Your requests** shows each request and its status: **Received**, **In
progress**, **Done** or **Declined**. You can have one open request of each
kind. A ready copy is downloaded from the same card with **Download your
data**, while you are signed in, until the date shown. The email doesn't carry
a download link.

## What deletion removes, de-identifies and keeps

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your name, email address and profile | Removed for good. The account can never sign in again. |
| Sessions, remembered browsers, learning devices | Ended straight away. |
| Two-factor settings, reset and verification links | Destroyed. |
| Academies you own | Archived: their websites go offline. |
| Billing history, audit entries, content you posted | Kept, but no longer linked to you by name. |
| Forensic watermark records | Kept until 730 days after each code was last shown, so a leaked recording can still be traced. |

Your organization's own record stays too, because it anchors billing and audit
history.

> [!NOTE]
> Atlas backs up its database every day and keeps each backup for 14 days, so
> deleted details can stay in a backup copy for up to 14 days. Backup copies of
> uploaded files, such as payment proofs, aren't deleted yet.

## Deleting an academy is permanent

Deleting an academy archives it and takes its website offline, and it can't be
brought back. See [Academy settings](category:academy).

## If your subscription ends

Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends: "Your academies, courses,
students and content are all still here and will be exactly as you left
them." See [Billing](category:billing).

## How long records are kept

Retention periods for lesson access records, quiz activity signals, watermark
records and other records are listed in
[What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

## Exporting data

> [!LIMITATION]
> There is no self-service export of an academy's data. Team members have no
> download button for their own data; they can ask the Atlas team from the
> [contact page](site:/contact?topic=support). Learners use **Request a copy**.
> No handling time is promised for any request.

Read the [privacy policy](site:/privacy-policy) for the full picture.
