Each term shows its Arabic name in the Arabic version of the app in brackets.

## Your account and team

- **Organization** (المؤسسة): the account that holds your academies, your team and your Atlas subscription. See [Your organization](doc:DOC-ORG-012).
- **Academy** (الأكاديمية): one school with its own name, website, courses and learners. An organization can have more than one, as its plan allows. See [Academy settings overview](doc:DOC-ACD-001).
- **Owner** (المالك): the person who owns the organization. Only the owner manages payment methods, approves learner payments and handles billing. See [Guide for academy owners](doc:DOC-ROLE-001).
- **Manager** (المدير): a team member who helps run an academy. See [Guide for academy managers](doc:DOC-ROLE-002).
- **Instructor** (المدرب): a team member who teaches and grades the courses assigned to them. See [Guide for instructors](doc:DOC-ROLE-003).
- **Learner** or **student** (المتعلم / الطالب): someone who takes courses at an academy. Learners use the academy's website, not the Atlas dashboard. See [Guide for learners](doc:DOC-ROLE-004).
- **Registration policy** (سياسة التسجيل): who can join your academy: **Open** (مفتوح), **Approval required** (يتطلب موافقة) or **Invite only** (بدعوة فقط). See [Choose who can join](doc:DOC-ORG-005).
- **Invite links** (روابط الدعوة): links you share so learners can join your academy. See [Invite learners with invite links](doc:DOC-ORG-006).
- **Two-factor authentication** (المصادقة الثنائية): a second check at sign-in, with a code from an authenticator app. See [Set up an authenticator app](doc:DOC-IAM-006).
- **Remember this device** (تذكّر هذا الجهاز): an option when you enter an emailed sign-in code, so that browser isn't asked again. See [Sign-in codes by email](doc:DOC-IAM-005).

## Plans and billing

- **Plan** (الخطة): what your organization subscribes to. Plans differ in their limits. See [Plan limits reference](doc:DOC-REF-001).
- **Subscription** (الاشتراك): your paid plan for a monthly or yearly period. See [Your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-001).
- **Trial** (فترة تجريبية): {trialDuration} with the full limits of the plan you pick, with no card and no automatic charge. See [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002).
- **Grace period** (فترة السماح): a short time after a period ends, when everything keeps working while your next payment arrives. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).
- **Gifted setup days** (أيام الإعداد المُهداة): extra days some plans add to your first paid subscription, before the paid period starts. See [Gifted setup days](doc:DOC-BIL-003).
- **Usage** (الاستخدام): the page that shows what you use against each plan limit. See [The Usage page](doc:DOC-BIL-006).

## Website and domain

- **Atlas dashboard** (لوحة تحكم أطلس): where owners, managers and instructors sign in and run their academies, at `dashboard.atlass.dpdns.org`. The Atlas website (home, pricing) stays at `atlass.dpdns.org`. See [Where to sign in](doc:DOC-IAM-002).
- **Atlas subdomain** (النطاق الفرعي في أطلس): the address Atlas gives every academy site. See [Your academy web address](doc:DOC-ACD-005).
- **Custom domain** (نطاق مخصص): your own domain, such as `learn.your-academy.com`, connected with DNS records. See [Custom domain DNS records](doc:DOC-REF-003).
- **Main language** (اللغة الرئيسية): the language your site opens in at its plain address. See [Academy site URLs](doc:DOC-REF-004).
- **Visual identity** (الهوية البصرية): your logo, colours and favicon. See [Brand Studio](doc:DOC-ACD-004).
- **Theme** (القالب): the design of your website. See [Choose or change your theme](doc:DOC-WEB-002).
- **Coming soon** (قريبًا): the page visitors see while your site is not published or your subscription is not active. See [The Coming Soon page](doc:DOC-ACD-009).

## Courses and learning

- **Course** (الدورة): a set of sections with lessons, quizzes and assignments. See [Courses overview](doc:DOC-LRN-001).
- **Lesson** (درس): a YouTube video, a link to another site or a file inside a course. See [Build your curriculum](doc:DOC-LRN-005).
- **Quiz** (اختبار) and **Assignment** (واجب): graded activities in a course. See [Create a quiz](doc:DOC-LRN-014) and [Assignments](doc:DOC-LRN-019).
- **Integrity mode** (وضع النزاهة): the quiz setting that decides which events are recorded for the reviewer. See [Exam integrity](doc:DOC-LRN-016).
- **Student overrides** (استثناءات الطلاب): extra time, extra attempts or a different window for one learner on one quiz. See [Give a learner a quiz exception](doc:DOC-LRN-018).
- **Completion rule** (شرط الإتمام): what a learner must do for a lesson to count as complete. See [Completion rules](doc:DOC-LRN-013).
- **Enrollment** (التسجيل): a learner's access to one course. See [Enroll a learner manually](doc:DOC-ORG-010).
- **Certificate** (شهادة) and **verification code** (رمز التحقق): the certificate a learner earns, and the code anyone can use to check it is real. See [How certificate verification works](doc:DOC-LRN-022).
- **My Learn** (تعلّمي): the learner area on your academy site, with courses, certificates, purchases and devices. See [Find your way around My Learn](doc:DOC-LRN-031).

## Payments

- **Payment methods** (طرق الدفع): the bank transfer (التحويل البنكي), e-wallet (محفظة إلكترونية) and InstaPay (إنستاباي) details learners pay to. See [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).
- **Payment proof** (إثبات الدفع): the screenshot or PDF a learner uploads after paying. See [Review payment proofs](doc:DOC-COM-003).
- **Checkout** (إتمام الشراء): the page where a learner sees how to pay for a course and uploads the proof. See [Buy a course](doc:DOC-COM-010).
- **Course orders** (طلبات شراء الدورات): the list of every purchase in your academy. See [Track course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).

## Protection

- **Content protection** (حماية المحتوى): the watermark, player restrictions and device limits that discourage sharing. See [Content protection](doc:DOC-MED-001).
- **Forensic watermark** (علامة مائية شخصية): a visible code tied to the viewer that moves across every video, so a leaked recording can be traced. See [The forensic watermark explained](doc:DOC-MED-002).
- **Devices** (الأجهزة): the browsers a learner learns on. Each academy allows a set number. See [Device limits](doc:DOC-MED-003).
- **Sharing signals** (مؤشرات المشاركة): a report that points out accounts that may be shared. See [Sharing signals report](doc:DOC-MED-006).
- **Academy Media** (وسائط الأكاديمية): your academy's library of uploaded images and PDFs. See [Media library](doc:DOC-MED-007).

## Messages and help

- **Messages** (الرسائل): emails and in-app notifications you send to your learners. See [Message your learners](doc:DOC-MSG-003).
- **Announcements** (الإعلانات): posts your learners see in their notifications. See [Post announcements](doc:DOC-MSG-004).
- **Atlas service requests** (طلبات خدمات أطلس): requests for work from the Atlas team, such as a logo or a custom feature. See [Request a service](doc:DOC-SUP-002).
- **Activity log** (سجل النشاط): a record of changes made in your academy. See [Academy activity log](doc:DOC-ANL-004).
- **Reports** (التقارير) and **Student analytics** (تحليلات الطلاب): views of enrollments, progress and learners. See [Academy reports](doc:DOC-ANL-003) and [Student analytics](doc:DOC-ANL-002).
