## Where do I sign in?

On your academy's website, at **Sign in**. Learners don't use the Atlas dashboard. See [Where to sign in](doc:DOC-IAM-002).

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

On your academy's sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the email. See [Reset or change your password](doc:DOC-IAM-003).

## Can I use one account at several academies?

Yes. On another Atlas academy's sign-up page, choose **Already have an Atlas account?** and sign in with your existing password. Your courses and progress in each academy stay separate. See [Sign up and join an academy](doc:DOC-LRN-030).

## How do I pay for a course?

Send the price to one of the academy's payment methods (bank transfer, mobile wallet or InstaPay), then upload a photo or PDF of your proof on the course's checkout page. See [Buy a course](doc:DOC-COM-010).

## My payment is under review or was rejected.

The academy checks each payment by hand, and you get an email when it decides. If it was rejected, you can pay again and send a new proof. See [Your purchases and payments](doc:DOC-COM-011).

## Why can't I watch on my new phone?

Each academy lets you learn on a set number of devices, and each browser counts as one. Open **Devices** in **My Learn** and remove a device you no longer use. See ["Device limit reached"](doc:DOC-TRB-003).

## Why do I see a code and part of my email on videos?

It is a personal watermark. It discourages sharing and lets a recording that's shared without permission be traced back to where it came from. Only you see your code, and only while you watch. Atlas keeps a record of each code for up to two years after it was last shown, and only Atlas can look a code up. See [The forensic watermark explained](doc:DOC-MED-002).

## Can I download the lessons?

No. Videos play only online and are never saved on your device.

## How do I get my certificate?

When you complete a course that gives a certificate, it appears in **Certificates** in **My Learn**, where you can download and share it. See [Download and share your certificate](doc:DOC-LRN-035).

## Can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?

Yes, by request. Open **Profile** in **My Learn**, go to the **Account** tab, and under **Your data** choose **Request a copy** or **Request deletion**. Atlas emails you a code to confirm it's you. The Atlas team handles the request and emails you when it is done. A deleted account can't be recovered.

![The Your data card on the learner's profile, with Request a copy and Request deletion](shot:faq/learner-your-data)

See [What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

## Who do I contact for help or a refund?

Your academy. Use **Contact your academy** in **My Learn**. Payments go to the academy, so refunds are decided by the academy, not by Atlas. See [How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003).
