## How much does Atlas cost?

Prices for every plan, monthly and yearly, are on the [pricing page](site:/pricing). All prices are in Egyptian pounds. Plans differ in their limits; see [Plan limits reference](doc:DOC-REF-001).

## Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The trial lasts {trialDuration}, needs no payment details and is never charged automatically. When it ends, you choose whether to subscribe. There is one trial per customer. See [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002).

## How do I pay for a plan?

By bank transfer, e-wallet or InstaPay. Choose your plan, send the amount to the details shown at checkout, then upload your proof. The Atlas team checks it and activates your plan. See [Pay for your subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-004).

## How long does payment approval take?

A person on the Atlas team checks each proof, so activation is not instant. Pay a few days before your trial or period ends so there is time to review it without a gap.

## Does my plan renew automatically?

No. Atlas never charges you automatically. To continue, you pay for each new period the same way. If the payment for a new period is late, a grace period keeps everything working until the date shown on your dashboard. See [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).

## What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

You can't manage your academies until you subscribe, and your academy website shows a Coming soon page to visitors. Subscribe to pick up where you left off. See [When a subscription lapses](doc:DOC-BIL-007).

## Can I change plans in the middle of a period?

You can upgrade at any time: the new limits start when your payment is approved, and the new plan's period starts when your current one ends. During a yearly plan, upgrades go through support. To move to a smaller plan, buy it near the end of your current period. See [Renew, upgrade or downgrade your plan](doc:DOC-BIL-005).

## How do I cancel?

Cancelling a trial ends it straight away. Cancelling a paid plan stops it from renewing, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. See [Cancel your trial or subscription](doc:DOC-BIL-008).

## Can I get a refund on my subscription?

Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](site:/terms). To ask about one, open a support ticket. See [Get help: open a support ticket](doc:DOC-SUP-001).

## Do I get an invoice?

You get an email when your payment is approved, and every payment you submitted is listed under **Payment history** in **Billing**. Atlas does not issue invoices yet. See [Billing receipts and emails](doc:DOC-BIL-009).

## What are gifted setup days?

Some plans add gifted setup days to your first paid subscription. They are added before your paid period starts, so you still get the full period you paid for. See [Gifted setup days](doc:DOC-BIL-003).

## Can I have more than one academy?

Yes, up to your plan's academy limit. See [Plan limits reference](doc:DOC-REF-001).

## Does Atlas take a commission on my course sales?

No. Learners pay you directly, and Atlas charges only your plan subscription. See the [Selling and payments FAQ](doc:DOC-FAQ-004).
