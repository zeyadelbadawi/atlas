## Does Atlas use my camera or microphone during a quiz?

No. Atlas never uses your camera or microphone, never records your screen or what you type, and sees nothing outside the quiz page. Nothing is blocked.

## What does Atlas record during a quiz?

It depends on the quiz's **Integrity mode**, which the instructor sets. When it is on, Atlas records, with the time: switching to another tab or window, leaving full screen, copying, pasting and printing on the page, and a connection check once a minute. The reviewer sees these with plain explanations. They are prompts for a conversation, not proof of anything. Before you start, the quiz shows what is recorded. See [Exam integrity and full-screen exams](doc:DOC-LRN-016).

## Will I be warned when something is recorded?

In **Monitor** mode, no. In **Warn** mode, you're warned each time, and reaching the limit flags the attempt for review. In **Strict** mode, you're warned each time, and when the limit is reached the quiz is submitted automatically with the answers you saved.

## What happens when time runs out?

The clock starts when you press Start and keeps running even if you close the page. When it reaches zero, the answers you have saved are submitted automatically. See [Quiz timer, autosave and full-screen problems](doc:DOC-TRB-011).

## Full screen doesn't work on my iPhone.

Safari on iPhone can't show the quiz in full screen. You can take the quiz as it is, and your instructor sees that full screen wasn't available. See [Quiz timer, autosave and full-screen problems](doc:DOC-TRB-011).

## Can a learner get extra time or another attempt?

Yes. In **Student overrides**, the instructor can give one learner extra time, extra attempts or a different window. It applies when that learner starts their next attempt, and the learner gets a notification. See [Give a learner a quiz exception](doc:DOC-LRN-018).

## Who grades essay answers?

A reviewer, usually the course instructor, grades them by hand. The learner sees "This question is graded by a reviewer." See [Review attempts and grade essay answers](doc:DOC-LRN-017).

## When do learners see their score?

That depends on the quiz settings. For example, a quiz can show the score and the correct answers only after all attempts are used. See [Quiz settings](doc:DOC-LRN-015).

## Can I export quiz data?

The integrity report can be downloaded as a CSV file with **Export integrity CSV**. See [Integrity report and CSV export](doc:DOC-LRN-025).
