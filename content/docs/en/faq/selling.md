## Which payment methods can learners use?

Bank transfer, mobile wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash and others) and InstaPay. You add your details for each method in **Payment methods** and turn on the ones you want. See [Set up payment methods](doc:DOC-COM-002).

## Can I add two bank accounts?

No. Each academy has one bank transfer, one e-wallet and one InstaPay method.

## Does Atlas take a share of my course sales?

No. Learners pay to the payment methods you set up for your academy, so the money goes straight into your own accounts and Atlas records no commission on these payments. Atlas charges only your plan subscription.

## Are payments approved automatically?

No. A learner sends the money and uploads a proof. You check in your bank or wallet app that the money arrived, then approve or reject it in **Payments**. Approving enrolls the learner and emails them. See [Review payment proofs and approve enrollments](doc:DOC-COM-003).

## Can a manager or instructor review payments?

No. Only the organization owner can set up payment methods and review payments, orders and revenue.

## What happens if I reject a payment?

The learner gets no access, and the order stays open so they can pay again and send a new proof.

## Where can I see my sales?

**Revenue & payouts** shows your approved payments for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, and **Course orders** lists every order with its status. See [Track course orders](doc:DOC-COM-004).

## How do refunds work?

The money went to you, so you agree the refund with the learner and send the money back yourself. Then record it with **Record refund…** on the payment: the learner's access ends, the order shows **Refunded** and the learner gets an email. A learner who asks Atlas for a refund is told to contact your academy. See [Refunds for course purchases](doc:DOC-COM-005).

## Do learners get a receipt?

Learners get an email when their proof is received and when their payment is approved or rejected, and they can see their purchases and payments in **My Learn**. See [Receipts and purchase emails](doc:DOC-COM-006).

## Can learners pay by card or Fawry?

No. Card and Fawry checkout are not available. See [Integrations](doc:DOC-INT-001).
