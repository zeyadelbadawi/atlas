## What is Atlas?

Atlas is a branded academy platform for Egyptian teachers who sell their lessons online. You get an academy website with your name on it, courses with YouTube lessons, quizzes, assignments and certificates, and payments from learners that you approve yourself. See [Getting started with Atlas](doc:DOC-START-001).

## Do I have to pay for Atlas?

Yes. Every plan is a monthly or yearly subscription in Egyptian pounds. You can try a plan for {trialDuration} first, with no card needed. See the [pricing page](site:/pricing) and [How the trial works](doc:DOC-BIL-002).

## Is there a mobile app?

No. Atlas works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, for you and for your learners. See [Browsers and devices](doc:DOC-REF-006).

## Can learners pay by card or Fawry?

No. Learners pay by bank transfer, mobile wallet (such as Vodafone Cash) or InstaPay, straight to you, and upload a proof. The organization owner checks the money arrived and approves. See [How selling courses works](doc:DOC-COM-001).

## Can I create coupon codes?

No, coupon codes are not available.

## Does Atlas send SMS or WhatsApp messages?

No. Atlas sends email and in-app notifications only. See [Notifications: in-app and email](doc:DOC-MSG-001).

## Can I teach live sessions in Atlas?

No. Live online sessions are not available in Atlas.

## Can I put my own video files on Atlas?

No. Video lessons are YouTube videos played inside your academy, or links to videos on another site. See [Add a YouTube video lesson](doc:DOC-LRN-006).

> [!LIMITATION]
> A YouTube lesson stays on YouTube. A learner who finds its YouTube link can watch it there, without your academy's watermark.

## Can learners record or share my videos?

Atlas discourages sharing and helps trace a leaked recording, but no website can stop screen recording. See the [Content protection FAQ](doc:DOC-FAQ-003).

## Is my academy site in Arabic?

Your site has English and Arabic versions of every page. You choose which language opens at your main address; the other one sits under `/ar` or `/en`. Where a translation is missing, visitors see the main-language text. See [Build your site in Arabic and English](doc:DOC-WEB-006).

## Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Your academy starts on its own Atlas subdomain, and you can connect a domain you own by adding DNS records at your domain provider. See [Connect your own domain](doc:DOC-ACD-006).

## Can I change my academy's Atlas address?

Yes, if you are the organization owner, once every 60 days: save a new **Academy Slug** in **Academy Settings**. Your website moves to the new address, and the old address keeps working and sends visitors to the same page on the new one. The old address stays reserved for your academy, so you can change back to it. See [Your academy web address](doc:DOC-ACD-005).

## Does Atlas have an API?

No. Atlas has no public API, API keys or webhooks. See [Integrations](doc:DOC-INT-001).

## Which team roles are there?

Owner, manager and instructor. Instructors see only the courses assigned to them, and only the owner approves payments. See [Roles and permissions](doc:DOC-ROLE-005).

## Can I run more than one academy?

Yes, as many as your plan allows. Each academy has its own name, website and learners. See [Create another academy](doc:DOC-ACD-007).

## Who can see my academy's data?

Each academy's students, courses and payments are kept separate, and your team sees only what their role allows. People at Atlas can see academy details to run the service. See [How your academy's data is kept separate](doc:DOC-SEC-002).

## Can I export my academy's data?

Not by yourself yet. To ask for a copy, a correction or a deletion of personal data, send a request from the [contact page](site:/contact). Learners can ask for a copy of their own data from their profile. See [What data Atlas keeps about learners](doc:DOC-SEC-004).

## How do I get help?

Owners and team members open a support ticket from **Support** in the dashboard. See [Get help: open a support ticket](doc:DOC-SUP-001). Learners contact their academy. See [How learners get help](doc:DOC-SUP-003).
