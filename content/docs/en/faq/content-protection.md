## Can Atlas stop learners from recording the screen?

No. No website can stop screen recording. Atlas makes copying harder and helps trace a leaked recording back to the account it came from. See [Content protection: what Atlas does and does not do](doc:DOC-MED-001).

## What is the watermark?

Each learner sees their own personal code and a masked hint of their account moving across every video, with a faint pattern of the code over the whole picture. It is visible on purpose, and it stays in full screen. See [The forensic watermark explained](doc:DOC-MED-002).

## How do I find out who leaked a video?

Contact Atlas support with the code you can see in the leaked recording. Atlas traces it to the account and session it came from and tells you. Only Atlas can look a code up. See [If your content leaks](doc:DOC-MED-005).

## Does the watermark work on YouTube lessons?

Inside your academy, yes: Atlas draws it over the YouTube video. But the video itself stays on YouTube, so anyone who finds its YouTube link can watch it there without the watermark.

## Can I turn the watermark off?

No. It is always on for every video lesson and course preview, on every plan.

## Does the watermark show on course previews?

Yes. Visitors watching a preview without signing in see a code tied to that visit instead of an account.

## Can learners download the videos?

The player has no download button, no "Save video as" option and no picture-in-picture, and videos are never saved on the device. That still doesn't stop someone from recording the screen.

## Can learners share one account?

Each learner account works on a set number of devices, and only a limited number of learning sessions can run at the same time, so sharing an account gets in the way of everyone using it. The sharing signals report helps you spot accounts that may be shared. See [Device limits](doc:DOC-MED-003) and [Sharing signals report](doc:DOC-MED-006).

## Does Atlas use DRM?

No. Atlas has no DRM. Protection comes from the watermark, the player's restrictions and the device limits.

> [!LIMITATION]
> These protections discourage copying and make a leaked recording traceable. They do not stop screen recording, and they do not cover a YouTube video watched on YouTube itself.
